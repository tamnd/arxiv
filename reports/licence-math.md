# The licence census of scope-math

794,887 papers are in scope-math, and 145,304 of them are in scope by a cross-list only. 129,697 are open, and 636,424 are record.
Counted on 26 October 2026 from the metadata plane. The list of the papers is manifests/scope/math.txt.

## How to read it

A paper is in scope when one of its categories is a math category, math-ph, or one of the old archives alg-geom, dg-ga, funct-an and q-alg (AX1.2).
The class is the class of spec 9.3 for the licence of the latest version (AX2.3). CC0, CC BY and CC BY-SA are open. CC BY-NC-SA is share-alike-nc. CC BY-NC-ND is verbatim. The arXiv licence and no licence are record.
The bulk surfaces give one licence for each paper, and it is the licence of the latest version. So the count of an earlier version can be wrong. ax licence resolve reads the licence of each version when a paper is selected.
The year is the year that the id names, so the year when arXiv announced the paper.
