# The licence census

Of 794,914 papers counted, 141,738 may be translated and 158,470 may be republished in English, both as upper bounds.
Counted on 26 October 2026 over 421 months.

## Why this is an upper bound

Every surface that serves arXiv metadata in bulk carries one licence per paper rather than one per version, and the one it carries is the latest version's.
A paper whose author relicensed between v1 and v3 is counted here under the v3 licence for all three of its versions.
360,959 papers counted here carry more than one version, which is 45.4% of the corpus.
A random sample of forty multi-version papers taken in September 2026 found five whose v1 licence differs from their latest, and four of those five had a latest version more permissive than the first.
That is the direction that costs something: it is the case where a corpus reads a permissive latest licence and republishes an earlier version that was never offered under it.
Resolving all of it means one page per version, which is over five million pages at arXiv's fifteen second pace, so the resolution happens per paper when a paper is selected and ax licence resolve is the command that does it.
