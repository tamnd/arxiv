<!-- definition-1: body_md -->
\label{def:package}
The \emph{marginal package} $M(C)$ of a configuration comprises:
(m1) per-example reconstruction $\mathbb E_{q(z\mid x)}\log p(x\mid z)$;
(m2) per-example rate $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(q(\cdot\mid x)\Vert p)$;
(m3) per-example ELBO, marginal likelihood $\log p(x)$, variational gap
$\Delta(x)=\log p(x)-\mathrm{ELBO}(x)$, and the generative law $p(x)$ as a
distribution;
(m4) $I(x;z)$ under the encoder joint and the per-dimension active-units
statistics $\mathrm{AU}_k=\mathrm{Var}_x\,\mathbb E[z_k\mid x]$;
(m5) encoder and decoder code histograms under $\bar q$, their entropies and
occupied-code counts;
(m6) every permutation-symmetric functional of the joint table $T^{\bar q}$
(every $F$ with $F(P_1TP_2^{\top})=F(T)$ for permutation matrices $P_1,P_2$),
including $I(c_{\mathrm{enc}};c_{\mathrm{dec}})$, all R\'enyi mutual informations, joint entropy,
both histograms as multisets, and the permutation-matched agreement
$\max_{\pi}\sum_iT(i,\pi(i))$;
(m7) prior-law agreement $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{prior}}$.

<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:invisibility}
\textbf{(I) One pair, whole package, shared data.}
For every $K\ge2$ there exist configurations $C^1,C^2$ with the same data
law and prior, differing only in encoder and decoder, with
$M(C^1)=M(C^2)$---every item of Definition~\ref{def:package}, per example
and in aggregate, to exact equality, including $p(x)$ as a
distribution---while $\mathcal A(C^1)=1$ and $\mathcal A(C^2)=0$.

\textbf{(I$'$) Moment blindness with a sharp threshold.}
For every $K\ge2$ and $m\ge2$ the pair in (I) can be built so that, in
addition: (N1) per-example posterior means are identical example by example,
so every functional of the means---per-dimension AU, their full covariance,
any statistic---coincides; (N2) every aggregate-posterior mixed moment of
degree below $m$ in the coordinates that $c_{\mathrm{dec}}$ reads coincides; (N3) the
threshold is sharp, with explicit degree-$m$ cross moments separating the
pair.  The blindness order $m$ is a design parameter independent of $K$.

\textbf{(II) Orbit form, for trained models.}
Every prior-preserving bimeasurable bijection $\tau$ of $\mathcal Z$ leaves
(m1)--(m3) and $I(x;z)$ exactly invariant for every $x$ (and per-dimension
AU when $\tau^{-1}$ acts as a translation on $\mathrm{supp}(\bar q)$), while
agreement transforms as $\mathcal A(C_\tau)=\bar q(\tau(\mathrm{Agr}))$; the full
statement and proof are in Appendix~\ref{app:orbit}.

\textbf{(a) Reconstruction side.}\label{thm:certification}

$d_{\mathrm{bin}}\bigl(\bar q(E)\,\Vert\,\bar p_\theta(E)\bigr)\le\bar\Delta$;

\textbf{(b) Generative side.}

<!-- theorem-3: body_md -->
\label{thm:price}
Write $\Lambda_Q=Q\Lambda Q^{\top}$.

\textbf{(a) Exact price and worst basis.}
$B_Q=\tfrac12\log\bigl[\det\mathrm{diag}(\Lambda_Q)/\det\Lambda_Q\bigr]$,
attained at $\mu^*=m_x$, $D^*_{ii}=1/(\Lambda_Q)_{ii}$.  Moreover
(i) $B_Q=0$ iff $\Lambda_Q$ is diagonal---exactly on the posterior
eigenbasis, up to axis permutations, signs, and within-eigenspace
rotations;
(ii) $\sup_Q B_Q=\tfrac d2\log\bigl(\mathrm{AM}(\lambda)/
\mathrm{GM}(\lambda)\bigr)$, finite and attained (Schur--Horn,
\citealp{marshall2011inequalities});
(iii) restricting the product family to Gaussians is without loss.

\textbf{(b) Estimand invariance under transport.}
For $R\in O(d)$, transport the configuration: $z\mapsto Rz$, decoder
$g'(w)=g(R^{\top}w)$, posterior $N(Rm_x,R\Sigma R^{\top})$, prior
$R_{\#}p$, and \emph{transported} maps $c'(w)=c(R^{\top}w)$.  Then the
joint law of $(c'_{\mathrm{enc}},c'_{\mathrm{dec}})$ under every
transported sampling law equals the original joint law; in particular
$\mathcal A_{q}$ under full-covariance posteriors, $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{prior}}$, and every functional of
code-pair laws are exactly invariant.

\textbf{(c) The asymmetry.}
For a fixed model, $Q\mapsto B_Q$ ranges over the closed interval
$\bigl[0,\tfrac d2\log(\mathrm{AM}/\mathrm{GM})\bigr]$ while every
transported estimand is constant; across models the price is unbounded
($\kappa\to\infty$).  A mean-field-budget certificate is a property of the
(model, basis) pair; the certified quantity is a property of the model.
Reporting a mean-field KL budget without its basis is ill-posed; the
price-minimizing basis is the posterior eigenbasis.  The declared code map is the
exact dual.  Theorem~\ref{thm:certification} holds for \emph{every} fixed
measurable pair $(c_{\mathrm{enc}},c_{\mathrm{dec}})$ at once, and a rate budget is a functional of
$\bar q$ alone, so changing the map moves the certified \emph{quantity} and leaves
the \emph{budget} fixed---the mirror of the basis, which moves the budget and
leaves the quantity fixed.  Agreement is a property of the (model, declared map)
pair and the budget a property of the model; reporting either without the other
is ill-posed, and Section~\ref{sec:exp-price} measures both halves on one
checkpoint.

\textbf{(d) Informative floor.}
Let $y\in[L]$ be any finite-alphabet label with $y\perp z\mid x$, and let
$\alpha$ be the held-out accuracy of any predictor of $y$ from the
\emph{sampled} code $c_{\mathrm{enc}}(z)$, $z\sim q(\cdot\mid x)$.  Then with
$h_2(\cdot)$ the binary entropy in nats and
$\gamma(\alpha)=H(y)-h_2(1-\alpha)-(1-\alpha)\log(L-1)$ (Fano's
inequality, \citealp{cover2006elements}),
\[
\bar R\;=\;D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\bar q\Vert p)+I(x;z)\;\ge\;I(x;z)\;\ge\;I(x;c_{\mathrm{enc}}(z))
\;\ge\;I(y;c_{\mathrm{enc}}(z))\;\ge\;\gamma(\alpha),
\]
and by Lemma~\ref{lem:width} every band
$\{a:d_{\mathrm{bin}}(\mathcal A\Vert a)\le B\}$ with $B\ge\gamma$ has width at least
$1-e^{-\gamma}$.  At $\gamma\ge1$ nat the width is $\ge1-e^{-1}\approx0.632$
regardless of the observed agreement.

\textbf{(e) Dataset floor.}  If the data law is empirical over $N$ points---the
same $N$ points that define $\bar q=\tfrac1N\sum_iq(\cdot\mid x_i)$ and
$\bar R$---then $I(x;z)\le H(x)=\log N$, so
$D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\bar q\Vert p)\ge\bar R-\log N$: \emph{every} licensed budget obeys
$B\ge\bar R-\log N$, the band has width at least $1-e^{-(\bar R-\log N)}$,
and certification at width $w^*$ is \textbf{impossible} whenever
$\bar R>\log N-\log(1-w^*)$---no estimator, however tight, can change this.

<!-- lemma-2: body_md -->
\label{lem:budgets}
Let $f$ be any measurable coarsening of $\mathcal Z$ and $\tilde q$ the
moment-matched Gaussian of $\bar q$.  Then
\[
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(f_{\#}\bar q\Vert f_{\#}p)\;\le\;D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\bar q\Vert p),\qquad

so binned/grid plug-ins (data processing) and moment-matched Gaussian plug-ins

<!-- lemma-3: body_md -->
\label{lem:harmonic}
Let $\mu_0$ be a distribution on $\mathbb R^2$ invariant under rotation by
$2\pi/m$, and $\mu_j=(R_{\theta_j})_{\#}\mu_0$ its rotations.  Then all
moment tensors of $\mu_j$ of total degree $t<m$ are independent of $j$; if
$\mathbb E_{\mu_0}[r^m e^{im\varphi}]\ne0$, the degree-$m$ tensors depend on $j$
through the phase $e^{im\theta_j}$.
<!-- end lemma-3 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
In polar form a degree-$t$ monomial moment is a linear combination of
circular harmonics $\mathbb E[r^te^{ik\varphi}]$ with $|k|\le t$ and
$k\equiv t\pmod2$.  Rotation by $\omega$ multiplies the $k$-th harmonic by
$e^{ik\omega}$; invariance of $\mu_0$ under $\omega=2\pi/m$ forces
$\mathbb E_{\mu_0}[r^te^{ik\varphi}]=0$ unless $m\mid k$.  For $t<m$ only $k=0$
survives, and $k=0$ harmonics are rotation-invariant, hence equal for all
$\mu_j$.  At $t=m$ the $k=\pm m$ harmonics survive and transform by
$e^{\pm im\theta_j}$.

\label{lem:clip}

<!-- lemma-5: body_md -->
\label{lem:aq}
Let $\hat{\mathcal A}_q$ average $S$ posterior draws at each of the $N$
test points, with independent noise for every (point, draw) pair, so that
it is the mean of $NS$ independent $\{0,1\}$-valued variables whose means
average to $\mathcal A_{q}$; then $C_q=[a_-,a_+]=\hat{\mathcal
A}_q\pm\sqrt{\ln(2/\delta_q)/(2NS)}$ contains $\mathcal A_{q}$ with probability
$\ge1-\delta_q$ (Hoeffding).  If $B\geD_{\mathrm{KL}}(\bar q\Vert p)$ with probability
$\ge1-\delta_B$, then
\[
U=\bigcup_{a\in C_q}\{b:d_{\mathrm{bin}}(a\Vert b)\le B\}=\bigl[\,b_-(a_-),\,b_+(a_+)\bigr],
\]
where $b_-(a)\le a\le b_+(a)$ are the two roots of $d_{\mathrm{bin}}(a\Vert b)=B$,
contains $\mathcal A_{\mathrm{prior}}$ with probability $\ge1-\delta_B-\delta_q$.
<!-- end lemma-5 -->
<!-- head-6: body_md -->
On the event that $\mathcal A_{q}\in C_q$ and $D_{\mathrm{KL}}(\bar q\Vert p)\le
B$, of probability $\ge1-\delta_B-\delta_q$ by a union bound,
Theorem~\ref{thm:certification}(b) gives $d_{\mathrm{bin}}(\mathcal A_{q}\Vert\mathcal A_{\mathrm{prior}})\le B$, so
$\mathcal A_{\mathrm{prior}}$ lies in the band at $a=\mathcal A_{q}\in C_q$ and hence in $U$.  Each band is
an interval containing $a$, and both of its endpoints are non-decreasing
in $a$: $\partial_ad_{\mathrm{bin}}(a\Vert b)=\log\frac{a(1-b)}{b(1-a)}$ has the
sign of $a-b$, so raising $a$ raises $d_{\mathrm{bin}}$ at every $b<a$ (the lower
root moves up) and lowers it at every $b>a$ (the upper root moves up);
hence the union of the bands over an interval of $a$ is the interval
stated.  \hfill$\square$
