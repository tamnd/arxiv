\label{Teo arnold}
    Let $G$ be a complex Lie group and $H_{0} \in \mathfrak{g}$ a semisimple element. Let $\mathcal{O} = \operatorname{Ad}(G)H_{0}$ be its adjoint orbit and $\kappa$ the (complex) KKS form. Then $\mathrm{Re}(\kappa)$ and $\mathrm{Im}(\kappa)$ are real symplectic forms on $\mathcal{O}$ and 
    \begin{enumerate}[(a)]
        \item The form $\mathrm{Re}(\kappa)$ (resp. $\mathrm{Im}(\kappa)$) is exact if and only if the eigenvalues of $H_{0}$ are real (resp. purely imaginary)
        \item In either case, $\mathcal{O}$ equipped with these real forms is symplectomorphic to $T^{\ast}\mathbb{F}$.
    \end{enumerate}
