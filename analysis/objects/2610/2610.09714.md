Let (as exists in some suitable differential field $K$)
$$
(\underline{x}, \underline{y})\in \big(\Theta \cap (V \times S)\big)(K)
$$
be of full rank 
$$
d={\rm rank}(\underline{x}, \underline{y})=\dim V.
$$

Let $W={\rm Zcl}(\theta(V))$, so that $\underline{y}\in W(K)$.
Thus $S'$ is the smallest coset containing $W$.
Let $R''$ be the smallest coset of $R$ containing $V$, so that
$$
R'' \times S'
$$
is the smallest coset in $R\times S$ such that
$(\underline{x}, \underline{y})\in (R''\times S')(K)$.

We apply Theorem 4.1 but note that the target group $S'$
in the statement is NVQ, so $S'_{\rm NVQ}=S$. 
We fix some decomposition
$$
R'' \cong R_{\rm NVQ} \times R_{\rm VEC}
$$
and observe that the restriction 
$$
R'' \times S' \rightarrow R_{\rm NVQ} \times S_{\rm NVQ}
$$
of the projection $R\times S \rightarrow R_{\rm NVQ} \times S_{\rm NVQ}$
is the identity on the second factor.

Let $R'$ be the smallest coset containing the image of $V$ under
projection to $R_{\rm NVQ}$, so that
$$
R'\times S'
$$
is the minimal coset containing the image of $(\underline{x}, \underline{y})$
under projection to $R_{\rm NVQ} \times S_{\rm NVQ}$
(this is still a coset in $R\times S$ and so is a product by the
disjoint pair assumption).

By Theorem 4.1 we have
$$
{\rm trd} (\underline{x}, \underline{y}) \ge 
{\rm rank} (\underline{x}, \underline{y})
+ \dim S'.
$$
Therefore we have
$$
d+\dim S' \le {\rm trd} (\underline{x}, \underline{y}) \le d +\dim W
$$
and since $W\subset S'$ we conclude $W=S'$.\ \qed
