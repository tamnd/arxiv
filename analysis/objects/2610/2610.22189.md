<!-- remark-1: body_md -->
It should be emphasised that, in the statement of Theorem~\ref{thm:global}, no additional assumption is postulated on $\bm{F}$; the interpretation is that drillstring-wellbore friction, being an intrinsically dissipative phenomenon, is itself non-malign for the well-posedness of the system. Aside from the viscous drag terms, the dominant source of nonlinearity in the PDEs arises indeed from the dependence of the normal force on the drillstring stress, pipe pressure, and annulus pressure, all of which evolve in closed loop with the frictional forces. Consequently, the principal obstacle to well-posedness is not friction itself, but the nonlinear feedback coupling between the generated tangential forces and the state-dependent normal load.

\label{remark:GDD}
Under the additional assumptions of Theorem~\ref{thm:global}, the coefficients $\phi_{i0}(\overline{\bm{w}}),\phi_{i2}(\overline{\bm{w}}),\phi_{i3}(\overline{\bm{w}}), \phi_{i4}(\overline{\bm{w}}), \phi_{if}(\overline{\bm{w}}) \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}$ become indepndent of $\overline{\bm{w}}$, and the matrix $\mathbf{\Theta}(\bm{\phi}_f(\overline{\bm{w}}),\bm{\eta},\overline{\bm{w}})$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:MathTheta} reduces to a matrix $\mathbf{\Theta}(\bm{\phi}_f,\bm{\eta})$, with $\mathbf{\Theta} \in C^0(\mathbb{R}_{> 0}^{16};\mathbf{M}_4(\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}))$. <!-- remark-3: body_md -->
In contrast to the well-posedness analysis of Sect.~\ref{sect:wellPos}, which demonstrated that -- for $\od{\theta 
\ensuremath{_\textnormal{p}}(z)}{z} \approx 0$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:nomN} -- drillstring-wellbore friction dynamics plays little to no role in determining the well-posedness of the ODE--PDE interconnection~\eqref{eq:ODE--PDEsystem}, Theorem~\ref{thm:Stability} suggests that it might alter its stability, even in the absence of oscillating normal loads. To elucidate this aspect, it is possible to assume $\od{\theta 
\ensuremath{_\textnormal{p}}(z)}{z} \approx 0$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:nomN} and consider the matrix $\mathbf{\Theta}(\bm{\phi}_f,\bm{\eta})$ of Remark~\ref{remark:GDD}, along with the more conservative sufficient condition $\norm{\mathbf{\Theta}(\bm{\phi}_f,\bm{\eta})} < 1$ in place of $\rho(\mathbf{\Theta}(\bm{\phi}_f,\bm{\eta})) < 1$. Denoting by $\mathbf{M}_{4}(\mathbb{R}) \ni \mathbf{\Theta}_0(\bm{\eta}) \triangleq \mathbf{\Theta}(\bm{0},\bm{\eta})$ the matrix $\mathbf{\Theta}(\bm{\phi}_f,\bm{\eta})$ in the absence of friction, suppose that $\norm{\mathbf{\Theta}_0(\bm{\eta})} < 1$. \ensuremath{_\textnormal{p}}(z)} < 1-\norm{\mathbf{\Theta}_0(\bm{\eta})}.

Therefore, friction may reduce the available stability margin associated with this sufficient condition. Of course, since the requirements of Theorem~\ref{thm:Stability} are only sufficient, their violation does not imply loss of stability; rather, it merely indicates that this particular criterion is no longer conclusive.

Accordingly, the following Lyapunov function is considered:
\begin{align}\label{eq:Lyapunov}
\begin{split}
V\bigl(\bm{X}(z,t)\bigr) \triangleq & \dfrac{1}{2}\bigggl( \dfrac{f^2(z,t)}{\bar{k}_0(z,t)} + \sum_{i=1}^{n_1}\dfrac{\bar{c}_{i,1}(z,t)}{\tau_{i,1}(z)}x_{i,1}^2(z,t) + \sum_{i=1}^{n_2}\dfrac{\bar{c}_{i,2}(z,t)}{\tau_{i,2}(z)}x_{i,2}^2(z,t)\bigggr),
\end{split}
\end{align}
which is differentiable with respect to time in the classical sense.
In fact, taking the time derivative of Eq.~\eqref{eq:Lyapunov} along the dynamics~\eqref{eq:ODEi} yields
\begin{align}
\begin{split}
\dpd{V(z,t)}{t} & \leq \abs{v(z,t)}_\varepsilon\abs{f(z,t)} - \dfrac{\abs{v(z,t)}_\varepsilon}{\mu\bigl(v(z,t),z\bigr)}f^2(z,t) \\
& \quad - \sum_{i=1}^{n_1}\bar{c}_{i,1}(z)\biggl(\dpd{x_{i,1}(z,t)}{t}\biggr)^2 - \sum_{i=1}^{n_2}\bar{c}_{i,2}(z)\biggl(\dpd{x_{i,2}(z,t)}{t}\biggr)^2,  \\

\end{split}
\end{align}

\begin{align}
\begin{split}

\ensuremath{_\textnormal{b}}(\tilde{z})}\biggr)}= \sqrt{1+\alpha_3^2(z,\tilde{z})} + \abs{\alpha_3(z,\tilde{z})}, \\
\norm{\mathbf{\Phi}_4(z,\tilde{z})} & = 1,
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
with $\alpha_i(z,\tilde{z})$, $i \in \{1,2,3\}$ as in Eq.~\eqref{eq:functionsAlpha}.

Hence, combining Eq.~\eqref{eq:solutionZI} with~\eqref{eq:boundfFric},~\eqref{coeffs:K}, and~\eqref{eq:gbarBound}, and exploiting the fact that the ICs vanish for $t\geq \lambda_i^{-1}$, it is possible to infer the existence of an arbitraty constant $\varrho \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ verifying
\begin{align}\label{eq:bound1Xi}
\begin{split}
\norm{\bm{\xi}_i(z,t)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2)} & \leq K_{i1}\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{-\varrho(t-\lambda_i^{-1})}\norm{\bm{\xi}_{i,0}(\cdot)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))} + K_{i2} \sup_{t^\prime\in[0,t]}\norm{\bm{\widetilde{h}}_i(t^\prime)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2)} +K_{i3}\sup_{t^\prime\in[0,t]}\bar{g}_i(t^\prime) \\
& \quad + K_{if}(\overline{\bm{w}})\sup_{t^\prime\in[0,t]}\Bigl(C_{f1}\bigl(\bm{X}_0(\cdot)\bigr)+ C_{f2}C_v\norm{\bm{w}_4(\cdot,t^\prime)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))}\Bigr), \\
& \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad t \in [0,T],\; i\in \{1,2,3,4\},
\end{split}
\end{align}
with $\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0} \ni K_{if} (\overline{\bm{w}})\triangleq K_{i3}\phi_{if}(\overline{\bm{w}})$, $i \in \{1,2,3,4\}$.
Furthermore, using the fact that the inequality
\begin{align}
\begin{split}
& K_{i2} \sup_{t^\prime\in[0,t]}\norm{\bm{\widetilde{h}}_i(t^\prime)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2)} + \quad K_{i3}\sup_{t^\prime\in[0,t]}\bar{g}_i(t^\prime) \\
& \quad + K_{if}(\overline{\bm{w}})\sup_{t^\prime\in[0,t]}\Bigl(C_{f1}\bigl(\bm{X}_0(\cdot)\bigr)+ C_{f2}C_v\norm{\bm{w}_4(\cdot,t^\prime)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))}\Bigr)\\
& \quad \leq \sup_{t^\prime \in [0,t]}\Biggl[ \eta_{i1}K_{i2}\norm{\bm{\widetilde{h}}_i(t^\prime)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2)} +\eta_{i2} K_{i3}\bar{g}(t^\prime) + \eta_{i3}K_{if}(\overline{\bm{w}})\sup_{t^\prime\in[0,t]}\Bigl(C_{f1} + C_{f2}C_v\norm{\bm{w}_4(\cdot,t^\prime)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))}\Bigr) \Biggr]
\end{split}
\end{align}
holds for every $\eta_{i1},\eta_{i2},\eta_{i3} \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ satisfying $\sum_{j=1}^3 \eta_{ij}^{-1}=1$, $i \in \{1,2,3,4\}$, the following ISS estimate is obtained from Eq.~\eqref{eq:bound1Xi}:
\begin{align}
\begin{split}
&  \norm{\bm{w}_i(z,t)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2)} \leq \sup_{t^\prime \in [0,t]}\Biggl[ \eta_{i1}K_{i2}\norm{\bm{\widetilde{h}}_i(t^\prime)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2)} +\eta_{i2} K_{i3}\bar{g}(t^\prime) \\
& \quad + \eta_{i3}K_{if}(\overline{\bm{w}})\sup_{t^\prime\in[0,t]}\Bigl(C_{f1}\bigl(\bm{X}_0(\cdot)\bigr) + C_{f2}C_v\norm{\bm{w}_4(\cdot,t^\prime)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))}\Bigr) \Biggr]\\
& \qquad+  K_{i1}\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{-\varrho(t-\lambda_i^{-1})}\norm{\bm{w}_{i,0}(\cdot)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))}, \quad t \in [0,T],\; i\in \{1,2,3,4\}.
\end{split}
\end{align}
Since the semigroup property holds for the solution~\eqref{eq:solutionZI}, $i \in\{1,2,3,4\}$, it is possible to invoke the fading memory inequality \cite[Lemma~7.1]{Krstic2} to conclude the existence of an arbitrary small constant $\epsilon \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ such that
\begin{align}\label{eq:fadingEstimate}
\begin{split}
&  \norm{\bm{w}_i(z,t)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2)}\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{\delta t} \\
& \quad \leq (1+\epsilon)\sup_{t^\prime \in [0,t]}\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{\delta t^\prime}\biggl( \eta_{i1}K_{i2}\norm{\bm{\widetilde{h}}_i(t^\prime)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2)} +\eta_{i2} K_{i3}\bar{g}(t^\prime) + \eta_{i3}K_{if}(\overline{\bm{w}}) C_{f2}C_v\norm{\bm{w}_4(\cdot,t^\prime)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))} \biggr)\\
& \qquad+  K_{i1}\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{\varrho\lambda_i^{-1}}\norm{\bm{w}_{i,0}(\cdot)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))} +(1+\epsilon)\eta_{i3}K_{if}(\overline{\bm{w}})C_{f1}\bigl(\bm{X}_0(\cdot)\bigr)\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{\delta t}, \quad t \in [0,T],\; i\in \{1,2,3,4\},
\end{split}
\end{align}
where $\delta \in (0,\varrho)$ depends only on $\varrho$ and $\lambda_i,K_{i1}$, $i \in \{1,2,3,4\}$. Consequently, recalling the bounds~\eqref{eq:boundsg_ihi} and defining the vectors $\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^4 \ni \bm{W}^\delta(t) \triangleq [W_1^\delta(t)\; W_2^\delta(t)\; W_3^\delta(t)\; W_4^\delta(t)]^{\mathrm{T}}$ and $\bm{\beta}^\delta (t) \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^3$ as
\begin{subequations}
\begin{align}
W_i^\delta(t) & \triangleq \sup_{t^\prime \in [0,t]}\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{\delta t^\prime} \norm{\bm{w}_i(\cdot,t^\prime)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))}, && i \in \{1,2,3,4\}, \\
\bm{\beta}^\delta(t) & \triangleq \begin{bmatrix} \ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{\delta t}C_{f1}\bigl(\bm{X}_0(\cdot)\bigr)\\ \ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{\delta t}\norm{\bm{g}_0(\cdot)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^8))}, \\ \sup_{t^\prime \in[0,t]} \ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{\delta t^\prime} \norm{\bm{h}_0(t^\prime)}_{\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^8)} \end{bmatrix}.
\end{align}
\end{subequations}
using both bounds in~\eqref{eq:boundsg_ihi}, taking the supremum in~\eqref{eq:fadingEstimate}, and collecting the four resulting inequalities, provides
\begin{align}\label{eq:EqEdelta} 
\bigl( \mathbf{I}_4- (1+\epsilon) \mathbf{\Theta}_w \bigr) \bm{W}^\delta(t) \leq \mathbf{A}(\varrho)\bm{W}_0 + (1+\epsilon) \mathbf{B}_w \bm{\beta}^\delta(t), \quad t\in[0,T], \end{align}
where, to alleviate the notation, the following matrices $\mathbf{\Theta}_w \in \mathbf{M}_4(\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0})$ and $\mathbf{B}_w \in \mathbf{M}_{4\times 3}(\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0})$ have been defined:
\begin{subequations} 
\begin{align} 
\mathbf{\Theta}_{w} &\triangleq \mathbf{\Theta} \bigl( \bm{\phi}_f(\overline{\bm{w}}), \bm{\eta}, \overline{\bm{w}} \bigr), \\ 
\mathbf{B}_{w} &\triangleq \mathbf{B} \bigl( \bm{\phi}_f(\overline{\bm{w}}), \bm{\eta}, \overline{\bm{w}} \bigr). 
\end{align} 
\end{subequations}
By virtue of Eq.~\eqref{eq:SpectralRadius}, $\rho(\mathbf{\Theta}_{w})<1$, and, since the spectral radius is continuous, there exists $\epsilon_0\in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ such that $\rho( (1+\epsilon)\mathbf{\Theta}_{w} )<1$ for all $\epsilon \in (0,\epsilon_0]$. In particular, the matrix-valued function $\mathbf{R}_w \in C^0([0,\epsilon_0];\mathbf{M}_4(\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}))$, reading 
\begin{align} \mathbf{R}_w(\epsilon) \triangleq \bigl( \mathbf{I}_4- (1+\epsilon)\mathbf{\Theta}_{w} \bigr)^{-1} = \sum_{k=0}^\infty (1+\epsilon)^k \mathbf{\Theta}_{w}^k, 
\end{align} 
is well-defined and componentwise nonnegative. Thus, premultiplying Eq.~\eqref{eq:EqEdelta} by $\mathbf{R}_w(\epsilon)$, then multiplying by $\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{-\delta t}$, and recalling Eq.~\eqref{eq:W0Betas} yields 
\begin{align}\label{eq:DwWW_dina} 
\bm{W}(t) \leq \mathbf{R}_w(\epsilon) \Bigl[ \mathbf{A}(\varrho)\bm{W}_0\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{-\delta t} + (1+\epsilon) \mathbf{B}_{w}\bm{\beta} \Bigr], \quad t\in[0,T], 
\end{align}
since $\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{-\delta t}\bm{\beta}^\delta(t)\leq\bm{\beta}$ componentwise. It remains to show that $\varrho \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ and $\epsilon \in (0,\epsilon_0]$ may be selected so that $\bm{w}(\cdot,t) \in \overline{\mathcal{B}}_w$ for all times. To this end, the following function $\bm{\mathcal{F}} \in C^0(\mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}\times[0,\epsilon_0];\mathbb{R}^4)$ is conveniently introduced: 
\begin{align}\label{eq:strictMarginMap} 
\bm{\mathcal{F}}(\varrho,\epsilon) \triangleq \overline{\bm{w}} - \mathbf{R}_w(\epsilon)\bigl[ \mathbf{A}(\varrho)\bm{W}_0 + (1+\epsilon) \mathbf{B}_{w}\bm{\beta} \bigr]. 
\end{align} 
From Eq.~\eqref{eq:condInitiDHDD}, it follows that 
\begin{align} 
\bm{\mathcal{F}}(0,0) = \overline{\bm{w}} - \mathbf{R}_w(0) \bigl[ \mathbf{A}(0)\bm{W}_0 + \mathbf{B}_{w}\bm{\beta} \bigr] >\bm{0}, 
\end{align} 
since multiplication by the componentwise nonnegative matrix $\mathbf{R}_w(\epsilon)$ preserves the strict componentwise inequality. Furthermore, the mapping $(\varrho,\epsilon)\mapsto \bm{\mathcal{F}}(\varrho,\epsilon)$ is continuous in a neighbourhood of $(0,0)$, implying the existence of $\varrho_0 \in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$, $\epsilon_1\in(0,\epsilon_0]$, and $\mathbb{R}_{>0}^4 \ni \bm{\nu} \triangleq [\nu_1\; \nu_2\; \nu_3\; \nu_4]^{\mathrm{T}}$ such that 
\begin{align}\label{eq:uniformStrictMargin} 
\bm{\mathcal{F}}(\varrho,\epsilon) \geq\bm{\nu}>\bm{0} 
\end{align} for every $(\varrho,\epsilon)\in(0,\varrho_0]\times(0,\epsilon_1]$. Hence, choosing sufficiently small $(\varrho,\epsilon)\in(0,\varrho_0]\times(0,\epsilon_1]$, and using the fact that $\ensuremath{\mathrm{e}}^{-\delta t}\leq1$, Eqs.~\eqref{eq:DwWW_dina} and~\eqref{eq:uniformStrictMargin} give 
\begin{align}\label{eq:strictBoxBound} 
\bm{W}(t) \leq \overline{\bm{w}}-\bm{\nu}, \quad t\in[0,T]. 
\end{align} 
Since $T\in (0,t_w)$ was arbitrary, Eq.~\eqref{eq:strictBoxBound} holds for every $t\in[0,t_w)$. Suppose, by contradiction, that $t_w<t 
\ensuremath{_\textnormal{max}}$: passing to the limit as $t\uparrow t_w$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:strictBoxBound}, and exploiting the continuity of $t\mapsto\bm{w}(\cdot,t)$ provides
\begin{align} 
\norm{\bm{w}_i(\cdot,t_w)}_{C^0([0,1];\ell^2(\mathbb{R}^2))} \leq \overline{w}_i-\nu_i < \overline{w}_i, \quad i\in\{1,2,3,4\}, 
\end{align} 
implying that $\bm{w}(\cdot,t_w) \in \operatorname{int}\overline{\mathcal{B}}_w$. By continuity, there exists $h\in \mathbb{R}_{>0}$ such that $\bm{w}(\cdot,t)\in \operatorname{int}\overline{\mathcal{B}}_w$ for all $t\in[t_w, t_w+h]$, contradicting the definition of $t_w$ in Eq.~\eqref{eq:firstExitTime}. Thus, $t_w = t 
\ensuremath{_\textnormal{max}}$, and $\sup_{t\in[0,t 
\ensuremath{_\textnormal{max}})} \norm{\bm{w}(\cdot,t)}_{C^0([0,1];\mathbb{R}^8)} <\infty$. Consequently, recalling Eq.~\eqref{eq:boundfDOUSX}, it may be concluded that $\sup_{t\in[0,t 
\ensuremath{_\textnormal{max}})} \norm{ ( \bm{X}(\cdot,t),\bm{w}(\cdot,t) )}_{C^0([0,1];\mathbb{R}^{n_X+8})} <\infty$, which excludes the blow-up alternative of Theorem~\ref{thm:Local}. Hence, $t_{\max}=\infty$, and Eq.~\eqref{eq:strictBoxBound} proves that $\bm{w}(\cdot,t)\in\overline{\mathcal{B}}_w$ for all $t\in\mathbb{R}_{\geq0}$. Finally, since the bound~\eqref{eq:DwWW_dina} is valid globally in time, combining~\eqref{eq:DwWW_dina} with~\eqref{eq:boundfDOUSX}, and using equivalence of norms on finite-dimensional spaces, permits deducing the existence of constants $\Theta_X,\Theta_w,\gamma\in\mathbb{R}_{>0}$ such that the estimate~\eqref{eq:BoundedandISSEst} holds for all $t\in\mathbb{R}_{\geq0}$.
