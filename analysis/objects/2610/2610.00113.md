Let $\mathcal C=(c_{i_{1}\cdots i_{m}})\in\mathbb{ R}^{[m,n]},$ if $x=(x_{1},x_{2}\cdots,x_{m})^{\top}\in\mathbb{ R}^{n}\setminus\{0\}$ is a nonzero real vector, $\lambda\in\mathbb{ R}$ is a real number, which satisfies the following tensor Z-eigenvalue complementarity problem:
\begin{equation*}
0\leq x\perp(\lambda x-\mathcal Cx^{m-1})\geq0\ \mbox{and} \ x^{\top}x=1,
\end{equation*}
then $\lambda$ is said to be a Pareto Z-eigenvalue of $\mathcal C$, $x$ is called the corresponding Pareto Z-eigenvector.

\begin{equation}\label{2.1}
\lambda x_{p}^{2}=\sum_{\substack{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\in [n] \\ p\in\{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\}}}c_{pi_{2}\cdots i_{m}}x_{p}x_{i_{2}}\cdots x_{i_{m}}+\sum_{\substack{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\in [n] \\ p\notin\{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\}}}c_{pi_{2}\cdots i_{m}}x_{p}x_{i_{2}}\cdots x_{i_{m}}.
\end{equation}
Taking the absolute value of both sides of Equation (\ref{2.1}), it holds that
\begin{equation*}
\begin{aligned}
|\lambda|x_{p}^{2}&=\Bigg|\sum_{\substack{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\in [n] \\ q\in\{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\}}}c_{pi_{2}\cdots i_{m}}x_{p}x_{i_{2}}\cdots x_{i_{m}}+\sum_{\substack{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\in [n] \\ q\notin\{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\}}}c_{pi_{2}\cdots i_{m}}x_{p}x_{i_{2}}\cdots x_{i_{m}}\Bigg|\\

\end{equation*}

\end{equation}
From Definition \ref{dy:1.1}, we have

\lambda x_{i}^{2}=\sum_{i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\in [n]}c_{ii_{2}\cdots i_{m}}x_{i}x_{i_{2}}\cdots x_{i_{m}}.
\end{equation}
Denote $x_{\alpha}=\max\{x_{i_{1}} x_{i_{2}} \cdots x_{i_{m}}\}:c_{i_{1}i_{2}\cdots i_{m}}\neq0, i_{1},i_{2},\cdots,i_{m}\in[n]\},$ Taking the modulus of \eqref{2.4}, for all $i\in[n],$ we can conclude that
