<!-- ansatz-1: body_md -->
\label{ansatz:mTO}
The strong topological operators of a (3+1)D mixed state topological order form a braided fusion 2-category.
<!-- end ansatz-1 -->
<!-- notation--1: body_md -->
In this Ansatz, and for all the examples in this paper, we are only concerned with \textit{bosonic} topological orders, which amounts to the fact that the theory does not need to couple to a spin structure to be defined. Even though the topological order is bosonic, there can still be emergent fermions in the spectrum of operators as discussed in Sec. \ref{sec:latmod}. To distinguish whether or not a theory has an emergent fermion we use the following terminology. In the case when all line operators of a bosonic topological order are bosons (loops may still carry fermionic statistics), the theory will be described by a ``bosonic'' braided fusion 2-category. In the case when the spectrum contains emergent fermions, the theory will be described by a ``emergently-fermionic'' (EF) braided fusion 2-category. We will not be concerned with inherently fermionic (i)mTOs in this paper, which require a spin structure to be defined.
<!-- end notation--1 -->
<!-- defn-1: body_md -->
\label{def:strongly}
     A bosonic (resp. EF) braided fusion 2-category ${\mathfrak C}$ with monoidal unit $\mathds{1}$ is a braided fusion 2-category such that $ \Omega {\mathfrak C}:=\operatorname{End}_{\mathds{1}}({\mathfrak C})$ is $\mathbf{Rep}(G)$ (resp. $\mathbf{Rep}(G,z)$), for some finite group $G$.
<!-- end defn-1 -->
<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:mainA}
Mixed state topological orders in (3+1)D whose point-like excitations are all bosons correspond to braided fusion 2-categories parametrized, up to equivalence, by the following data:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item \label{item:A1}A finite abelian group $E$;
    \item \label{item:A2} A class $\beta \in \mathrm{H}^5(B^2 E; \mathbb{C}^\times)$;
    \item \label{item:A3} A finite group N;
    \item \label{item:AmaptoPic}  A faithfully graded $N$-crossed braided extension ${\mathfrak C}$ of ${\mathfrak B} = \mathbf{2Vect}^\beta_E$;
    \item \label{item:A5} A finite group $H$;
    \item \label{Gaction} An action $\rho:B H \rightarrow B\mathscr{A}ut^{\mathrm{br}}({\mathfrak C}^N)$, where $\mathscr{A}ut^{\mathrm{br}}({\mathfrak C}^N)$ denotes the 3-group of braided autoequivalences of the equivariantization of ${\mathfrak C}$ by $N$.
    \end{enumerate}
<!-- end theorem-1 -->
<!-- theorem-2: body_md -->
\label{thm:mainB}
    Mixed state topological orders in (3+1)D whose point-like excitations include an emergent fermion correspond to braided fusion 2-categories parametrized, up to equivalence, by the following data:
    \begin{enumerate}
        \item \label{item:B1} A finite abelian group $E$;
        \item \label{item:B2} A class $\kappa \in \mathrm{H}^2(B^2 E; \mathbb{Z}_2)$ and a  supercohomology  class $\varsigma \in \mathrm{SH}^{5+\kappa}(B^2 E)$\footnote{We refer the reader to Appendix \ref{subsection:fermionicbackground} for the definition of supercohomology.};
        \item A finite group $N$;
        \item  \label{item:BmaptoPic} A faithfully graded $N$-crossed braided extension ${\mathfrak C}$ of ${\mathfrak B} = \mathbf{2SVect}^{(\kappa,\varsigma)}_{E}$, a braided fusion 2-category with $\mathbf{SVect}$ as the category of one-morphisms;
        \item \label{item:groupH} A finite group $H$;
        \item An action $\rho:B H\rightarrow B\mathscr{A}ut^{\mathrm{br}}({\mathfrak C}^N)$.
    \end{enumerate}
<!-- end theorem-2 -->
<!-- lemma-1: body_md -->
\label{lem:nonfaithful-crossed}
Let ${\mathfrak B}$ be a braided fusion $2$-category, and let $N \trianglelefteq G$ be a normal subgroup. Faithfully $N$-graded $G$-crossed braided extensions of $\mathcal B$ are classified by the following data:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A faithfully $N$-graded $N$-crossed braided extension
    ${\mathfrak C}$ of ${\mathfrak B}$;

    \item An action of $G$ on ${\mathfrak C}$ preserving the crossed
    braiding and inducing conjugation on the grading;

    \item An identification between the two induced actions of
    $N$ on ${\mathfrak C}$.
\end{enumerate}
<!-- end lemma-1 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
Let $\mathfrak{C}$ be a faithfully $N$-graded $G$-crossed braided extension of $\mathfrak{B}$. In particular, we have that $\mathfrak{C}$ is equipped with an action by $G$ that preserves the crossed braiding and acts by conjugation on the grading. Restricting this action to $N$, we find that $\mathfrak{C}$ is a faithfully graded $N$-crossed braided fusion 2-category. These two structures have to be compatible in the obvious way. Conversely, given the data (1)--(3), the $G$-action on $\mathcal{C}$ together with the identification (3) promotes the $N$-crossed braiding to a $G$-crossed braiding, yielding a faithfully $N$-graded $G$-crossed braided extension of $\mathfrak{B}$.
<!-- end proof-1 -->

<!-- corollary-1: body_md -->
\label{cor:BFSF2C}
Braided fermionic strongly fusion 2-categories are  classified by a finite abelian group $E$, a class $\kappa \in \mathrm{H}^2(B^2 E;{\mathbb Z}_2)$, and a class $\varsigma  \in \mathrm{SH}^{5+\kappa}(B^2 E)$.
<!-- end corollary-1 -->
<!-- remark-1: body_md -->
The theory $\mathbf{2SVect}^{(\kappa,\varsigma)}_E$ 
    parallels the theory  $\mathbf{2Vect}^\beta_E$ that was used to construct mTOs. More generally, for any group $E$ and $\kappa\neq 0$, $\mathrm{SH}^{5+\kappa}(B^2 E)\cong {\mathbb Z}_2$, and the contribution from this group lies completely in the row of the spectral sequence with $\mathbb{C}^\times$ coefficients. In contrast to Sec.~\ref{subsection:allboson}, pure-state TOs with an emergent fermion do \emph{not} arise from trivial $E$: $\mathbf{2SVect}$ itself is degenerate, as the fermion is transparent. Rather, they arise from equivariantizing faithfully $G$-graded $G$-crossed braided extensions of $\mathcal{S}=\mathbf{2SVect}^{(\kappa,0)}_{\mathbb{Z}_2}$~\cite{Kong:2024ykr}, while extensions of $\mathcal{T}$ yield imTOs.
<!-- end remark-1 -->
<!-- remark-2: body_md -->
Unlike the description with emergent fermion excitations, when the topological order has local fermions then it is genuinely a fermionic theory and requires a spin structure rather than a weaker twisted spin structure. Categorically, these theories are realized as $\mathbf{2SVect}$-central braided fusion 2-categories i.e. a braided fusion 2-category ${\mathfrak B}$ with a sylleptic monoidal 2-functor  $\mathbf{2SVect} \to \mathcal Z_{(2)}({\mathfrak B})$.
    We can realize these categories also from equivariantization of a $G$-action. Starting with a $G$-crossed strongly braided fusion 2-category ${\mathfrak D} = \boxplus_{g\in G}{\mathfrak D}_g$, we additionally require that there is a $G$-equivariant sylleptic monoidal 2-functor $\mathbf{2SVect} \to \mathcal Z_{(2)}({\mathfrak D}_e)$, where $\mathbf{2SVect}$ carries the trivial $G$-action.
<!-- end remark-2 -->
<!-- defn-2: body_md -->
\label{def:BF2C}
    Suppose ${\mathfrak B}$ is a monoidal 2-category with tensor bifunctor $\otimes$, associator $\alpha$, and pentagonator $\pi$. A \textit{braiding} on ${\mathfrak B}$ consists of a natural (in both variables) equivalence 
\begin{equation}\label{eq:braidingF2C}
    b_{x|y}:x \otimes y \to y \otimes x,
\end{equation}
 together with \textit{hexagonators} $R_{(x|-,-)}$ and $S_{(-,-|x)}$ that provide the monoidality of $b$:
 \begin{widetext}
\begin{equation}
\adjustbox{scale=0.75}{
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep = tiny]
                                                                    & (y \otimes x) \otimes z \arrow[r, "\alpha "]     & y \otimes (x \otimes z) \arrow[rd, "{b_{x|z}}"]       &                         \\
(x\otimes y)\otimes z \arrow[rd, "\alpha "] \arrow[ru, "{b_{x|y}}"] &       \hspace{15mm} \Downarrow                   & {\hspace{-15mm}R_{(x|y,z)}}                           & y \otimes( z \otimes x) \\
                                                                    & x \otimes (y \otimes z)  \arrow[r, "{b_{x|y z}}"] & (y \otimes z )\otimes x \arrow[l, phantom] \arrow[ru,"\alpha"] &                        
\end{tikzcd}
},
\quad
\adjustbox{scale=0.75}{
\begin{tikzcd}[column sep = tiny]
                                                                    & (z \otimes y) \otimes x \arrow[r, "{b_{zy|x}} "]     & x \otimes (z \otimes y) \arrow[rd, "\alpha"]       &                         \\
z\otimes (y\otimes x) \arrow[rd, "{b_{y|x}} "] \arrow[ru, "\alpha"] &       \hspace{15mm} \Downarrow                   & {\hspace{-15mm}S_{(z,y|x)}}                           & (x \otimes z) \otimes y \\
                                                                    & z \otimes (x \otimes y)  \arrow[r, "\alpha"] & (z \otimes x )\otimes y\,. \arrow[l, phantom] \arrow[ru,"{b_{z|x}}"] &                        
\end{tikzcd}}
\end{equation}
$R$ and $S$ must solve various equations in \cite[Appendix C.2]{SP}.
\end{widetext}
<!-- end defn-2 -->
<!-- ex-1: body_md -->
In Definition \ref{def:BF2C} the data of $\alpha$ and $b$ are automatically trivial
in the bosonic strongly fusion case.
However, there is still data of a 4-cocycle $\pi \in Z^4(B G; \mathbb{C}^\times)$ that one may  use to twist the pentagonator $\mathbf{2Vect}_G$. This produces the fusion 2-category $\mathbf{2Vect}^\pi_G$. The resulting fusion 2-category depends up to monoidal equivalence only on the class of $\pi$ in the cohomology group $\mathrm{H}^4(B G;\mathbb{C}^\times)$.  By \cite[Theorem A]{JFY} for $G$ a finite group all bosonic strongly fusion 2-categories arise as $G$-graded extensions of $\mathbf{2Vect}$, and take the form $\mathbf{2Vect}^\pi_G$.
<!-- end ex-1 -->
<!-- defn-3: body_md -->
The triple $(\pi,R,S)$ consisting of pentagonator $\pi$ and hexagonators $R$ and $S$, is the data of a \textit{braided cocycle}: they define a class in the Eilenberg--Mac Lane cohomology
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cohobraided}
 [\pi,R,S] \in \mathrm{H}^4_{\mathrm{br}}(E;\mathbb{C}^\times) := \mathrm{H}^5(B^2 E;\mathbb{C}^\times),
\end{equation}
for an abelian group $E$.
<!-- end defn-3 -->
<!-- defn-4: body_md -->
\label{def:syllepticcenter}
    The sylleptic center of a braided fusion 2-category ${\mathfrak B}$, denoted by $\mathcal Z_{(2)}({\mathfrak B})$, is the sylleptic fusion 2-category defined as follows
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Objects given by a pair $(A,v_{A,-})$ where $A$ is an object in ${\mathfrak B}$ and $v_{A,-}$ is an invertible modification:
        \begin{equation}
            \begin{tikzcd}[sep=tiny]
                A\otimes X \arrow[rr,"\cong"] \arrow[rdd,"b_{A|X}",swap]&  &A\otimes X\\
               & {\Downarrow v_{A||X}} & \\
                & X\otimes A \arrow[ruu,"b_{X|A}",swap]&
            \end{tikzcd}
        \end{equation}
        \item The 1-morphisms between $(A,v_A)$ and $(A',v_{A'})$ are 1-morphisms $f:A \to A'$ in $\mathfrak{B}$ such that the following diagram commutes:
         \begin{equation}
            \begin{tikzcd}[sep=tiny]
                A\otimes X \arrow[rr,"\cong"] \arrow[ddd]\arrow[rdd,"b_{A|X}",swap]&  &A\otimes X \arrow[ddd]\\
               & {\Downarrow v_{A||X}} & \\
                & X\otimes A \arrow[ruu,"b_{X|A}",swap] \arrow[ddd]& \\
                A'\otimes X \arrow[rr] \arrow[rdd,"b_{A'|X}",swap]& & A'\otimes X \\
                 & {\phantom{aaa}\Downarrow v_{A'||X}} & \\
                 & X\otimes A' \arrow[ruu,"b_{X|A'}",swap]&
            \end{tikzcd}
        \end{equation}
        \item The 2-morphisms are exactly the 2-morphisms in $\mathfrak{B}$.
    \end{itemize}

\label{thm:crossedextn}
Let ${\mathfrak B}$ be a braided fusion 2-category. Faithfully graded $G$-crossed braided extensions of ${\mathfrak B}$ are classified by homotopy classes of maps $B G \rightarrow B \mathscr{P}ic({\mathfrak B})$.

\label{prop:braidedextn}
    Let ${\mathfrak B}$ be a braided fusion 2-category, and $E$ a finite abelian group. Faithfully $E$-graded braided extensions of $\mathfrak{B}$ are classified by homotopy classes of maps $B^2 E \rightarrow B^2 \mathscr{P}ic^{\mathrm{br}}(\mathfrak{B})$.

<!-- theorem-4: body_md -->
\label{thm:equiv}
    There is an equivalence of 3-categories between braided fusion 2-categories containing $\mathbf{2Rep}(G)$ fully faithfully and $G$-crossed braided fusion 2-categories whose
trivially graded component is nondegenerate.
<!-- end theorem-4 -->
<!-- defn-5: body_md -->
Given a space $X$ equipped with a map $\kappa:X\to  B^2{\mathbb Z}_2$, or, equivalently, a $B{\mathbb Z}_2$-equivariant structure, the \textit{$\kappa$-twisted $n$-th supercohomology group} of $X$ is the group $\mathrm{SH}^{n+\kappa}(X)$ of homotopy classes of $B{\mathbb Z}_2$-equivariant maps from $X\rightarrow B^{n-2}\mathbf{2SVect}^\times$. In other words, maps over $B {\mathbb Z}_2$ of the form
\begin{equation}
    \begin{tikzcd}
        X \arrow[rr] \arrow[rd,"\kappa"]& & B^{n-2}\mathbf{2SVect}^\times\arrow[dl]\\
 &B^2 {\mathbb Z}_2\,. &  
    \end{tikzcd}
\end{equation}
