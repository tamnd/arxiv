\[
\|\boldsymbol{x}_R(p_*) - \boldsymbol{p}_R(p_*)\|_2 = \|(C_x - C_p)\boldsymbol{q}_R(p_*)\|_2 \le \|C_x - C_p\|_2 \|\boldsymbol{q}_R(p_*)\|_2.

The proof combines the Hermite interpolation formula \cite[Eq. 4.2]{Saf2010} \cite[\S 11]{Tre2020}. with logarithmic potential theory \cite{Saf2010}

According to \cite[Thm. 11.1]{Tre2020}, the polynomial interpolant at the snapshot points can be written as

Note that $\boldsymbol{p}(p_*) \in \mbox{span}(X)$. The error $ \boldsymbol{x}(p_*) -\boldsymbol{p}(p_*)$ satisfies

Note that our problem has a crucial difference from \cite[\S 5.2]{Jon2025}, that is, the bound in \eqref{eqn:firstbound} and in \eqref{eq: bound}, does \textit{not} depend on $A(p)$ but only on $X$.
