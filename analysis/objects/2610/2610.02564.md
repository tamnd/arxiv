\label{thm1}

Let the time series data $\{\mathbf{x}_n,\mathbf{y}_n\}_{n=1}^N$ be samples of the solution to \eqref{eq:ode} at $t_n = n\Delta t$, for some fixed $\Delta t>0$.
Let $\Phi_{\mathbf{x}}$ denote the one-step map induced by a globally order-$q$ numerical time stepping method for the $\mathbf{x}$-component of \eqref{eq:ode}.
Assume that the KRR predictor $\widetilde{\Phi}_y$, as defined in \eqref{eq:KRRPredictor}, satisfies \eqref{approxerror}.
Define the degree-$p$ stable interpolation operator, $\mathcal{I}_n: \mathbb{R}^{(p+1)\times d_y}\to C\big([t_n,t_{n+1}];\mathbb{R}^{d_y}\big)$.
For fixed $T = N_T \Delta t >0$,
    \[
\max_{0\leq n \leq N_T}
\|\mathbf{x}_n - \widehat{\mathbf{x}}_n\|
  \leq
    C_T
    [\delta(\varepsilon,N)+ (\Delta t)^{p+1} + (\Delta t)^q]
,
    \]
where $\widehat{\mathbf{x}}_n$ is defined by \eqref{eq:estimator} with initial conditions $\widehat{\mathbf{x}}_0=\mathbf{x}_0, \widehat{\mathbf{y}}_0=\mathbf{y}_0$.
