<!-- head-1: body_md -->
In the zero-displacement case, 
a CVGS  $\rho_\sigma$ is a state with a Wigner function of the form \cite{Weedbrook12,Ferraro05,Wang07,Adesso14,Navarrete22}
\begin{equation}
\!\!\!\begin{array}{l}
\mathcal{W}_{\!\varrho_\sigma} (q,p)\!=\!\frac{2}{h \sqrt{\det\sigma}}\, {\rm exp}\left\{\!-\frac{2\pi }{h} {\scriptsize (\,q\ \ p\, )}{\scriptsize \left(\!\!\!
\begin{array}{ll}
\sigma_{11} & \!\sigma_{12}\\
\sigma_{12} & \!\sigma_{22}
\end{array}\!\!\!\right)^{\!\!-1} \!\!\!\left(\!\!
\begin{array}{c}
q\\
p
\end{array}\!\!\right)}\!\right\},
\end{array}
\end{equation}
where  the covariance matrix
\begin{equation}\label{sigcon}
\sigma\!=\!{\scriptsize \left(\!\!
\begin{array}{cc}
\sigma_{11} & \sigma_{12}\\
\sigma_{12} & \sigma_{22}
\end{array}\!\!\right)},\quad \mbox{satisfying}\quad \sigma \!+\!{\rm i} {\scriptsize \left(\!\!
\begin{array}{cc}
0 & 1\\
-1 & 0
\end{array}\!\!\right)}\!\geq \!0,
\end{equation}
is a real $2\!\times\!2$ symmetric positive-definite matrix.
<!-- end head-1 -->
<!-- head-2: body_md -->
If  $\mathfrak{B}\!=\!\{ |\psi_1\rangle, \, |\psi_2\rangle,\, ...\, , \, |\psi_d\rangle\}$ and 
$\mathfrak{B}'\!=\!\{ |\varphi_1\rangle, \, |\varphi_2\rangle,\, ...\, , \, |\varphi_d\rangle\}$ are two 
orthonormal bases in $\mathcal{H}$, then 
$\mathfrak{F}\!=\!\{ |\psi_1\rangle, \, |\psi_2\rangle,\, ...\, , \, |\psi_d\rangle, |\varphi_1\rangle, \, |\varphi_2\rangle,\, ...\, , \, |\varphi_d\rangle\}$
is a tight frame in $\mathcal{H}$.\\
<!-- end head-2 -->
<!-- head-3: body_md -->
The discrete coherent states \cite{Vourdas04,Tolar97,Klimov09} \\
$|n,k\rangle=\tilde{D}(n,k)\tilde{g}_1$, defined explicitly as
\begin{equation} 
\langle m|n,k\rangle=\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi \mathrm{i}}{d}kn}\ 
\mathrm{e}^{\frac{2\pi \mathrm{i}}{d}km}\ \tilde{g}_1(m-n),
\end{equation}
form a tight frame in $\mathcal{H}_d$, namely
$\frac{1}{d}\!\!\sum\limits_{n,k=-s}^s\!\!|n,k\rangle\langle n,k|\!=\!\mathbb{I}.$\\
<!-- end head-3 -->
<!-- head-4: body_md -->
The canonical coherent states 
$|q,p\rangle=D(q,p)|g_1\rangle$, where $g_1(x)=\sqrt[4]{2/h}\ \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{h}x^2}$ is the groundstate of the quantum harmonic oscillator, 
is the most remarkable known tight frame \cite{Gazeau09} in the infinite-dimensional Hilbert space $L^2(\mathbb{R})$. The functions $|q,p\rangle$, explicitly described as
\begin{equation} \label{coherentst}
\langle x|q,p\rangle=\sqrt[4]{\frac{2}{h}}\ \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi \mathrm{i}}{h}pq}\,
\mathrm{e}^{\frac{2\pi \mathrm{i}}{h}px}\ \mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{h}(x-q)^2},
\end{equation}
satisfy the identity 
$\frac{1}{2\pi}\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^2}|q,p\rangle\langle q,p| \, dqdp=\mathbb{I}.$
<!-- end head-4 -->
<!-- head-5: body_md -->
The {\it $\ell_1$-norm of coherence} of a state $\varrho$ with respect to the frame $\mathcal{F}=\{ \, |\varphi _1\rangle,\, |\varphi _2\rangle,\, ...\, |\varphi _m\rangle\, \}$ is
\begin{equation} \label{framecoherence}    
\mathcal{C}_\mathcal{F}(\varrho)=\frac{d^2}{2m^2}\sum\limits_{j,k=1}^m|\langle\varphi_j|\varrho|\varphi_k\rangle|.
\end{equation}
<!-- end head-5 -->
<!-- head-6: body_md -->
The frame-dependent coherence of CVGS $\varrho_\sigma$ with respect to the frame of canonical coherent states is
\begin{equation} \label{coherencecsigma}
\mathcal{C}_\mathcal{F}(\varrho_\sigma)=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\det\sigma}}\sqrt{\det \sigma + \mathrm{tr}\, \sigma +1},
\end{equation}
where \ $\mathrm{det}\, \sigma=\sigma_{11}\sigma_{22}-\sigma^2_{12}$ \ and \ 
$\mathrm{tr}\, \sigma =\sigma_{11}+\sigma_{22}$.\\
{\it Proof.} See End Matter.\\

\left[\beta +\delta-\frac{c}{a+1}(\alpha +\gamma)\right]^2}
\end{array}
\]




By denoting $\eta\!=\!(a\!+\!1)(b\!+\!1)-c^2$ and using the change of variables $x\!=\!\alpha\!+\!\gamma$, $y\!=\!\beta\!+\!\delta$,
$u\!=\!\alpha\!-\!\gamma$, $v\!=\!\beta\!-\!\delta$, we get
\[
\begin{array}{l}
\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^4}|\langle \alpha,\beta|\varrho_\sigma|\gamma,\delta\rangle|d\alpha d\beta d\gamma d\delta =\\[3mm]
\qquad \qquad =\frac{1}{4\sqrt{\det\sigma}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\eta}}\, 
\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^4}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{a}{a+1}x^2}
\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}y^2}
\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{1}{a+1}v^2} 
\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h} \frac{a+1}{\eta}
\left[u+\frac{c}{a+1}v\right]^2} 
\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\pi}{2h} \frac{a+1}{\eta}
\left[y-\frac{c}{a+1}x\right]^2}dxdydudv\\[2mm]
              
\qquad \qquad =\frac{1}{4\sqrt{\det\sigma}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\eta}}\, 
\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^2}
\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h} \frac{a+1}{\eta}
\left[u+\frac{c}{a+1}v\right]^2}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{1}{a+1}v^2}  dudv\
\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^2}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h} \frac{a+1}{\eta}
\left[y-\frac{c}{a+1}x\right]^2}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{a}{a+1}x^2}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}y^2}dxdy\\[2mm]
              
\qquad \qquad =\frac{1}{4\sqrt{\det\sigma}}\frac{1}{\sqrt{\eta}}\, 
\sqrt{\frac{2h}{\pi}}\frac{\sqrt{\eta}}{\sqrt{a+1}} \sqrt{\pi}\ 
\sqrt{\frac{2h}{\pi}}\sqrt{a+1} \sqrt{\pi}\\[3mm]
 
\qquad \qquad \qquad \qquad\int\limits_{\mathbb{R}^2}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h} \left[\sqrt{\frac{ab+b-c^2}{\eta}}y+\frac{c}{\eta}
\sqrt{\frac{\eta}{ab+b-c^2}}x\right]^2}dy \ 
\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{c^2}{\eta(a+1)}x^2}\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{a}{a+1}x^2}\mathrm{e}^{\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{c^2}{\eta^2}\frac{\eta}{ab+b-c^2}x^2}dx\\[2mm]
              
\qquad \qquad =\frac{h}{2\sqrt{\det\sigma}}
\int\limits_{-\infty}^\infty\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h} \left[\sqrt{\frac{ab+b-c^2}{\eta}}y+\frac{c}{\eta}
\sqrt{\frac{\eta}{ab+b-c^2}}x\right]^2}dy \ 
\int\limits_{-\infty}^\infty
\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{ab-c^2}{ab+b-c^2}x^2}dx\\[2mm]
              
\qquad \qquad =\frac{h}{2\sqrt{\det\sigma}}
\sqrt{\frac{2h}{\pi}}\frac{\sqrt{\eta}}{\sqrt{ab+b-c^2}} \sqrt{\pi}\ 
\int\limits_{-\infty}^\infty
\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{ab-c^2}{ab+b-c^2}x^2}dx\\[2mm]
              
\qquad \qquad =\frac{h\sqrt{h}}{\sqrt{2\, \det\sigma}}
\frac{\sqrt{\eta}}{\sqrt{ab+b-c^2}}\ 
\int\limits_{-\infty}^\infty
\mathrm{e}^{-\frac{\pi}{2h}\frac{ab-c^2}{ab+b-c^2}x^2}dx=\frac{h\sqrt{h}}{\sqrt{2\, \det\sigma}}
\frac{\sqrt{\eta}}{\sqrt{ab+b-c^2}}\ \sqrt{\frac{2h}{\pi}}\frac{\sqrt{ab+b-c^2}}{\sqrt{ab-c^2}} \sqrt{\pi}\ 
\\[2mm]
              
\qquad \qquad =\frac{h^2}{\sqrt{\det\sigma}}
\frac{\sqrt{\eta}}{\sqrt{ab-c^2}}  
 =\frac{h^2}{\sqrt{\det\sigma}}
\frac{\sqrt{ab+a+b+1-c^2}}{\sqrt{ab-c^2}}  
 =\frac{h^2}{\sqrt{\det\sigma}}
\sqrt{ \det\sigma +\mathrm{tr}\, \sigma +1}.
\end{array}
\]
