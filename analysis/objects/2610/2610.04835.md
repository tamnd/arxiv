<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:unified-main}
For \(\epsilon\in\{0,1/2\}\), the nontrivial simple finite
Cartan-free modules over \(\mathfrak g^\epsilon\) or
\(\widehat{\mathfrak g}^{\,\epsilon}\) are exactly
\[
 \Pi^\delta H_{\epsilon,b}(P),\qquad
 \delta\in\mathbb Z_2,\quad b\in\mathbb C\setminus\{\tfrac12\},
\]
where \(P\) is a simple \(\mathcal D\)-module finite free over \(\mathbb C[D]\)
and \(C\) acts trivially in the centrally extended case.
The parameters \(b,\delta\) and the isomorphism class of \(P\)
are uniquely determined.
<!-- end theorem-1 -->
<!-- corollary-1: body_md -->
\label{cor:finite-generated-simple}
Every simple \(\mathfrak g^\epsilon\)- or
\(\widehat{\mathfrak g}^{\,\epsilon}\)-module finitely generated
over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\) is either a one-dimensional trivial module,
concentrated in even or odd degree, or one of the modules in
\cref{thm:unified-main}.
<!-- end corollary-1 -->
<!-- lemma-1: body_md -->
\label{lem:weyl-finite-length}
Let \(E\) be a \(\mathcal D\)-module finitely generated over \(\mathbb C[D]\).
Then \(E\) is free of finite rank over \(\mathbb C[D]\), and its
composition length as a \(\mathcal D\)-module is at most
\(\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb C[D]}E\).
<!-- end lemma-1 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
The freeness assertion is the one-variable case of the result for
Laurent Weyl modules in \cite{GuoLiuLuZhao2020}.
Every nonzero \(\mathcal D\)-subquotient of \(E\) is finitely generated
over \(\mathbb C[D]\), and hence free of positive rank.
The length bound follows by additivity of rank.
<!-- end proof-1 -->
<!-- definition-1: body_md -->
\label{def:unified-H}
For \(\epsilon\in\{0,1/2\}\), \(b\in\mathbb C\), and a
\(\mathcal D\)-module \(P\), put \(H_{\epsilon,b}(P)=P\oplus\overline P\) with
\(P\) even and \(\overline P\) odd.  The action is given by
\begin{align}
 L_m v&=t^m\bigl(D+(m-2\epsilon)b\bigr)v,\notag\\
 L_m\overline v
   &=\overline{t^m\bigl(D+(m-2\epsilon)b+\tfrac{m+2\epsilon}{2}\bigr)v},
 \label{eq:H-even-components}\\
 G_pv&=\overline{t^{p-\epsilon}(D+2(p-\epsilon)b)v},\notag\\
 G_p\overline v&=-t^{p+\epsilon}v,
 \label{eq:H-odd-components}
\end{align}
where \(m\in\mathbb Z\), \(p\in\mathbb Z+\epsilon\), and \(v\in P\).
The central element acts by zero.
<!-- end definition-1 -->
<!-- lemma-2: body_md -->
\label{lem:automatic-balance}
Let \(M\) be a finite Cartan-free module with central charge
\(c\) in the centrally extended case.  Its two parity components
have equal rank over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\).
<!-- end lemma-2 -->
<!-- proof-2: body_md -->
Take \(c=0\) in the centerless case.
In the Ramond case, \(G_0^2=-(L_0+c/24)\), so \(G_0\) is
injective because \(M\) is torsion-free over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\).
In the Neveu--Schwarz case, if \(0\ne v\in\ker L_1\), the
commutation relations and \(\mathbb C[L_0]\)-torsion-freeness imply that
the vectors \(L_{-1}^nv\), \(n\ge0\), are linearly independent
over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\), contradicting the finite rank of \(M\).
Thus \(L_1\) is injective, and \(G_{1/2}^2=-L_1\) implies that
\(G_{1/2}\) is injective as well.
Since \(G_\epsilon f(L_0)=f(L_0-\epsilon)G_\epsilon\), the injective
operator \(G_\epsilon\) preserves \(\mathbb C[L_0]\)-linear independence.
Applying this to both parity components shows that their ranks are equal.
<!-- end proof-2 -->
<!-- remark-1: body_md -->
\label{rem:H-Cartan-rank}
Suppose that \(P\) has a \(\mathbb C[D]\)-basis \(v_1,\ldots,v_r\).
By \eqref{eq:H-even-components} with
\(m=0\), the sets \(\{v_1,\ldots,v_r\}\) and
\(\{\overline{v_1},\ldots,\overline{v_r}\}\) form \(\mathbb C[L_0]\)-bases
of the even and odd components of \(H_{\epsilon,b}(P)\), respectively.
For the centerless Ramond algebra, put
\(\mathfrak h_R=\mathbb C L_0\oplus\mathbb C G_0\).
Since \(G_0^2=-L_0\) and \(G_0\overline{v_i}=-v_i\), the vectors
\(\overline{v_1},\ldots,\overline{v_r}\) form a
\(U(\mathfrak h_R)\)-basis of \(H_{0,b}(P)\).
After parity change, this basis is even.
<!-- end remark-1 -->
<!-- proposition-1: body_md -->
\label{prop:unified-H-simple}
Let \(P\) be a simple \(\mathcal D\)-module finite free over \(\mathbb C[D]\).
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(H_{\epsilon,b}(P)\) is simple if and only if
\(b\ne1/2\).
\item The module \(H_{\epsilon,1/2}(P)\) contains the simple
Cartan-free submodule
\begin{equation}
 K_\epsilon(P)=P\oplus\overline{DP}\simeq\Pi H_{\epsilon,0}(P),
 \label{eq:exceptional-parity-zero}
\end{equation}
with purely odd trivial quotient \(\Pi(P/DP)\).
Moreover, \(H_{\epsilon,1/2}(P)\) has no nontrivial simple quotient.
\end{enumerate}
<!-- end proposition-1 -->
<!-- proof-3: body_md -->
For \textup{(i)}, finite \(\mathbb C[D]\)-freeness excludes the natural
Laurent module \(\mathbb C[t^{\pm1}]\) and gives \(DP\ne P\), so
\cite[Theorem~4.5]{ChenDaiLiuPei2024} applies.
For \textup{(ii)}, the simplicity of \(K_\epsilon(P)\) and
\eqref{eq:exceptional-parity-zero} follow from
\cite[proof of Lemma~4.4]{ChenDaiLiuPei2024}.
Its Cartan freeness follows from \Cref{rem:H-Cartan-rank}, and
the quotient is determined by the defining actions.
Since \(G_{-\epsilon}\overline v=-v\), every nonzero submodule
of \(H_{\epsilon,1/2}(P)\) meets \(K_\epsilon(P)\) nontrivially
and hence contains it.  Thus every simple quotient is trivial.
<!-- end proof-3 -->
<!-- corollary-2: body_md -->
\label{cor:unified-H-uniqueness}
Let \(P,P'\) be simple \(\mathcal D\)-modules finite free over
\(\mathbb C[D]\).  For \(b,b'\ne1/2\) and
\(\delta,\delta'\in\mathbb Z_2\), we have
\[
 \Pi^\delta H_{\epsilon,b}(P)
 \simeq\Pi^{\delta'}H_{\epsilon,b'}(P')
\]
if and only if \(\delta=\delta'\), \(b=b'\), and \(P\simeq P'\).
<!-- end corollary-2 -->
<!-- proof-4: body_md -->
By \eqref{eq:H-odd-components}, \(G_\epsilon\) maps the odd component
of \(H_{\epsilon,b}(P)\) onto its even component, but not conversely,
since \(DP\ne P\).  The same holds for \(H_{\epsilon,b'}(P')\).
Thus an isomorphism as above forces \(\delta=\delta'\).
The remaining assertions follow from
\cite[Lemmas~4.6--4.7]{ChenDaiLiuPei2024}.
<!-- end proof-4 -->
<!-- proposition-2: body_md -->
\label{prop:uniform-central-charge}
Let \(M\) be a nontrivial simple
\(\widehat{\mathfrak g}^{\,\epsilon}\)-module which is finite Cartan-free.
Then \(C\) acts trivially on \(M\).
<!-- end proposition-2 -->
<!-- proof-5: body_md -->
Since \(M\) is countable dimensional, Dixmier's lemma
\cite{Dixmier1977}, applied to even endomorphisms, gives
\(C|_M=c\,\mathrm{id}_M\) for some \(c\in\mathbb C\).
A nonzero cyclic graded submodule of
\(\mathcal W^\epsilon_\lambda(M)\) has a simple quotient, which is
uniformly bounded and has the same central charge \(c\).
The Harish--Chandra classification
\cite{Su1995,CaiLiuLu2021,CaiLu2022} therefore gives \(c=0\).
<!-- end proof-5 -->
<!-- lemma-3: body_md -->
\label{lem:uniform-four-differentiators-weight}
There exists \(N\geq2\) such that
\begin{equation*}
 \Omega^{(N)}_{X,Y}(k,p)
    \mathcal W^\epsilon_\lambda(M)=0
\end{equation*}
for all \(X,Y\in\{L,G\}\) and indices \(k,p\).
<!-- end lemma-3 -->
<!-- proof-6: body_md -->
The restriction of \(\mathcal W^\epsilon_\lambda(M)\) to the Witt
algebra spanned by the \(L_n\) is uniformly bounded and supported on
at most two cosets of \(\mathbb Z\).  Every nonzero subquotient
supported on a coset \(\alpha+\mathbb Z\) has a simple subquotient
and hence, by Mathieu's classification \cite{Mathieu1992}, a nonzero
weight space at \(\alpha\) if \(\alpha\notin\mathbb Z\), or at
\(0\) or \(1\) otherwise.  By additivity, the dimensions at these
finitely many weights bound the length of every strict submodule
chain.  Thus the restriction has finite composition length.

Applying
\cite[Lemmas~3.6--3.7]{CaiLiuLu2021} in the Ramond case and
\cite[Lemmas~2.2(1) and~4.3(1)]{CaiLu2022} in the Neveu--Schwarz
case, we find that the operators \(\Omega^{(N)}_{L,L}(k,p)\) and
\(\Omega^{(N)}_{G,L}(k,p)\) annihilate
\(\mathcal W^\epsilon_\lambda(M)\) at sufficiently large orders.  By
\eqref{eq:uniform-higher-difference}, we may choose a common order
\(N\geq2\).
For \(k\in\mathbb Z+\epsilon\) and \(p\in\mathbb Z\), the
commutation relations give
\begin{equation*}
 \Omega^{(N)}_{G,L}(k,p)
  =(-1)^N\Omega^{(N)}_{L,G}(p+N,k-N).
\end{equation*}
Finally, for \(k,s\in\mathbb Z+\epsilon\), we have
\[
 \frac32\Omega^{(N)}_{G,G}(k,s)
 =[\Omega^{(N)}_{G,L}(k,0),G_s]
 -[\Omega^{(N)}_{G,L}(k,1),G_{s-1}]=0
\]
on \(\mathcal W^\epsilon_\lambda(M)\).
<!-- end proof-6 -->
<!-- lemma-4: body_md -->
\label{lem:uniform-differentiator-lift}
There exists \(N\geq2\) such that
\begin{equation*}
 \Omega^{(N)}_{X,Y}(k,p)M=0
\end{equation*}
for all \(X,Y\in\{L,G\}\) and indices \(k,p\).
<!-- end lemma-4 -->
<!-- proof-7: body_md -->
Choose \(N\) as in \Cref{lem:uniform-four-differentiators-weight}.
Since \(\Omega^{(N)}_{X,Y}(k,p)\) is homogeneous of
\(L_0\)-degree \(k+p\) and annihilates
\(\mathcal W^\epsilon_\lambda(M)\), its action on the summands of
weights \(\lambda+n\), \(n\in\mathbb Z\), gives
\begin{equation*}
 \Omega^{(N)}_{X,Y}(k,p)v
 \in\bigcap_{n\in\mathbb Z}(L_0-\lambda-n-k-p)M,
 \qquad v\in M.
\end{equation*}
Since \(M\) is free over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\), this intersection is zero.
<!-- end proof-7 -->
<!-- theorem-2: body_md -->
\label{thm:uniform-cover-finite}
Let \(M\) be a finite Cartan-free
\(\mathfrak g^\epsilon\)-module such that
\(\mathfrak g^\epsilon M=M\).  Then
\(\widehat M_\epsilon\) is finitely generated over
\(\mathbb C[L_0]\).
<!-- end theorem-2 -->
<!-- proof-8: body_md -->
Choose \(N\geq2\) as in \Cref{lem:uniform-differentiator-lift}, and
fix \(X,Y\in\{L,G\}\), indices \(k,p\), and \(v\in M\).
The tensor
\[
 w=\sum_{i=0}^{N}(-1)^i\binom Ni
       X_{k-i}\otimes Y_{p+i}v
\]
belongs to \(K_\epsilon(M)\).  Indeed, by
\eqref{eq:uniform-A-on-g}, for every \(s\in\mathbb Z\),
\begin{align*}
 \sum_{i=0}^{N}(-1)^i\binom Ni
 (t^sX_{k-i})Y_{p+i}v
 &=\Omega^{(N)}_{X,Y}(k+s,p)v=0,\\
 \sum_{i=0}^{N}(-1)^i\binom Ni
 (t^s\xi X_{k-i})Y_{p+i}v
 &=\begin{cases}
  \frac12\Omega^{(N)}_{G,Y}(k+s+\epsilon,p)v=0,&X=L,\\
  0,&X=G.
 \end{cases}
\end{align*}
Hence
\begin{equation}
 \sum_{i=0}^{N}(-1)^i\binom Ni
 X_{k-i}\boxtimes Y_{p+i}v=0.
 \label{eq:uniform-cover-difference}
\end{equation}

Since \(M=\mathfrak g^\epsilon M\), the cover is spanned by
\(X_k\boxtimes Y_pv\).  For \(X=L\),
\eqref{eq:uniform-cover-difference} gives
\[
 L_k\boxtimes Y_pv=
 \begin{cases}
  \displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}(-1)^{i+1}\binom Ni
  L_{k-i}\boxtimes Y_{p+i}v,&k\geq N,\\[6pt]
  \displaystyle\sum_{i=1}^{N}(-1)^{i+1}\binom Ni
  L_{k+i}\boxtimes Y_{p-i}v,&k<0.
 \end{cases}
\]
Hence, by induction,
\[
 L_k\boxtimes Y_pv
 \in\sum_{q=0}^{N-1}L_q\boxtimes M,
 \qquad k\in\mathbb Z.
\]
The same argument for \(X=G\) leaves only
\(\epsilon,\ldots,\epsilon+N-1\).  Hence
\begin{equation}
 \widehat M_\epsilon
   =\sum_{q=0}^{N-1}
       \bigl(L_q\boxtimes M+G_{q+\epsilon}\boxtimes M\bigr).
 \label{eq:uniform-cover-bounded-index}
\end{equation}
Choose a homogeneous \(\mathbb C[L_0]\)-basis
\(e_1,\ldots,e_d\) of \(M\).  The diagonal action gives
\begin{equation*}
 X_k\boxtimes f(L_0)v
  =f(L_0-k)(X_k\boxtimes v),
 \qquad f\in\mathbb C[L_0].
\end{equation*}
\begin{samepage} 
Together with \eqref{eq:uniform-cover-bounded-index}, this shows that
\[
 \left\{
  L_q\boxtimes e_j,\ G_{q+\epsilon}\boxtimes e_j:
  0\leq q<N,\ 1\leq j\leq d
 \right\}
\]
generates \(\widehat M_\epsilon\) over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\).
\end{samepage}
<!-- end proof-8 -->
<!-- proposition-3: body_md -->
\label{prop:uniform-minimal-cover}
Let \(M\) be a nontrivial simple finite Cartan-free
\(\mathfrak g^\epsilon\)-module.  There exist a simple admissible
\(\mathcal A\mathfrak g^\epsilon\)-module \(E\) free of finite rank
over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\) and a surjective
\(\mathfrak g^\epsilon\)-homomorphism \(E\twoheadrightarrow M\).
<!-- end proposition-3 -->
<!-- proof-9: body_md -->
By \Cref{thm:uniform-cover-finite,lem:weyl-finite-length}, the
cover \(\widehat M_\epsilon\) has a finite composition series of
admissible modules
\[
 0=E_0\subset E_1\subset\cdots\subset E_\ell=\widehat M_\epsilon.
\]
Let \(i\) be the least index such that \(\pi_M(E_i)\neq0\).
Then \(E=E_i/E_{i-1}\) is simple and admissible, and \(\pi_M\) induces
the required epimorphism.  The module \(E\) is finite free over
\(\mathbb C[L_0]\) by \Cref{lem:weyl-finite-length}.
<!-- end proof-9 -->
<!-- lemma-5: body_md -->
\label{lem:sector-finite-tensor-factorization}
Let \(\mathcal R\) be a unital associative superalgebra, and \(E\) a simple
graded \(\mathcal K\otimes\mathcal R\)-module finitely generated over
\(\mathbb C[t\partial_t]\).
Then
\[
 E\simeq\Pi^\delta S(P)\otimes V,\qquad\delta\in\mathbb Z_2,
\]
where \(P\) is a simple \(\mathcal D\)-module finite free
over \(\mathbb C[t\partial_t]\) and \(V\) is a finite-dimensional simple graded
\(\mathcal R\)-module.
<!-- end lemma-5 -->
<!-- proof-10: body_md -->
By \Cref{lem:weyl-finite-length}, \(E\) is free over
\(\mathbb C[t\partial_t]\) and has finite length as a
\(\mathcal D\)-module, hence also as a graded \(\mathcal K\)-module.
Choose a simple graded \(\mathcal K\)-submodule \(S\subseteq E\).
Then \(S\simeq\Pi^\delta S(P)\)
for some simple \(\mathcal D\)-module \(P\) and \(\delta\in\mathbb Z_2\).
Since \(P\) is a direct summand of \(S\) over \(\mathbb C[t\partial_t]\),
it is free of finite rank.  Moreover, \(S\) is strictly simple and
\(\mathcal K\) has a countable basis, so \(E\simeq S\otimes V\)
for some simple graded \(\mathcal R\)-module \(V\) by
\cite[Lemma~2.2(4)]{XueLu2021}.
Since \(E\) is finitely generated over
\(\mathbb C[t\partial_t]\), \(V\) is finite dimensional.

<!-- proposition-5: body_md -->
\label{prop:NS-simple-admissible}
Let \(E\) be a simple admissible \(\mathcal A\mathfrak g^{1/2}\)-module
finitely generated over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\).  Then
\[
 E\simeq\Pi^\delta H_{1/2,b}(P),\qquad
 b\in\mathbb C,\quad\delta\in\mathbb Z_2,
\]
where \(P\) is a simple \(\mathcal D\)-module finite free over \(\mathbb C[D]\).
<!-- end proposition-5 -->
<!-- proof-12: body_md -->
By \Cref{lem:weyl-finite-length}, \(E\) is finite free over
\(\mathbb C[L_0]\).  Set \(\mathscr C=\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal D}(E)\),
where \(D\) acts as \(L_0\).  If \(T\in\mathscr C\) satisfies
\(TE\subseteq L_0E\), then \(Tt=tT\) gives the inclusions
\[
 TE=T(t^nE)=t^nTE\subseteq t^nL_0E=(L_0-n)E,
 \qquad n\in\mathbb Z,
\]
which imply \(T=0\).
Thus the action of \(\mathscr C\) on \(E/L_0E\) is faithful,
and \(\mathscr C\) is finite dimensional.

The operators \(t^{-n}L_n-L_0\) belong to \(\mathscr C\), and
\[
 [t^{-m}L_m,t^{-n}L_n]
 =(n-m)t^{-m-n}L_{m+n}+mt^{-m}L_m-nt^{-n}L_n.
\]
The same relation holds after replacing each \(t^{-n}L_n\) by
\(t^{-n}L_n-L_0\).  Applying
\cite[Theorem~15]{BilligFutornyIoharaKashuba2020}, in the purely even
one-variable case, to their induced action on \(E/L_0E\), and
using faithfulness, we obtain polynomiality of \(t^{-n}L_n\) in \(n\).
By interpolation, each \(t^{-n}L_n\), \(n\in\mathbb Z\), is a linear
combination of operators \(t^{-j}L_j\) with \(j\geq0\).
Thus every submodule for \(\mathcal A\) and the
positive contact subalgebra, generated by
\(L_m\), \(m\geq-1\), and \(G_p\), \(p\geq-1/2\), is
invariant under all \(L_n\).  Since
\[
 [L_m,G_{1/2}]=\frac{1-m}{2}G_{m+1/2},\qquad m\leq-2,
\]
it is also invariant under all \(G_p\).
Hence \(E\) remains simple upon restriction.
By \cite[Lemmas~3.3--3.4]{CaiLu2022}, this restriction is a simple
\(\mathcal K\otimes U(\mathcal T)\)-module, where \(\mathcal T\) is
isomorphic to the nonnegative part of the Neveu--Schwarz algebra.
Under the isomorphism of \cite[Lemma~3.3]{CaiLu2022},
\[
 \partial_t=L_{-1},\qquad
 \partial_\xi=\xi L_{-1}-G_{-1/2},\qquad
 L_0=t\partial_t+\frac12\xi\partial_\xi+L'_0.
\]
The operator \(L'_0\) belongs to \(\mathscr C\) and is algebraic.
Since \(t\partial_t\), \((1/2)\xi\partial_\xi\), and \(L'_0\)
commute and the last two are algebraic, \(E\) is
finitely generated over \(\mathbb C[t\partial_t]\).

\Cref{lem:sector-finite-tensor-factorization} now gives
\[
 E\simeq\Pi^\delta S(P')\otimes V,
\]
where \(P'\) is a simple \(\mathcal D\)-module finite free over
\(\mathbb C[t\partial_t]\), and \(V\) is a finite-dimensional simple
graded \(\mathcal T\)-module.  Since \(V\) is generated by a
homogeneous \(L'_0\)-eigenvector and \(\operatorname{ad}L'_0\)
is diagonalizable on \(\mathcal T\), \(V\) is a weight module.  Thus
\cite[Lemma~3.7]{CaiLu2022} implies that \(V\) is one dimensional
and that all positive-degree elements of \(\mathcal T\) act
trivially.  Let \(b\) be the scalar by which \(L'_0\) acts, and
absorb the parity of \(V\) into \(\delta\).
Retaining the \(t\)-action on \(P'\) and setting
\(D=t\partial_t+2b\) defines a simple \(\mathcal D\)-module \(P\), still
finite free over \(\mathbb C[D]\).  The expressions for \(L_m\) and
\(G_p\) in \cite[(3.1)--(3.2)]{CaiLu2022} yield
\eqref{eq:unified-H-operator-L}--\eqref{eq:unified-H-operator-G}
with \(\epsilon=1/2\), initially for \(m\geq-1\) and
\(p\geq-1/2\).  Polynomiality of \(t^{-m}L_m\) extends
\eqref{eq:unified-H-operator-L} to all \(m\in\mathbb Z\).
The commutator with \(G_{1/2}\) then extends
\eqref{eq:unified-H-operator-G} to all \(p\in\mathbb Z+1/2\),
proving the assertion.

Let \(M\) be a nontrivial simple finite Cartan-free module over
\(\mathfrak g^\epsilon\) or \(\widehat{\mathfrak g}^{\,\epsilon}\).
In the latter case, \Cref{prop:uniform-central-charge} gives
\(C=0\), so \(M\) is a \(\mathfrak g^\epsilon\)-module.
By \Cref{prop:uniform-minimal-cover}, there is a simple admissible
module \(E\) finite free over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\) with an epimorphism
\(E\twoheadrightarrow M\).  Applying
\Cref{prop:Ramond-simple-admissible,prop:NS-simple-admissible} in the
respective cases gives
\begin{equation}
 \Pi^\delta H_{\epsilon,b}(P)\twoheadrightarrow M,
 \label{eq:H-onto-M-unified}
\end{equation}
where \(P\) is simple and finite free over \(\mathbb C[D]\).
By \Cref{prop:unified-H-simple}\textup{(ii)},
\(H_{\epsilon,1/2}(P)\) has no nontrivial simple quotient, so

makes the source of
\eqref{eq:H-onto-M-unified} simple, so the epimorphism is an
isomorphism.  Conversely, every listed module is finite Cartan-free

Parameter uniqueness follows from \Cref{cor:unified-H-uniqueness}.

<!-- proof-14: body_md -->
Let \(T\) be the \(\mathbb C[L_0]\)-torsion of \(M\).  For
\(X\in\{L,G\}\) and \(s\) in the corresponding index set, the
relation \([L_0,X_s]=sX_s\) gives
\[
 f(L_0-s)X_sv=X_sf(L_0)v.
\]
Thus \(T\) is a graded submodule, and simplicity gives \(T=0\)
or \(T=M\).  If \(T=0\), the structure theorem over
\(\mathbb C[L_0]\) makes both parity components finite free, and
\Cref{thm:unified-main} applies.  If \(T=M\), finite generation
makes \(M\) finite dimensional.

distinct such \(m\) in
\[
 0=[L_m,L_{-m}]=-2mL_0+\frac{m^3-m}{12}C
\]

\[
 X_s=\frac{1}{s}[L_0,X_s]=0,\qquad s\ne0.
\]
In the Ramond case,
\[
 0=[L_1,G_{-1}]=-\frac{3}{2}G_0,
\]
so \(G_0=0\).  Hence the whole algebra acts trivially, and
simplicity leaves the one-dimensional trivial module or its
parity change.

\label{prop:finite-generated-finite-length}

<!-- proposition-7: body_md -->
\label{prop:H-fully-faithful}
For fixed \(\epsilon\in\{0,1/2\}\) and \(b\in\mathbb C\), the
functor \(H_{\epsilon,b}\) is exact and fully faithful.
More precisely, for any \(\mathcal D\)-modules \(P,Q\),
\[
 \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(P,Q)
 \simeq\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathfrak g^\epsilon}
 \bigl(H_{\epsilon,b}(P),H_{\epsilon,b}(Q)\bigr),
\]
via \(f\mapsto f\oplus\overline f\).
A short exact sequence of \(\mathcal D\)-modules splits if and only if
its image under \(H_{\epsilon,b}\) splits.
<!-- end proposition-7 -->
<!-- proof-16: body_md -->
Write an even homomorphism as \(f\oplus\overline g\).
Compatibility with \(G_{-\epsilon}\) on the odd component
gives \(f=g\), and compatibility with \(G_{1-\epsilon}\)
gives \(ft=tf\).  Compatibility with \(L_0\) on the even
component then gives \(fD=Df\).  Conversely, every
\(\mathcal D\)-homomorphism induces an even homomorphism by
\eqref{eq:H-even-components}--\eqref{eq:H-odd-components}.
Exactness follows from \(H_{\epsilon,b}(P)=P\oplus\overline P\),
and the assertion about splitting follows from full faithfulness.

<!-- proposition-8: body_md -->
\label{prop:H-self-extension}
Let \(P\) be a simple \(\mathcal D\)-module free of finite rank \(r\)
over \(\mathbb C[D]\), and put \(M=H_{\epsilon,b}(P)\), where
\(b\ne1/2\).  Let \(\eta\) be even with \(\eta^2=0\).
Replacing \(b\) by \(b+\eta\) in
\eqref{eq:H-even-components}--\eqref{eq:H-odd-components}
defines an action on
\(E=(P\oplus\overline P)\otimes_{\mathbb C}\mathbb C[\eta]/(\eta^2)\)
and a nonsplit exact sequence
\begin{equation}
 0\longrightarrow M\longrightarrow E\longrightarrow M
 \longrightarrow0.
 \label{eq:parameter-self-extension}
\end{equation}
The module \(E\) is indecomposable of length two, and each
parity component is free of rank \(2r\) over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\).
<!-- end proposition-8 -->
<!-- proof-17: body_md -->
The defining relations are polynomial identities in \(b\),
so they remain valid after this substitution.  Multiplication
by \(\eta\) and reduction modulo \(\eta\) give
\eqref{eq:parameter-self-extension}.
Suppose that the projection has an even section, written
\[
 v\longmapsto v+\eta a(v),\qquad
 \overline v\longmapsto\overline v+\eta\overline{c(v)},
 \qquad a,c\in\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(P).
\]
Compatibility with \(G_p\) on the odd component gives
\(at^{p+\epsilon}=t^{p+\epsilon}c\).
Taking \(p=-\epsilon\) and \(p=1-\epsilon\) yields
\(a=c\) and \(at=ta\).  Compatibility with \(L_m\) on the
even component therefore gives
\[
 [a,D]=(m-2\epsilon)\operatorname{Id}_P,\qquad m\in\mathbb Z,
\]
which is impossible for \(m=0,1\).  Thus the sequence does
not split.  By \Cref{prop:unified-H-simple}\textup{(i)},
its two factors are simple, so \(E\) is indecomposable of
length two.  The sequence splits over \(\mathbb C[L_0]\) in each
parity because its quotient is free, and
\Cref{rem:H-Cartan-rank} gives the asserted ranks.
