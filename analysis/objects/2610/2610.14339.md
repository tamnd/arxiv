<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:exactness}
Let $Q$ and $G$ be convex and continuously differentiable and $H$ affine in $y$ and define $\mathbb X_{\mathrm D}:=\{x\in\mathbb X:\ d(x,u,v)=\varphi(x)\ \text{for some } u\geq0,\ v\}$.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*), leftmargin=*]
\item The feasible sets of MPCC, WDP, MDP, eMDP, and LDR have the same projection onto $(x,y)$, namely
\begin{equation}\label{eq:projection}
    \{(x,y):\ x\in\mathbb X_{\mathrm D},\ y\in\mathbb S_{\mathrm{rel}}(x)\}.
\end{equation}
In particular, every feasible point of any of the five formulations is feasible for \eqref{eq:bilevel_rel}, and the projection equals the feasible set of \eqref{eq:bilevel_rel} if and only if $\mathbb X_{\mathrm D}=\mathbb X$.
\item Every feasible point of an $\varepsilon$-relaxed WDP, MDP, eMDP, or LDR satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:three_term}
    \underbrace{Q(y)-\varphi(x)}_{\text{follower subopt.}}
    +\underbrace{\varphi(x)-d^*(x)}_{\text{duality gap}}
    +\underbrace{d^*(x)-d(x,u,v)}_{\text{multiplier subopt.}}
    \leq\varepsilon,
\end{equation}
and each of the three terms is nonnegative.
\item Let $x\in\mathbb X$, $y\in\mathbb S_{\mathrm{rel}}(x)$, and $\tau(x):=\varphi(x)-d^*(x)$. Then $(x,y)$ lifts to a feasible point of the $\varepsilon$-relaxed LDR for every $\varepsilon>\tau(x)$ and for no $\varepsilon<\tau(x)$; at $\varepsilon=\tau(x)$ a lift exists if and only if the supremum defining $d^*(x)$ is attained. The same holds for the $\varepsilon$-relaxed WDP whenever the infimum defining $d(x,u,v)$ is attained at the lifting multipliers, for instance when $Q$ is strongly convex. If $x\in\mathbb X_{\mathrm D}$, then $(x,y)$ lifts to every relaxed formulation for every $\varepsilon\geq0$.
\end{enumerate}

<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
(i) Let a point be feasible for one of the five formulations. Feasibility of $y$ and weak duality give $Q(y)\geq\varphi(x)\geq d^*(x)\geq d(x,u,v)$. Next, we show $Q(y)\leq d(x,u,v)$. For LDR this is trivial from Eq. \eqref{eq:ldr_certificate}. For WDP, MDP, and eMDP, stationarity \eqref{eq:dual_stationarity} and convexity of $\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,\cdot,u,v)$ give $d(x,u,v)=\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,z,u,v)$, and Eq. \eqref{eq:wdp_certificate} then implies $Q(y)\leq d(x,u,v)$; the MDP conditions sum to Eq. \eqref{eq:wdp_certificate}, and the eMDP conditions sum to the MDP conditions. For MPCC, complementarity and $H(x,y)=0$ give $\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,y,u,v)=Q(y)$, and stationarity at $y$ gives $d(x,u,v)=\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,y,u,v)$. Hence, the claim holds and we obtain $y\in\mathbb S_{\mathrm{rel}}(x)$ and $x\in\mathbb X_{\mathrm D}$. Conversely, let $x\in\mathbb X_{\mathrm D}$, $y\in\mathbb S_{\mathrm{rel}}(x)$, and $(u,v)$ with $d(x,u,v)=\varphi(x)=Q(y)$. Then
\[
    d(x,u,v)\leq\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,y,u,v)=Q(y)+u^{\mathsf T}G(y)\leq Q(y)=d(x,u,v),
\]
so $u^{\mathsf T}G(y)=0$, which gives $u_\ell G_\ell(y)=0$ for every $\ell$ because each product is nonpositive, and $y$ minimizes $\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,\cdot,u,v)$, which gives $\nabla_y\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,y,u,v)=0$. Thus $(x,y,u,v)$ is MPCC feasible, and $z=y$ makes it feasible for WDP, MDP, eMDP, and LDR. The last claim follows because $\mathbb S_{\mathrm{rel}}(x)$ is nonempty for every $x\in\mathbb X$.
 
(ii) The argument of (i) applied to the relaxed conditions gives $Q(y)-d(x,u,v)\leq\varepsilon$. The three terms in Eq. \eqref{eq:three_term} are nonnegative by feasibility of $y$ and weak duality, and they telescope to $Q(y)-d(x,u,v)$.
 
(iii) Because $Q(y)=\varphi(x)$, a lift to the relaxed LDR exists if and only if $d(x,u,v)\geq\varphi(x)-\varepsilon=d^*(x)+\tau(x)-\varepsilon$ for some $(u,v)$. If $\varepsilon>\tau(x)$, the definition of the supremum provides such $(u,v)$. If $\varepsilon<\tau(x)$, no $(u,v)$ qualifies because $d(x,u,v)\leq d^*(x)$. If $\varepsilon=\tau(x)$, the requirement is $d(x,u,v)=d^*(x)$. For WDP, let $z$ attain the infimum defining $d(x,u,v)$ at the lifting multipliers. Then $z$ satisfies Eq. \eqref{eq:dual_stationarity} by convexity, and $Q(y)-\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,z,u,v)=Q(y)-d(x,u,v)\leq\varepsilon$ is the relaxed WDP inequality. Strong convexity of $Q$ makes $\boldsymbol{\mathit{L}}(x,\cdot,u,v)$ strongly convex, so its infimum is attained. Finally, if $x\in\mathbb X_{\mathrm D}$, the exact lift constructed in (i) is feasible for every relaxed formulation at every $\varepsilon\geq0$.
