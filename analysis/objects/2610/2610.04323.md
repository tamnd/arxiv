<!-- lemma-9: body_md -->
\label{lem:persistence}
 Let~$\gamma = 0$ and~$\lambda_1 > \lambda_c$.
 Then, for all~$\epsilon > 0$, there exists an event~$G_{m, n}^2$ measurable with respect to the graphical representation in~$\mathscr{B}_{m, n}$ such that
 $$ E_{m, n} \cap F_{m, n} \cap G_{m, n}^2 \subset E_{m, n}' \quad \hbox{and} \quad P (G_{m, n}^2) \geq 1 - \epsilon / 4 \ \hbox{for all~$L$ large}. $$
<!-- end lemma-9 -->
<!-- proof-9: body_md -->
The key ingredient is the construction due to Durrett and Neuhauser~\cite{durrett_neuhauser_1997}, which gives the result for the supercritical contact process.
 More precisely, let~$\zeta_t$ be the standard contact process with parameter~$\lambda_1 > \lambda_c$ and configuration
 $$ \zeta_{2nL^2} (x) = \mathbf{1} \{\xi_{2nL^2} (x, 1) \} \ \ \hbox{for all} \ \ x \in \mathbb{Z}^d \quad \hbox{at time} \ 2nL^2, $$
 so the process has at least one~1 in each small cube in the flat region~$\mathscr{A}_{m, n}$.
 Then, it follows from the proof of~\cite[Proposition~3.1]{durrett_neuhauser_1997} that each small cube in~$\mathscr{A}_{m, n}'$ is also occupied by at least one~1 with probability arbitrarily close to one when~$L$ is sufficiently large.
 Their proof relies on the existence of dual paths starting from~$\mathscr{A}_{m, n}'$ and landing on occupied sites in~$\mathscr{A}_{m, n}$ that they can redirect using a so-called repositioning algorithm.
 In addition, \cite[Lemma~3.7]{durrett_neuhauser_1997} shows that the position of these dual paths is controlled by large deviation estimates giving probabilities that are exponentially close to one, whereas the number of dual paths starting from~$\mathscr{A}_{m, n}'$ is only polynomial.
 In particular, with probability arbitrarily close to one when~$L$ is large, all the dual paths constructed from the repositioning algorithm are contained in~$\mathscr{B}_{m, n}$.
 This implies the existence of an event~$G_{m, n}^2$ measurable with respect to the graphical representation in~$\mathscr{B}_{m, n}$ such that
 $$ E_{m, n} \cap G_{m, n}^2 \subset E_{m, n}' \quad \hbox{and} \quad P (G_{m, n}^2) \geq 1 - \epsilon / 4 \ \hbox{for all~$L$ large} $$
 for the contact process~$\zeta_t$.
 To deduce the lemma, recall that, in the absence of~2s initially, and when~$\gamma = 0$, which prevents the creation of~2s later on, we have
 $$ \xi_t = \textsc{irpa} (\lambda_1, \lambda_2, \phi_1, \phi_2, \gamma, N) = \textsc{irp} (\lambda_1, \phi_1, N) \geq \textsc{cp} (\lambda_1) = \zeta_t. $$
 Since in addition the event~$F_{m, n}$ and the assumption~$\gamma = 0$ force the absence of~2s in the block~$\mathscr{B}_{m, n}$, and the event~$G_{m, n}^2$ is measurable with respect to the graphical representation in this block~(the dual paths of interest are contained in this block), we deduce that
 $$ E_{m, n} \cap F_{m, n} \cap G_{m, n}^2 \subset E_{m, n}' \quad \hbox{when} \quad \gamma = 0 $$
 for the epidemic model~$\xi_t$.
 This completes the proof.
