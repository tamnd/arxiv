<!-- definition-1: body_md -->
\label{Kripkeval}
 A pair $ \langle W,\vartheta\rangle $ is said to be an \textbf{S5}-model iff $ W\not=\emptyset $ and $ \vartheta $ is a mapping from $ W\times\mathscr{L}_{\Box\Diamond} $ to $\{T,F\}$ such that it preserves classical conditions for truth-value connectives and for each $A\in\mathscr{L}_{\Box\Diamond}$ and $x\in W$ we have:
 {\small \begin{enumerate}[$ - $]\itemsep=0pt
 	\item $ \vartheta(\Box A,x)=T $ iff $ \forall_{y\in W}\vartheta(A,y)=T $,
 	\item $ \vartheta(\Diamond A,x)=T $ iff $ \exists_{y\in W}\vartheta(A,y)=T $.
 \end{enumerate}}
<!-- end definition-1 -->
<!-- definition-2: body_md -->
A formula $ A $ is \textit{true in a world} $w \in W$ iff $ \vartheta(A,w)=T $. A (multi)set of formulas $ \Delta $ \textit{follows} from a (multi)set of formulas $ \Gamma $ in \textbf{S5} ($ \Gamma\models_{\bf S5}\Delta $) iff for every \textbf{S5}-model $ \langle W,\vartheta\rangle $ and every $w \in W$, if every $ B\in\Gamma $ is  true in $w$, then some $ C\in\Delta $ is true in $w$. 
  A formula is \textbf{S5}-\textit{valid} iff it follows from the empty (multi)set of formulas.
<!-- end definition-2 -->
<!-- definition-3: body_md -->
Let $ \langle W,\vartheta\rangle $ be an \textbf{S5}-model. 
 The truth conditions for the two-valued versions of the essence and accident modalities are as follows:
 {\small \begin{enumerate}[$ - $]\itemsep=0pt
 	\item $ \vartheta({\circ} A,x)=T $ iff $\vartheta(A,x)=F$ or $ \forall_{y\in W}\vartheta(A,y)=T$.
 	\item $ \vartheta({\bullet}A,x)=T $ iff $\vartheta(A,x)=T$ and $ \exists_{y\in W}\vartheta(A,y)=F$.
 	\item $ \vartheta(\widetilde{\circ} A,x)=T $ iff $\vartheta(A,x)=T$ or $ \forall_{y\in W}\vartheta(A,y)=F$,
 	\item $ \vartheta(\widetilde{\bullet} A,x)=T $ iff $\vartheta(A,x)=F$ and $ \exists_{y\in W}\vartheta(A,y)=T$.
 \end{enumerate}}
<!-- end definition-3 -->
<!-- definition-4: body_md -->
\label{TruthVal}
 Using the set $ \{T,F\} $ of truth values of classical logic, we can define the set $ \{t,b,n,f\} $ of truth values of \textbf{FDE} and its extensions as follows:  $ \{ \{T\}, \{T,F\}, \emptyset, \{F\} \} $, where $ t $ stands for $ \{T\} $, $ b $ stands for $ \{T,F\} $, $ n $ for $ \emptyset $, and $ f $ for $ \{F\} $. We introduce the following convention: for a formula $ A $ and a valuation $ \vartheta $, we write $ \vartheta(A)\in\{t,b\} $ iff $ T\in \vartheta(A)$ and $ \vartheta(A)\in\{f,b\} $ iff $ F\in \vartheta(A)$.
<!-- end definition-4 -->
<!-- definition-5: body_md -->
\label{KripkeNelsonval}\label{Four-valuedS5}
 A pair $ \langle W,\vartheta\rangle $ is said to be an $ \bf FDE_{\Box\Diamond}^{\bf S5}$-model iff $ W\not=\emptyset $  and $ \vartheta $ is a mapping from $ W\times\mathscr{L}_{\Box\Diamond}^\neg $ to $\{t,b,n,f\}$ such that for
 any $A\in\mathscr{L}_{\Box\Diamond}^\neg$ and $x\in W$ we have:
 {\small \begin{enumerate}[$ - $]\itemsep=0pt
 \begin{multicols}{2}
 \item $ T\in\vartheta(\neg A,x) $ iff $ F\in\vartheta(A,x) $,
 \item $ T\in\vartheta({\sim} A,x) $ iff $ T\not\in\vartheta(A,x) $,
 	\item $ T\in\vartheta(\Box A,x) $ iff $ \forall_{y\in W}T\in\vartheta(A,y) $,	
 	\item $ T\in\vartheta(\Diamond A,x) $ iff $ \exists_{y\in W}T\in\vartheta(A,y) $,
 \item $ F\in\vartheta(\neg A,x) $ iff $ T\in\vartheta(A,x) $,
 \item $ F\in\vartheta({\sim} A,x) $ iff $ F\not\in\vartheta(A,x) $,
 \item  $ F\in\vartheta(\Box A,x) $ iff $ \exists_{y\in W}F\in\vartheta(A,y) $,	
 \item  $ F\in\vartheta(\Diamond A,x) $ iff $ \forall_{y\in W}F\in\vartheta(A,y) $.
 \end{multicols} \vspace{-.15in}
 \item $ T\in\vartheta(A\wedge B,x) $ iff $ T\in\vartheta(A,x) $ and $ T\in\vartheta(B,x) $,

<!-- definition-9: body_md -->
A sequent is an ordered pair written as $\Gamma \Rightarrow \Delta$, where $\Gamma$ and $\Delta$ are finite multisets of formulas. A hypersequent is a finite multiset of sequents, written as $\Gamma_1 \Rightarrow \Delta_1 \mid \ldots \mid \Gamma_n \Rightarrow \Delta_n$. A sequent $\Gamma \Rightarrow \Delta$ is valid in a logic $\mathbf{L}$ iff $\Gamma \models_{\mathbf{L}} \Delta$. A hypersequent is valid iff at least one of its component sequents is valid. For a more precise definition via a translation of hypersequents into formulas, see \cite[p.~211]{IndrzejczakSC}.

<!-- remark-1: body_md -->
\label{RestallRemark}
A few comments are in order:   
\begin{enumerate}[$(1)$]\itemsep=0pt
\item Following \cite{MMLS5}, we extend Restall's original formulation \cite{Restall} by adding rules for  $\vee$,  $\rightarrow$, and $\Diamond $. The rules for $ \wedge $ and $ \vee $ are given in multiplicative form in order to shorten the proofs of the embedding theorems. 
\item The axiom (Ax) $ p\Rightarrow p$, for each $p\in P$, can be generalised for the case of arbitrary formulas by induction:  $ A\Rightarrow A$. Such a generalised version is used in \cite{Restall}.
\item The original formulation in \cite{Restall} uses the following rules of external weakening:
\begin{center}
{\small (EW$\Rightarrow$) $ \dfrac{H}{A\Rightarrow\;\mid H} $\qquad				
($\Rightarrow$EW) $ \dfrac{H}{\Rightarrow A\mid H} $\qquad}
\end{center}
However, as shown in \cite{PetrukhinS5}, in order to obtain a constructive cut-elimination proof for Restall's calculus one has to use a more general version of external weakening, which allows us to add not only sequents of the form $ A\Rightarrow $ or $ \Rightarrow A$, but arbitrary hypersequents, including the empty one. Although we do not deal with constructive cut elimination in this paper, we adopt this more general formulation of external weakening.
    \item The internal weakening and external contraction rules are admissible:  
\begin{center}
{\small 	{\small (IW$\Rightarrow$)} $ \dfrac{\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta\mid H}{A,\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta\mid H} $\qquad				
	{\small ($\Rightarrow$IW)} $ \dfrac{\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta\mid H}{\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta, A\mid H} $\qquad
	{\small (EC)} $ \dfrac{\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta\mid\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta\mid H}{\Gamma\Rightarrow\Delta\mid H} $\qquad	}									\end{center}

\item As pointed out by a reviewer, the rule (Merge) is not invertible, since the hypersequent $p \Rightarrow \mid \Rightarrow p$ is not valid, whereas $p \Rightarrow p$ is valid. However, this does not affect the cut-elimination proof for this calculus given in \cite{Restall} (see also \cite{IndrzejczakSC}). In what follows, we prove cut elimination for $\mathbf{FDE}^{\bf S5}_{\Box\Diamond}$ and $\mathbf{FDE}^{\bf S5}_{\circ\bullet}$ using embedding procedures that do not require the rules to be invertible.
\item The notion of a proof in this hypersequent calculus 
is defined in the standard way. 
\item A modification of Restall's hypersequent calculus for \textbf{S5} that formalises the two-valued essence and accident modalities as primitive connectives is presented in \cite{PetrukhinS5}.
\end{enumerate}

<!-- proof-9: body_md -->
Assume that $ \mathfrak{M}_1=\langle W,\vartheta\rangle $ is $ {\bf FDE^{\bf S5}_{\Box\Diamond}} $-model and $ \mathfrak{M}_2=\langle W,\vartheta^\prime\rangle $ is $ {\bf S5} $-model, where $ \vartheta^\prime $ is a valuation such that for all $ p \in \mathcal{P}$ and $ x\in W$:	
\begin{enumerate}[$\blacktriangleright$]\itemsep=0pt
\item $ T\in\vartheta(p,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $ \vartheta^\prime(p,x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $,
\item $ T\in\vartheta(\neg p,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $ \vartheta^\prime(p^\prime,x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $.
	\end{enumerate}
	
The proof is by induction on the construction of the $\mathscr{L}_{\Box\Diamond}^\neg$-formula $ A $.
	
	\begin{enumerate}[$ (1) $]\itemsep=0pt
\item $ A $ is $ p $ or $ \neg p $. Follows from the assumption and Definition \ref{f}.
		
\item $ A $ is $ B\rightarrowC $. By Definition \ref{KripkeNelsonval}, $ T\in\vartheta(B\rightarrowC,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff $ T\in\vartheta(B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ implies $ T\in\vartheta(C,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff (by the induction hypothesis) $ \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(B),x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$ implies $ \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(C),x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$ iff $ \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(B)\rightarrow \mathfrak{f}(C),x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$ iff (Definition \ref{f}) $ \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(B\rightarrowC),x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$. 

\item $ A $ is $ \neg(B\rightarrowC) $. 
 By Definition \ref{KripkeNelsonval}, $ T\in\vartheta(\neg(B\rightarrowC),x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff $ F\in\vartheta(B\rightarrowC,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff $ T\in\vartheta(B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ and $ F\in\vartheta(C,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff $ T\in\vartheta(B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ and $ T\in\vartheta(\neg C,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff (by the induction hypothesis) $ \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(B),x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$ and $ \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(\neg C),x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$ iff $ \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(B)\wedge \mathfrak{f}(\neg C),x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$ iff (Definition \ref{f})  $ \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(\neg(B\rightarrow C)),x)=T $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$.

\item $ A $ is $ \neg\Box B$. By Definition \ref{KripkeNelsonval}, $ T\in\vartheta(\neg\Box B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff $ F\in\vartheta(\BoxB,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff  
$\exists_{y\in W} F\in\vartheta(B,y) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ iff  
$\exists_{y\in W} T\in\vartheta(\negB,y) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1$ 
iff (by the induction hypothesis) $\exists_{y\in W}\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(\negB),y)=T$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$ iff (by Definition \ref{Kripkeval}) $\vartheta^\prime(\Diamond \mathfrak{f}(\negB),x)=T$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2$ iff (by Definition \ref{f}) $\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{f}(\neg\BoxB),x)=T$ in~$ \mathfrak{M}_2$.
	\end{enumerate}

<!-- lemma-6: body_md -->
\label{Sem4to4L1}
For any $ {\bf FDE^{\bf S5}_{\circ\bullet}} $-model $ \mathfrak{M}_1=\langle W,\vartheta\rangle $ we can construct an $ {\bf FDE^{\bf S5}_{\Box\Diamond}} $-model $ \mathfrak{M}_2=\langle W,\vartheta^\prime\rangle $ such that for each $\mathscr{L}_{\circ\bullet}^\neg$-formula $ A $ and any $ x\in W$, it is true that

\item

\end{enumerate}
<!-- end lemma-6 -->
<!-- proof-16: body_md -->
Assume that $ \mathfrak{M}_1=\langle W,\vartheta\rangle $ is $ {\bf FDE^{\bf S5}_{\circ\bullet}} $-model and $ \mathfrak{M}_2=\langle W,\vartheta^\prime\rangle $ is $ {\bf FDE^{\bf S5}_{\Box\Diamond}} $-model, where $ \vartheta^\prime $ is a valuation such that for all $ p \in \mathcal{P}$ and $ x\in W$:	
\begin{enumerate}[$\blacktriangleright$]\itemsep=0pt
\item $ T\in\vartheta(p,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $ T\in\vartheta^\prime(p,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $,
\item $ T\in\vartheta(\neg p,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $ T\in\vartheta^\prime(\neg p,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $.
	\end{enumerate}
	
The proof is by induction on the construction of the $\mathscr{L}_{\circ\bullet}^\neg$-formula $ A $.
	
	\begin{enumerate}[$ (1) $]\itemsep=0pt
\item  $ A $ is $ p $ or $ \neg p $. Follows from the assumption and Definition \ref{g}.	

\item $ A $ is $ B\wedge C $.  By Definition \ref{KripkeBelpanEA}, $ T\in\vartheta(B\wedge C,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $T\in\vartheta(B,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ and $T\in\vartheta(C,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff (ind.\;hyp.) $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ and $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(C),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff (Def. \ref{Four-valuedS5}) $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B)\wedge\mathfrak{g}(C),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff (Def. \ref{g}) $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B\wedge C),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $.

\item $ A $ is $ \neg(B\wedge C) $.  $ T\in\vartheta(\neg(B\wedge C),x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $ F\in\vartheta(B\wedge C,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff (Def. \ref{KripkeBelpanEA}) $F\in\vartheta(B,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ or $F\in\vartheta(C,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff (ind.\;hyp.) $F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ or $F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(C),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff (Def. \ref{Four-valuedS5}) $F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B)\wedge\mathfrak{g}(C),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff (Def. \ref{g}) $F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B\wedge C),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(\neg(B\wedge C)),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $.
		
\item $ A $ is $ \circ_IB $, where $ I\in\{T,F \} $. By Def. \ref{KripkeBelpanEA}, $ T\in\vartheta({\circ_I} B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $I\in\vartheta(B,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ implies $ \forall_{y\in W}I\in\vartheta(B,y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ 
iff (Lemma \ref{X}) $T\in\vartheta(B^I,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ implies $ \forall_{y\in W}T\in\vartheta(B^I,y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $
iff (by the ind.\;hyp.) $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^I),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ implies $ \forall_{y\in W}T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^I),y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $
iff (by Def. \ref{Four-valuedS5}) $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^I),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ implies $ T\in\vartheta^\prime(\Box \mathfrak{g}(B^I),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff (by Def. \ref{Four-valuedS5}) $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^I)\rightarrow\Box \mathfrak{g}(B^I),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff (by Def. \ref{g}) $ T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}({\circ_I} B),x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $.

\item $ A $ is $ \neg{\circ_I}B $, where $ I\in\{T,F \} $. By Def. \ref{KripkeBelpanEA}, $ T\in\vartheta(\neg{\circ_I} B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $ F\in\vartheta({\circ_I} B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff  $I\in\vartheta(B,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ and $ \exists_{y\in W}{\sim} I\in\vartheta(B,y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff (Lemma \ref{X}) $T\in\vartheta(B^I,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ and $ \exists_{y\in W} T\in\vartheta(B^{{\sim} I},y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff $T\in\vartheta(B^I,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ and $ \exists_{y\in W} F\in\vartheta(B^{I},y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $
iff (by the ind.\;hyp.) $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^I),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ and $ \exists_{y\in W}F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^{I}),y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ 
iff (Def. \ref{Four-valuedS5}) $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^I),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ and $ F\in\vartheta^\prime(\Box\mathfrak{g}(B^{I}),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff $F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^I)\rightarrow\Box\mathfrak{g}(B^I),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\neg(\mathfrak{g}(B^I)\rightarrow\Box\mathfrak{g}(B^I)),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff (Def. \ref{g}) 
 $F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}({\circ_I}B),x)$ in~$ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(\neg{\circ_I}B),x)$ in~$ \mathfrak{M}_2 $. 

\item $ A $ is $ \circ_iB $, where $ i\in\{t,b,n,f \} $.  By Def. \ref{KripkeBelpanEA}, $ T\in\vartheta({\circ_i} B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff  
 $\vartheta(B,x)=i$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ implies $ \forall_{y\in W} \vartheta(B,y)=i$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff (Lemma \ref{X}) 
 $T\in\vartheta(B^i,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ implies $ \forall_{y\in W} T\in\vartheta(B^i,y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ 
  iff (this lemma, (2)(a))
 $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^i),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ implies $ \forall_{y\in W}T\in \vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^i),y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $  
  iff (Def. \ref{Four-valuedS5})  
  $T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B)^i),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ implies $ T\in \vartheta^\prime(\Box(\mathfrak{g}(B^i)),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $  
  iff (Def. \ref{Four-valuedS5})
   $T\in\vartheta^\prime((\mathfrak{g}(B^i)\rightarrow\Box(\mathfrak{g}(B^i)),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ 
  iff (Def. \ref{g}) $ T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}({\circ_i} B),x) $~in~$ \mathfrak{M}_2 $. 
  
\item $ A $ is $ \neg{\circ_i}B $, where $ i\in\{t,b,n,f \} $.  By Def. \ref{KripkeBelpanEA}, $ T\in\vartheta(\neg{\circ_i} B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff  $ F\in\vartheta({\circ_i} B,x) $ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ iff 
$\vartheta(B,x)=i$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ and $ \exists_{y\in W}\vartheta(B,y)\not=\ast i$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $

$T\in\vartheta(B^i,x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ and $ \exists_{y\in W}F\in\vartheta(B^i,y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_1 $ 
 iff 
(this lemma, (2))
$T\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^i),x)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ and $ \exists_{y\in W}F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^i),y)$ in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $

    $F\in\vartheta^\prime(\mathfrak{g}(B^i)\rightarrow\Box \mathfrak{g}(B^i),x)$  in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $

  $ F\in\vartheta^\prime(g({\circ_i} B),x) $  in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $ iff $ T\in\vartheta^\prime(g(\neg{\circ_i} B),x) $  in $ \mathfrak{M}_2 $.
	\end{enumerate}
