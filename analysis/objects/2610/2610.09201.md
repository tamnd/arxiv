\label{theorem:cabling-module-intro}
	For every knot $K\subset S^3$ and integers $n>0$ and $m$, there is an isomorphism
	\[
	H_*\left(\mathcal X_0(K)^{\mathcal K}\widehat{\boxtimes}{}_{\mathcal K}\mathcal C_{n,m}\right)\cong\mathcal{HFL}(K_{n,nm})
	\]
	as modules over $R_n=\mathbb F[\mathscr{U}_1,\mathscr{V}_1,\ldots,\mathscr{U}_n,\mathscr{V}_n]$. Moreover, the type-$A$ module ${}_{\mathcal K}\mathcal C_{n,m}$ admits an explicit description by generators, relations, and structure maps.

<!-- remark-1: body_md -->
As mentioned above, the cabling modules constructed in this paper are also motivated by the study of Floer lasagna modules. At present, however, our description does not seem sufficiently flexible to give a general computation of these modules. One difficulty is that the $R_n$-modules arising from our cabling formula are in general not free. This makes it substantially harder to manipulate the resulting homology and to keep track of the coefficient actions. For example, in Chen's construction \cite{chen2022floer}, one works with the hat version of link Floer homology, which requires setting the variables $\mathscr{U}_i$ and $\mathscr{V}_i$ equal to zero; carrying this operation out directly from our non-free $R_n$-module description is not immediate.
	
	There is also additional cobordism information that is not addressed in the present paper. A computation of the Floer lasagna module also requires an understanding of the pair-of-pants, braid, and basepoint-moving cobordism maps. We expect to explore these maps in future work.

\label{def:Amodule}
	Suppose $\mathcal{A}$ is an associative algebra over $\boldsymbol{\mathbf{k}}$. A \emph{left $A_\infty$-module} (or, succinctly, \emph{left type $A$-module}) over $\mathcal{A}$, denoted by ${}_\mathcal{A} M$, is a left $\boldsymbol{\mathbf{k}}$-module $M$ equipped with a family of $\boldsymbol{\mathbf{k}}$-module maps 
	\[
	\{m_{j+1}: \mathcal{A}^{\otimes j} \otimes_{\boldsymbol{\mathbf{k}}} M \to M\}_{j \ge 0}
	\]
	such that, for each $a_1, \ldots, a_n \in \mathcal{A}$ and $x \in M$,

	\oplus
	\bigoplus_{k=2}^{r+1}B_k,
	\qquad 0\leq r\leq n-1,
	\]
	where $E_0=0$. In $E_r/E_{r-1}$, the inclusion map
	$\overline\sigma_r$ disappears because its target $B_r$ lies
	in $E_{r-1}$. Hence this quotient is the mapping cone of
	\[
	\overline\tau_r:A_r\longrightarrow B_{r+1}.
	\]
	By Lemma~\ref{lem:localizedpatternflip}, this map is a chain
	homotopy equivalence, so its cone is contractible. Successively
	canceling these finitely many pairs shows that $E$ is
	contractible.
	
	The quotient of $\mathscr C_\infty(C)$ by $E$ is $A_n$.
	Consequently, projection to the rightmost column is a chain
	homotopy equivalence
	\[
	\mathscr C_\infty(C)\simeq A_n
	=C\otimes_R M_n^0,
	\]
	where the last equality uses $q_n=1$. In particular, the
	internal differential of the full knot complex $C$ remains
	in the rightmost column.
	
	Lemma~\ref{lem:homologycolimit} and
	Lemma~\ref{lem:cablingflat} now give
	\begin{align*}
		\varinjlim_m H_*(\mathscr C_m(C))
		&\cong H_*(\mathscr C_\infty(C))\\
		&\cong H_*(C\otimes_R M_n^0)\\
		&\cong H_*(C)\otimes_R M_n^0.
	\end{align*}
	The final isomorphism uses the flatness of $M_n^0$ over $R$.
	
	For completeness, the rightmost coefficient module has the
	presentation
	\[
	M_n^0\cong\mathbb{F}[\mathscr{V},\mathscr{V}_1,\ldots,\mathscr{V}_n],
	\qquad
	\mathscr{U}=\prod_{i=1}^n\mathscr{V}_i,
	\qquad
	\mathscr{U}_i=\mathscr{V}\prod_{j\ne i}\mathscr{V}_j.
	\]
	Applying the elliptic interchange to the cable variables gives
	the colored homology in the orientation convention of the
	theorem. This completes the $R_n$-module computation.
