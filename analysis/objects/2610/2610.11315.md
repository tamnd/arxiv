Substituting $L_2^\star$ into Equation~\textup{(\ref{eq:joint_obj})} replaces $\widetilde H$ with the deflated Hessian
\begin{equation}
    \widetilde H_\perp = \widetilde H - \widetilde H L_1 \left( L_1^\top\widetilde H L_1 \right)^\dagger L_1^\top\widetilde H, \qquad \text{satisfying} \quad \widetilde H_\perp L_1 = 0.
    \label{eq:deflation}
\end{equation}
The derivation of $L_2^\star$ and proof are provided in Appendix~\ref{app:deflation}.
The identity $\widetilde H_\perp L_1=0$ shows that errors compensable by the protected low-rank component are eliminated from the residual objective; residual quantization thus targets only the remaining reconstruction error.
