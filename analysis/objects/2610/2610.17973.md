<!-- remark-1: body_md -->
Notice the contrast between the columns in \cref{eq:kinetic_source} and \cref{eq:total_energy_source} as the left-hand rates contain $\bm u$ and $\bm u_s\cdot\bm v$ and are therefore of no definite sign once the body moves, whereas their relative counterparts are sign definite for every prescribed $\bm u_s$. This is the main motivation for writing the energy balance relative to the body.

<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:semidiscrete_entropy}
Assume that the discrete metric identities \cref{eq:metric_identities} hold, the inviscid volume flux satisfies \cref{eq:tadmor_condition}, the interface flux is entropy stable, the viscous and physical-boundary discretisations satisfy their standard entropy estimates, and the nodal states are admissible. If $\theta\leq1$, $\lambda_{e,ijk}\geq0$, and the immersed source is evaluated at the quadrature nodes, then the coupled DGSEM satisfies
\begin{equation}
    \frac{\,\mathrm{d}}{\,\mathrm{d} t}
    \sum_{e\in\Omega}\left\langle J\eta(\bm q),1\right\rangle_{E,P}
    +\mathcal B_\eta
    =-\mathcal D_h^{v}
     -\mathcal D_h^{\mathrm{int}}
     -\mathcal D_h^{\mathrm{IB}}
    \leq0,
    \label{eq:semidiscrete_entropy_inequality}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal B_\eta$ contains the prescribed physical-boundary entropy transfer, $\mathcal D_h^{v}\geq0$ is the viscous entropy dissipation, $\mathcal D_h^{\mathrm{int}}\geq0$ is the interface dissipation, and
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal D_h^{\mathrm{IB}}
    =(1-\theta)
    \sum_{e\in\Omega}\sum_{(i,j,k)\in\mathcal I_e^s}
    \omega_i\omega_j\omega_k J_{e,ijk}
    \lambda_{e,ijk}\frac{\rho_{e,ijk}^2}{p_{e,ijk}}
    \lvert\bm u_{e,ijk}-\bm u_{s,e,ijk}\rvert^2
    \geq0.
    \label{eq:discrete_ib_dissipation}
\end{equation}
<!-- end theorem-1 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
Choose the entropy variables as test functions in \cref{eq:dg_weak_form} and sum over all elements. The temporal term satisfies $\bm w^T\partial_t\bm q=\partial_t\eta$. The SBP identity and the Tadmor condition reduce the inviscid volume contributions to entropy fluxes at element boundaries. Interior entropy-conservative contributions telescope, while the dissipative interface flux produces $-\mathcal D_h^{\mathrm{int}}$. The entropy-stable viscous discretisation produces $-\mathcal D_h^v$. At each collocation node, \cref{eq:entropy_contraction} gives
\begin{equation}
    \bm w_{e,ijk}^T\bm s_{\mathrm{IB},e,ijk}^{(\theta)}
    =-(1-\theta)\lambda_{e,ijk}
      \frac{\rho_{e,ijk}^2}{p_{e,ijk}}
      \lvert\bm u_{e,ijk}-\bm u_{s,e,ijk}\rvert^2.
\end{equation}
Multiplication by the positive nodal norm $\omega_i\omega_j\omega_kJ_{e,ijk}$ and summation gives $-\mathcal D_h^{\mathrm{IB}}$. Combining all contributions proves \cref{eq:semidiscrete_entropy_inequality}.
<!-- end proof-1 -->
<!-- theorem-2: body_md -->
\label{thm:semidiscrete_energy}
Let a collocated discretisation possess a positive diagonal norm and an entropy estimate before the immersed source is added, and apply \cref{eq:nodal_source} at any subset of nodes with $\chi_i\in[0,1]$. For $\lambda_{e,ijk}\geq0$ the entropy estimate is preserved whenever $\theta\leq1$, and the source contribution to the discrete relative total energy is
\begin{equation}
    \left.\frac{\,\mathrm{d}}{\,\mathrm{d} t}
    \sum_{e\in\Omega}\sum_{i,j,k}\omega_i\omega_j\omega_kJ_{e,ijk}
    \rho E_{\mathrm{rel},e,ijk}
    \right|_{\mathrm{IB}}
    =-\theta\sum_{e\in\Omega}\sum_{(i,j,k)\in\mathcal I_e^s}
    \omega_i\omega_j\omega_kJ_{e,ijk}
    \lambda_{e,ijk}\rho_{e,ijk}\lvert\bm u_{e,ijk}-\bm u_{s,e,ijk}\rvert^2\leq0
    \label{eq:discrete_energy_source}
\end{equation}
whenever $\theta\geq0$, whatever the prescribed $\bm u_s$. Penalised nodes contribute non-positive terms in both budgets and unpenalised nodes contribute zero, so neither result requires the entire cut element to be penalised, and both apply to positive-weight Gauss and Gauss--Lobatto collocation.
<!-- end theorem-2 -->
<!-- proof-2: body_md -->
The two source contributions are weighted sums of the pointwise identities \cref{eq:entropy_contraction} and \cref{eq:total_energy_source}, which hold at every collocation node. Every weight and Jacobian is positive, so multiplication by $\omega_i\omega_j\omega_kJ_{e,ijk}$ and summation preserves the sign of each term. Removing any terms from a sum of non-positive contributions cannot change its sign.
<!-- end proof-2 -->
