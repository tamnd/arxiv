\label{rem:VBCharts}
    For the tangent bundle $\pi: T\mathcal{X} \to \mathcal{X}$ of a manifold $\mathcal{X}$ we can define trivializations naturally by setting $\tau_{x,i}(v):=v_i$, where $v_i$ is the chart representation of $v \in T_x\mathcal{X}$ in the chart $(U_i, \phi_i)$ of $\mathcal{X}$ at $x$. Since tangent vectors transform by $t_{ji}^\prime(\phi_i(x))v_{i} = v_{j}$ under change of charts, the transition mapping is given by $T(x)=t_{ji}^\prime(\phi_i(x))$.

\label{def:Lagrangian}
    We define the \emph{Lagrangian function} corresponding to the optimal control problem \eqref{eq:OCP} as

        L : \mathcal{E} \times U &\to \mathbb{R} \\

    \end{align*}
