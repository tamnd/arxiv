<!-- proposition-1: body_md -->
\label{sec:tang-cotang-cover-1}
  Let $\mathscr{E}$ be an equation. Its tangent cover possesses the following
  properties
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item \label{item:1} sections of~$\mathbf{t}$ that are morphisms of
    equations are in one-to-one correspondence with symmetries
    of~$\mathscr{E}$\textup{;}
  \item \label{item:2} $\mathbf{t}$-shadows of symmetries linear in~$q_\sigma$
    are identified with $\mathscr{C}$-differential operators
    $\mathscr{R}\colon \varkappa\to \varkappa$ that take symmetries
    of~$\mathscr{E}$ to symmetries\textup{,} i.e.\textup{,} with recursion
    operators for symmetries\textup{;}
  \item \label{item:3} $\mathbf{t}$-shadows of cosymmetries linear
    in~$q_\sigma$ are identified with $\mathscr{C}$-differen\-tial operators
    $\mathscr{S}\colon \varkappa\to \hat{P}$ that take symmetries
    of~$\mathscr{E}$ to cosymmetries\textup{,} i.e.\textup{,} with
    pre-symplectic structures\textup{;}
  \item \label{item:4} $\mathscr{T}\mathscr{E}$ is endowed with a natural
    nilpotent vector field
    $\mathbf{X} = \sum_\sigma q_\sigma\partial/\partial u_\sigma$ of
    parity~$1$.
  \end{enumerate}
  Similarly\textup{,} the cotangent covering enjoys the properties
  \begin{enumerate}
  \item \label{item:5} sections of~$\mathbf{t^*}$ that are morphisms of
    equations are in one-to-one correspondence with cosymmetries
    of~$\mathscr{E}$\textup{;}
  \item \label{item:6} $\mathbf{t^*}$-shadows of symmetries linear
    in~$p_\sigma$ are identified with $\mathscr{C}$-differen\-tial operators
    $\mathscr{H}\colon \hat{P}\to \varkappa $ that take cosymmetries
    of~$\mathscr{E}$ to symmetries\textup{,} i.e.\textup{,} with pre-Poisson
    structures\textup{;}
  \item \label{item:7} $\mathbf{t^*}$-shadows of cosymmetries linear
    in~$p_\sigma$ are identified with $\mathscr{C}$-differen\-tial operators
    $\mathscr{R}\colon \hat{P}\to \hat{P}$ that take cosymmetries
    of~$\mathscr{E}$ to cosymmetries\textup{,} i.e.\textup{,} with recursion
    operators for cosymmetries\textup{;}
  \item \label{item:8} for any~$\mathscr{E}$\textup{,} its cotangent equation
    is always an Euler-Lagrange one and consequently carries a natural
    symplectic structure.
  \end{enumerate}

<!-- proposition-2: body_md -->
\label{sec:tang-cotang-cover-2}
  There is a one-to-one correspondence between conservation laws
  of~$\mathscr{T}\mathscr{E}$ linear in~$q_\sigma$ and cosymmetries
  of~$\mathscr{E}$. In a similar way\textup{,} There is a one-to-one
  correspondence between conservation laws of~$\mathscr{T}^*\mathscr{E}$
  linear in~$p_\sigma$ and symmetries of~$\mathscr{E}$.
<!-- end proposition-2 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
The proof is essential for us, because it provides an explicit method to
  construct conservation laws of the tangent and cotangent equations that is
  used in the forthcoming computations.

  Let $\varphi$ be a symmetry of~$\mathscr{E}$ and~$\bar{\varphi}$ be its extension
  to the space of the ambient jets. Then, by the Green formula~\eqref{eq:2},
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:5}
    \langle\ell_F(\bar{\varphi}), p\rangle - \langle\bar{\varphi},
    \ell_F^*(p)\rangle = d_h\omega,
  \end{equation}
  and we put $\omega_\varphi = \left.\omega\right|_{\mathscr{T}^*\mathscr{E}}$. Quite
  similarly, let~$\psi$ be a cosymmetry and~$\bar{\psi}$ be its
  extension. Then
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:4}
    \langle q, \ell_F^*(\psi)\rangle - \langle\ell_F(q), \psi\rangle =
    d_h\omega 
  \end{equation}
  and $\omega_\psi = \left.\omega\right|_{\mathscr{T}\mathscr{E}}$.
<!-- end proof-1 -->

<!-- proposition-3: body_md -->
Let $\varphi$ be a symmetry of Equation~\eqref{eq:6} with $a + b + 1 = 0$
  and~$\varphi_1$\textup{,} $\varphi_2$\textup{,} $\varphi_3$ be solutions to
  System~\eqref{eq:a9}. Then
  \begin{equation*}
    \varphi_1 = \mathscr{R}_1(\varphi) = \frac{u_x\varphi}{f_1},\quad
    \varphi_2 = \mathscr{R}_2(\varphi) = \frac{u_y\varphi}{f_2},\quad
    \varphi_3 = \mathscr{R}_3(\varphi) = \frac{u_t\varphi}{f_3}.    
  \end{equation*}
  are symmetries of~\eqref{eq:6} as well\textup{,} i.e.\textup{,} system of
  relations~\eqref{eq:a8} and~\eqref{eq:a9} is a B\"{a}cklund transformation
  that defines a recursion operator for symmetries of~\eqref{eq:6}. The
  inverse statement is also valid.
