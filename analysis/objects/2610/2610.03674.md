<!-- lemma-1: body_md -->
\label{lem:guessingfidelity}
Suppose $\{p_j,\varphi_B(j)\}_{j=1}^n$ is an ensemble of states and $\{\Lambda_B(j)\}_{j=1}^n$ is a POVM for which 
\begin{equation}
\sum_{j=1}^n p_j \operatorname{Tr}[\Lambda_B(j)\varphi_B(j)]\geq 1-\varepsilon\,.
\end{equation}
For $\ket{\varphi(j)}_{BE}$ a purification of $\varphi_B(j)$ for all $j$ and $V$ the following coherent implementation of $\Lambda$:
\begin{equation}
V:\ket{\psi}_{BE}\mapsto \sum_{j=1}^n \sqrt{\Lambda_B(j)}\ket{\psi}_{BE}\ket{j}_{A'}\,, 
\end{equation}
define the states
\begin{align}
\ket{\Psi}_{AA'BE}
&=\sum_{j=1}^n \sqrt{p_j} \ket{j}_{A}V\ket{\varphi(j)}_{BE}
=\sum_{j,j'=1}^n \sqrt{p_j} \ket{j}_A \sqrt{\Lambda_B(j')}\ket{\varphi(j)}_{BE} \ket{j'}_{A'}\\
\ket{\Psi'}_{AA'BE} 
&= \sum_{j=1}^n \sqrt{p_j} \ket{j}_A \ket{\varphi(j)}_{BE} \ket{j}_{A'}\,. 
\end{align} 
Then $\braket{\Psi'|\Psi}\geq 1-\varepsilon$.
<!-- end lemma-1 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
The proof just uses the operator inequality $\sqrt{\Gamma}\geq \Gamma$, which holds for all $0\leq \Gamma\leq  
  \text{\usefont{U}{bbold}{m}{n}1} 
$:
\begin{align}
\braket{\Psi'|\Psi}
&=\sum_{j=1}^n p_k \bra{\varphi(j)}\sqrt{\Lambda(j)}\ket{\varphi(j)}\geq \sum_{j=1}^n p_k \bra{\varphi(j)}{\Lambda(j)}\ket{\varphi(j)}\geq 1-\varepsilon\,. 
\end{align}
<!-- end proof-1 -->
<!-- lemma-2: body_md -->
\label{lem:coherentinfocalc}
Consider an arbitrary state $\sigma_{AB}$ and define $\rho_{AB}=\sum_{k\in \ensuremath{\mathbb{F}}_d} \ket{k}\!\!\bra{k}_A\sigma_{AB}\ket{k}\!\!\bra{k}_A$ and $\tau_{XAB}=\tfrac1d\sum_{x\in \ensuremath{\mathbb{F}}_d}\ket{x}\!\!\bra{x}_X\otimes Z_A^x\sigma_{AB} Z_A^{-x}$. 
Then 
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:coherentinfocalc}
-H(A|B)_\sigma=\log_2 d-H(A|B)_\rho-H(X|AB)_{\tau}\,.
\end{equation}
<!-- end lemma-2 -->
<!-- proof-2: body_md -->
By chain rules we have $H(X|AB)_{\tau}=H(AB|X)_{\tau}+H(X)_{\tau}-H(AB)_{\tau}$. 
Because the conditional states of $AB$ given $X$ are all related by unitaries, $H(AB|X)_{\tau}=H(AB)_\sigma$. 
In the next term we have $H(X)_{\tau}=\log_2 d$ by construction.
And since the average over the $Z^x_A$ operators will dephase the $A$ part of the state, $H(AB)_{\tau}=H(AB)_\rho$. 
Meanwhile, $H(A|B)_\rho=H(AB)_\rho-H(B)_\rho=H(AB)_\rho-H(B)_\sigma$, since $\rho_B=\sigma_B$. 
Substituting these equalities into the righthand side of \eqref{eq:coherentinfocalc} gives $H(B)_\sigma-H(AB)_\sigma$, the lefthand side.
<!-- end proof-2 -->
<!-- lemma-3: body_md -->
\label{lem:truncation}
Applied to $\ket{\gamma^{(\bar n)}}_{AB}$, the pure loss channel is a PDPC. 
Call the output $\sigma_{AB}$. With $r=\tfrac{\bar n}{\bar n+1}$,
\begin{equation}
-H(A|B)_{\sigma}\geq -H(A|B)_{\sigma'}- 4r^{d/2} g(\bar n/r^{d/2})-2h_2(r^{d/2})\,. 
\end{equation}
<!-- end lemma-3 -->
<!-- proof-3: body_md -->
Computing the squared overlap of the two input states we find  
\begin{align}
\braket{\chi^{(\bar n)}|\gamma^{(\bar n)}}^2
&={\bra{\chi^{(\bar n)}}P\ket{\chi^{(\bar n)}}}
=\frac{1}{\bar n+1}\sum_{n=0}^{d-1} r^n
=\frac{1}{\bar n+1}\frac{1-r^d}{1-r}
=1-r^d\,.
\end{align}
Therefore, by the relation between fidelity and trace distance, $\delta(\sigma'_{ABE},\sigma_{ABE})\leq r^{d/2}$. 
The proof is completed by appealing to Winter's entropy bound for energy-constrained states, in particular Lemma 15 of \cite{winter_tight_2016}. 

To do so, first note that both the $B$ and $E$ marginals are also close by monotonicity of the trace distance. 
For pure $\sigma$ we have $-H(A|B)_\sigma=H(B)_\sigma-H(E)_\sigma$, and similarly for $\sigma'$. 
Therefore the mean energy of $B$ is at most $\eta\bar n$ for $\sigma$ and $\sigma'$ (since truncating will just move weight to lower energies), while for $E$ the mean energy is at most $(1-\eta)\bar n$ in both states. 
Therefore Lemma 15 implies $|H(B)_\sigma-H(B)_{\sigma'}|\leq 2 r^{d/2}g(\bar nr^{-d/2})+h_2(r^{d/2})$. 
Combining this with the same argument for the entropy of the $E$ marginals gives the desired statement.
<!-- end proof-3 -->
<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:edcircuit}
For any $\sigma_{AB}$ and choice of orthonormal basis $\{\ket{k}_A\}$, 
the fidelity of $\Psi_{\bar A\bar R}$ with the maximally entangled state $\ket{\Phi}_{\bar A\bar R}$ satisfies 
\begin{equation}
F(\Phi_{\bar A \bar R},\Psi_{\bar A \bar R})\geq 1-(\sqrt{\varepsilon_X}+\sqrt{\varepsilon_Z})^2\,.
\end{equation}
<!-- end theorem-1 -->
<!-- proof-4: body_md -->
Let $\ket{\sigma}_{ABE}$ be a purification of $\sigma_{AB}$ and define the pure states $\{\ket{\varphi_k}_{BE}\}_{k\in \ensuremath{\mathbb{F}}_d}$ by $\sqrt{p_k}\ket{\varphi(k)}_{BE}={}_{A}\!\braket{k|\sigma}_{ABE}$. 
The state after the action of $V$ is 
\begin{equation}
\ket{\psi_1}\coloneq V\ket{\sigma}^{\otimes m}=\sum_{k^m} \sqrt{p_{k^m}} \ket{Hk^m}_{A^m} \ket{\varphi(k^m)}_{B^mE^m}\,.
\end{equation}
Applying $U_\Lambda$ with the ancilla $\ket{0}_{A'^m}$ gives 
\begin{equation}
\ket{\psi_2}\coloneq U_\Lambda\ket{\psi_1}=
\sum_{k^m,k'^m} \sqrt{p_{k^m}} \ket{Hk^m}_{A^m} \sqrt{\Lambda_{B^m}(k'^m,H_Zk^m)}\ket{\varphi(k^m)}_{B^mE^m}\ket{k'^m}_{A'^m}\,.
\end{equation}
By Lemma~\ref{lem:guessingfidelity}, $\braket{\psi_2'|\psi_2}\geq 1-\varepsilon_Z$ for 
\begin{equation}
\ket{\psi_2'}\coloneq \sum_{k^m} \sqrt{p_{k^m}} \ket{Hk^m}_{A^m} \ket{\varphi(k^m)}_{B^mE^m}\ket{k^m}_{A'^m}\,.
\end{equation}

Continue with $\ket{\psi_2'}$. 
Expressing the $A^m$ system in the Fourier conjugate basis yields:
\begin{align}
\ket{\psi'_2}
&=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{d^m}}\sum_{k^m,x^m} \sqrt{p_{k^m}}\ket{\widetilde{x^m}}_{A^m} \omega^{-x^m\cdot Hk^m}\ket{\varphi(k^m)}_{B^mE^m}\ket{k^m}_{A'^m}\\
&=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{d^m}}\sum_{k^m,x^m} \sqrt{p_{k^m}}\ket{\widetilde{x^m}}_{A^m} \ket{\varphi(k^m)}_{B^mE^m}Z_{A'^m}^{-(x^m)^TH}\ket{k^m}_{A'^m}\\
&=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{d^m}}\sum_{x^m} \ket{\widetilde{Gx^m}}_{A^m} Z_{A'^m}^{-x^m} \ket{\sigma}_{A'BE}^{\otimes m}\,.
\end{align} 
The Fourier transforms on the $\widehat{A}$ and $\underline{A}$ systems mean that subsequent control operations will extract the value of $G_Xx^m$. 
                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             
Then $U_\Gamma$ will yield
\begin{equation}
\ket{\psi_3'}\coloneq U_\Gamma\ket{\psi_2'}=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{d^m}}\sum_{x^m,\bar{y}} \ket{\widetilde{Gx^m}}_{A^m} \sqrt{\Gamma_{A'^mB^m}(\bar{y},G_Xx^m)}\, Z_{A'^m}^{-x^m} \ket{\sigma}_{A'BE}^{\otimes m}\ket{\bar{y}}_{\bar R}\,. 
\end{equation}
Here we define $(\bar x,\check x, \hat x,\underline{x})=Gx^m$. 
By Lemma~\ref{lem:guessingfidelity}, $\braket{\psi_3''|\psi_3'}\geq 1-\varepsilon_X$ for 
\begin{equation}
\ket{\psi_3''}\coloneq\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{d^m}}\sum_{x^m} \ket{\widetilde{Gx^m}}_{A^m} Z_{A'^m}^{-x^m} \ket{\sigma}_{A'BE}^{\otimes m}\ket{\bar{x}}_{\bar{R}}\,.
\end{equation}


Continuing with $\ket{\psi_3''}$, the controlled-$\bar Z$ from $\bar R$ to $A'^m$ removes the $\bar x$ dependence from the exponent; the most immediate way to accomplish this operation is to apply $V$ to $A'^m$ and then a controlled phase operation from $\bar R$ to $\bar A'$. 
Meanwhile, the inverse Fourier transform on $\bar A$ returns $\bar x$, meaning that the state $W\ket{\psi_3''}$ has the form $\ket{\Phi}_{\bar A\bar R}\otimes \ket{\xi}_{\widecheck{A}\widehat{A}\underline{A}B^mE^mA'^m}$ for a suitable $\ket{\xi}$. 

Now consider the fidelity of $W\ket{\psi''_3}$ with $WU_\Gamma U_\Lambda V\ket{\sigma}^{\otimes m}=WU_\Gamma \ket{\psi_2}$:
\begin{equation}
F(\ket{\Phi}_{\bar A\bar R}\ket{\xi},WU_\Gamma U_\Lambda V\ket{\sigma}^{\otimes m})
=|\bra{\psi_3''}U_\Gamma\ket{\psi_2}|.
\end{equation}
Applying the triangle inequality for fidelity (e.g.\ \cite[Proposition 10.5]{renes_quantum_2022}) gives 
\begin{align}
\arccos |\bra{\psi_3''}U_\Gamma\ket{\psi_2}| 
&\leq \arccos |\bra{\psi_3''}U_\Gamma\ket{\psi'_2}|+\arccos |\braket{\psi_2'|\psi_2}|\\
&\leq \arccos (1-\varepsilon_X)+\arccos (1-\varepsilon_Z)\,. 
\end{align}
Thus the fidelity of the $\bar A\bar R$ output with the maximally-entangled state is at least $1-(\sqrt{\varepsilon_X}+\sqrt{\varepsilon_Z})^2$.

<!-- lemma-4: body_md -->
\label{lem:resources}
The quantum operation $WU_\Gamma U_\Lambda V$ can be implemented by consuming $\ell$ entangled pairs and transmitting $s_x+s_z+2\ell$ dits of classical information. The net rate of entanglement distillation is therefore $\tfrac1m(k-\ell)\log_2 d$.

Since the $\widecheck{A}$ and $\widehat{A}$ registers will anyway be discarded immediately after the single control operations, we may replace those control operations by measurement and classical communication. 
On the other hand, register $\underline{A}$ needs to be transmitted to Bob via teleportation, at a cost of $\ell$ entangled pairs.

<!-- theorem-2: body_md -->
\label{thm:polar_scheme}
Let $0<\beta<\tfrac12$ and let the registers be given by~\eqref{eq:registers}. Let $\{\Lambda^{(y)}_w\}$ and $\{\Gamma^{(y)}_w\}$ be decoders for the transformed amplitude and phase information with average error probabilities $\bar P_{e,Z}$ and $\bar P_{e,X}$.
Then the scheme of Theorem~\ref{thm:edcircuit} with $H=\mathsf K_m^T$ generates $|\bar A|$ maximally entangled pairs of local dimension $d$. It consumes $|\underline A|$ such pairs and transmits $|\widecheck A|+|\widehat A|+2|\underline A|$ dits of classical information. The fidelity of its output with the maximally entangled state satisfies the bound of Theorem~\ref{thm:edcircuit} with $\varepsilon_Z=\bar P_{e,Z}$ and $\varepsilon_X=\bar P_{e,X}$.
For the successive-cancellation decoders, $\bar P_{e,Z}\leq(d-1)m2^{-m^\beta}$ and $\bar P_{e,X}\leq4(d-1)m2^{-m^\beta}$. The net rate $(|\bar A|-|\underline A|)\log d/m$ converges to $-H(A|B)_\sigma$.
