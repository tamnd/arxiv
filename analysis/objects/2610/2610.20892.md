<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:chain}
For every even $t \ge 4$ let $M = t^4$ and $r = t^3$, and consider the minimum-cost-flow LP on a path with return arcs $\min \sum_{i=1}^M u_i$ subject to $u_j - u_{j+1} - v_j = b_j$ ($1 \le j < M$), $u, v \ge 0$, with an explicit rational $b \in [0.97, 1.03]^{M-1}$. It has a unique nondegenerate optimal basis, and three dual-feasible starts satisfy, under exact DSE,
\begin{center}\@setsize\@setsize\@setsize\@setsize\@setsize\@setsize\@setsize\@setsize\small{10pt}\ixpt\@ixpt{10pt}\ixpt\@ixpt{10pt}\ixpt\@ixpt{10pt}\ixpt\@ixpt{10pt}\ixpt\@ixpt{10pt}\ixpt\@ixpt{10pt}\ixpt\@ixpt{10pt}\ixpt\@ixpt
\begin{tabular}{lccc}\toprule
Start & Overlap with $B^\star$ & Pivots & Work $K$\\ \midrule
Cold (all-slack: every $v_j$ basic) & $0$ & $M-1$ & $\sim \tfrac32 t^6$\\
Warm A (last $r$ chain arcs missing: singletons) & $1 - r/(M-1)$ & $r$ & $\sim 2t^5$\\
Warm B (every second of the last $2r$ arcs missing: pairs) & $1 - r/(M-1)$ & $r$ & $\sim t^7$\\ \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Every choice on the three paths is unique, every basis factorizes without fill, and the two warm bases have equal nonzero counts after every pivot. Hence $K_B/K_A \sim t^2/2 = \Theta(\sqrt{M})$, and $K_B/K_{\text{cold}} = \Theta(M^{1/4})$.

<!-- corollary-1: body_md -->
\label{cor:proxy}
With $r-1$ missing arcs, warm B has strictly higher overlap and one fewer pivot than warm A, and still $\sim t^2/2$ times its work.
<!-- end corollary-1 -->
