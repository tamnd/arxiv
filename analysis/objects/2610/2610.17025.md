As shown in Fig. \ref{fig1} for $n=63$, the theoretical probabilities in \eqref{W8} and \eqref{W9} agree well with the simulation results. More importantly, the foregoing analysis show that the distinction between error and non-error positions based on component counting is not restricted to minimum-weight dual codewords, but remains valid for dual codewords within the derived range of Hamming weights. This observation provides the theoretical basis for exploiting multi-weight dual codewords in the subsequent shift-sum decoding algorithms.
