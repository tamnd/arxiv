<!-- question-1: body_md -->
\label{question:BSS}
Given a constructible sheaf \(F\) of \(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\)-vector spaces on \(A\), does the direct limit
\[
  \varinjlim_n\ \mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[\ell^n]^{*}F\bigr),
\]
formed along the pullback maps of the tower of multiplication by \(\ell\), vanish for
\(i>\dim\operatorname{Supp} F\)?
<!-- end question-1 -->
<!-- thm-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:A}
Let \(A\) and \(\ell\) be as above and let \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(A,\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\).\footnote{All perverse sheaves in
this article are for the middle perversity.} For every positive integer \(m\) invertible in \(k\)
and every \(i\in\mathbb{Z}\),
\[
  \dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}\mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[m]^{*}P\bigr)\ \leq\ C_{P}\,m^{2(g-|i|)},
\]
where \(C_{P}\) is a constant independent of \(m\).\footnote{These bounds are optimal. For
\(A=E_1\times\dots\times E_g\) a product of elliptic curves and \(P=i_{B*}\mathbb{F}_{\ell}[b]\) with
\(B=E_1\times\dots\times E_b\subseteq A\) and
\(0\leq b\leq g\), one has \(\dim_{\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}\mathrm{H}^{\pm b}\bigl(A,[m]^{*}P\bigr)=m^{2(g-b)}\).}
<!-- end thm-1 -->
<!-- thm-2: body_md -->
\label{thm:B}
Let \(A\) be an abelian variety over an algebraically closed field \(k\), let \(\ell\) be a prime
invertible in \(k\), and let \(F\) be a constructible \(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\)-sheaf on \(A\). There is an integer
\(e\geq0\), depending on \(F\), such that the pullback map
\[
  [\ell^{e}]^{*}\colon\mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[\ell^{n}]^{*}F\bigr)\longrightarrow\mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[\ell^{n+e}]^{*}F\bigr)
\]
is zero for all \(n\geq0\) and all \(i>\dim\operatorname{Supp} F\). In particular
\[
  \varinjlim_n\ \mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[\ell^n]^{*}F\bigr)\ =\ 0
  \qquad\text{for all } i>\dim\operatorname{Supp} F.
\]
<!-- end thm-2 -->
<!-- thm-3: body_md -->
\label{thm:C}
Let \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(A,\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\). Then
\[
  \operatorname{codim}_{\operatorname{Spec} S}\ \operatorname{Supp}\ \mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(P)\bigr)\ \geq\ 2i \qquad\text{for all } i\geq 0.
\]

<!-- cor-1: body_md -->
\label{cor:D1}
Let \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(A,\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell})\). Then
\[
  \operatorname{codim}_{\operatorname{Spec}\mathfrak R}\ \operatorname{Supp}\ \mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(\mathfrak{FM}_A(P)\bigr)\ \geq\ 2i
  \qquad\text{for all } i\geq 0.
\]
The same estimate holds on \(\operatorname{Spm}(\mathfrak R)\).

<!-- cor-2: body_md -->
\label{cor:D3}
Let \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(A,\Lambda)\).
\begin{enumerate}[(1)]
\item If \(\Lambda=\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\), there is a closed subset \(Z\subseteq\operatorname{Spec} S\) of codimension at least
\(2\) such that, on \(\operatorname{Spec} S\setminus Z\), the complex \(\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(P)\) is a locally free sheaf
concentrated in degree \(0\), whose rank is \(\chi(A,P)\geq0\).
\item If \(\Lambda=\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}\), there is a closed subset
\(Z\subseteq\operatorname{Spm}(\mathfrak R)\) of codimension at least \(2\) such that, for every
\(\chi\notin Z\),
\[
  \mathrm{H}^{i}(A,P\otimes L_{\chi})=0\quad(i\neq0),\qquad
  \dim_{\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_{\ell}}\mathrm{H}^{0}(A,P\otimes L_{\chi})=\chi(A,P)\geq0.\footnotemark
\]
\end{enumerate}
\footnotetext{For non-negativity of \(\chi(A,P)\), see Franecki--Kapranov
\cite[Corollary~1.4]{FraneckiKapranov00} over \(\mathbb{C}\), and Debarre--Moonen
\cite[Proposition~3.7]{DebarreMoonen25} in arbitrary characteristic.}

<!-- para-1: body_md -->
\label{para:conventions}
Throughout, \(k\) is an algebraically closed field and \(\ell\) is a prime invertible in \(k\). Let
\(\Lambda\) denote the coefficient ring, which unless otherwise specified is \(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\). Since \(k\) is
algebraically closed, we suppress Tate twists.

For a scheme \(X\) of finite type over \(k\), let \(\Lambda_X\), or simply \(\Lambda\) when \(X\) is clear
from the context, denote the constant sheaf with value \(\Lambda\). Let \(D^{b}_{c}(X,\Lambda)\) denote the
bounded derived category of \(\Lambda\)-sheaves with constructible cohomology on the \'etale site of
\(X\), and let \(\operatorname{Perv}(X,\Lambda)\subseteqD^{b}_{c}(X,\Lambda)\) be the heart of the middle perverse
\(t\)-structure \cite{BBDG18}. Unless otherwise stated, cohomology of schemes of finite type over \(k\)
is \'etale cohomology.

Let \(\operatorname{CH}_a(X)\) denote the group of \(a\)-dimensional cycles on \(X\) with integer coefficients
modulo rational equivalence, and let \(\operatorname{CH}_*(X)=\bigoplus_a\operatorname{CH}_a(X)\).

Let \(A\) be an abelian variety over \(k\) of dimension \(g\geq1\). Unless stated otherwise, \(m\) denotes a
power \(\ell^n\) of \(\ell\), with \(n\geq0\). The multiplication map \([m]\colon A\to A\) is an \'etale
isogeny of degree \(m^{2g}\). Verdier duality on \(A\) is normalized by
\(\mathbb{D}(-)=R\mathcal{H}om_A(-,\Lambda_A[2g])\), so that \(\mathbb{D}\) preserves \(\operatorname{Perv}(A,\Lambda)\). Since \([m]\)
is \'etale, \([m]^*=[m]^!\) is perverse \(t\)-exact and commutes with \(\mathbb{D}\).
<!-- end para-1 -->
<!-- para-2: body_md -->
\label{para:transitions}
For \(F\inD^{b}_{c}(A,\Lambda)\) and powers \(m\mid m'\) of \(\ell\), pullback by \([m'/m]\) gives
\[
  \mathrm{H}^{i}(A,[m]^{*}F)\longrightarrow\mathrm{H}^{i}(A,[m']^{*}F).
\]
The direct limit of this system is the one in Question~\ref{question:BSS}.

Dually, the trace maps of the \'etale \([m'/m]\) induce
\(\mathrm{H}^{i}(A,[m']^{*}F)\to\mathrm{H}^{i}(A,[m]^{*}F)\). Verdier duality exchanges the two systems. Since \(A\)
is proper and \([m]^{*}\mathbb{D} F=\mathbb{D}[m]^{*}F\), there are natural isomorphisms
\[
  R\Gamma\bigl(A,[m]^{*}F\bigr)\ \simeq\ R\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}\bigl(R\Gamma(A,[m]^{*}\mathbb{D} F),\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\bigr),
  \qquad
  \mathrm{H}^{i}(A,[m]^{*}F)^{\vee}\ \cong\ \mathrm{H}^{-i}(A,[m]^{*}\mathbb{D} F),
\]
compatible with transitions, since duality carries the unit \(\mathrm{id}\to[m'/m]_{*}[m'/m]^{*}\) to
the counit \([m'/m]_{*}[m'/m]^{!}\to\mathrm{id}\).
<!-- end para-2 -->
<!-- para-3: body_md -->
\label{para:group-algebra}
Following \cite[\S3.1]{EK21}, for a profinite abelian group \(G\), define the completed group algebra (with \(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\)-coefficients) by
\[
  \mathbb{F}_{\ell}[[G]]\ :=\ \varprojlim_U \mathbb{F}_{\ell}[G/U],
\]
where \(U\) ranges over the open subgroups of \(G\). Let
\(T_{\ell} A=\varprojlim_n A[\ell^n]\cong\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}^{2g}\) be the \(\ell\)-adic Tate module. The subgroups
\(\ell^nT_{\ell} A\) are cofinal among the open subgroups of \(T_{\ell} A\), and the projection to
\(A[\ell^n]\) identifies \(T_{\ell} A/\ell^nT_{\ell} A\) with \(A[\ell^n]\). Thus
\[
  S\ :=\ \mathbb{F}_{\ell}[[T_{\ell} A]]\ \simeq\ \varprojlim_n\ \mathbb{F}_{\ell}\bigl[A[\ell^n]\bigr].
\]
Choose a \(\mathbb{Z}_{\ell}\)-basis \(\gamma_1,\dots,\gamma_{2g}\) of \(T_{\ell} A\), and let \(\gamma_{j,m}\) denote
the image of \(\gamma_j\) in \(A[m]\). Reducing this basis modulo \(m\) gives an isomorphism
\((\mathbb{Z}/m)^{2g}\xrightarrow{\sim}A[m]\) sending the \(j\)-th standard generator to \(\gamma_{j,m}\).
For every \(m=\ell^n\), there is therefore an isomorphism
\[
  \phi_m\colon
  \mathbb{F}_{\ell}[x_1,\dots,x_{2g}]/(x_1^m,\dots,x_{2g}^m)
  \ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ S_m:=\mathbb{F}_{\ell}[A[m]],
  \qquad x_j\longmapsto[\gamma_{j,m}]-1.
\]
Indeed \([\gamma_{j,m}]^m=1\), and
\((1+x_j)^m-1=x_j^m\) because \(m\) is a power of \(\ell\). For \(m\mid m'\), let
\(q_{m',m}\) be the quotient on the left sending each \(x_j\) to \(x_j\), and let
\(\tau_{m',m}\colon S_{m'}\to S_m\) be induced by \([m'/m]\colon A[m']\to A[m]\). Since
\([m'/m](\gamma_{j,m'})=\gamma_{j,m}\), we have
\[
  \tau_{m',m}\circ\phi_{m'}\ =\ \phi_m\circ q_{m',m}.
\]
Thus the isomorphisms \(\phi_m\) are compatible with the transition maps.

Let \(\mathfrak{m}=(x_1,\dots,x_{2g})\) and \(I_m=(x_1^m,\dots,x_{2g}^m)\). The inclusions
\[
  \mathfrak{m}^{2g(m-1)+1}\ \subseteq\ I_m\ \subseteq\ \mathfrak{m}^m
\]
show that the ideals \(I_m\) are cofinal with the powers of \(\mathfrak{m}\). Passing to inverse limits in the
compatible isomorphisms above gives a topological isomorphism
\[
  \mathbb{F}_{\ell}[[x_1,\dots,x_{2g}]]\ \xrightarrow{\ \sim\ }\ S,
  \qquad 1+x_j\longmapsto[\gamma_j],
\]
under which \(S\to S_m\) is the quotient by \(I_m\). In particular, \(S\) is a complete regular local
ring of dimension \(2g\) with maximal ideal \(\mathfrak{m}\) and residue field \(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\).

<!-- para-4: body_md -->
\label{para:tautological}
We take the identity as base point and let \(\pi_1(A)=\pi_1(A,0)\). For \(m=\ell^{n}\), the
multiplication map \([m]\colon A\to A\) is an \(A[m]\)-torsor for the translation action, hence a
connected Galois cover classified by a surjection \(\pi_1(A)\twoheadrightarrow A[m]\)
\cite[Tag~\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03SF}{03SF}]{stacks-project}.
These surjections are compatible with the maps \([m'/m]\colon A[m']\to A[m]\) and induce a surjection
\(\pi_1(A)\toT_{\ell} A\).
For \(v\inT_{\ell} A\) with images \(v_m\in A[m]\), the basis elements \([v_m]\in S_m\) are compatible under
the maps \(\tau_{m',m}\) of (\ref{para:group-algebra}) and define a unit \([v]\in S\) with inverse
\([-v]\). The continuous tautological character is
\[
  \rho\colon\pi_1(A,0)\twoheadrightarrowT_{\ell} A\xrightarrow{\ v\mapsto[v]\ }S^{\times},
\]
the canonical character of \cite[\S4.1]{EK21} for \(\pi=T_{\ell} A\).

For every Artinian quotient \(S'\) of \(S\), let \(L_{S'}\) be the rank one \(S'\)-local system
attached to the character induced by \(\rho\). A further quotient \(S'\twoheadrightarrow S''\)
induces a transition map \(L_{S'}\to L_{S''}\) and an isomorphism
\(L_{S'}\otimes_{S'}S''\simeq L_{S''}\). Let \(\mathcal{L}=(L_{S'})_{S'}\) be this projective system.
Its restriction to \(S/\mathfrak{m}^{N}\), for \(N\geq1\), is an \(\mathfrak{m}\)-adic projective system with lisse
terms \cite[Expos\'e~V, D\'efinition~3.1.1]{SGA5}, and defines an object of the heart of the
\(t\)-structure on adic complexes of \cite[Theorem~6.3(i)]{Eke90}.

For \(F\inD^{b}_{c}(A,\Lambda)\), let \(F\otimes_{\Lambda}\mathcal{L}\) denote the constructible adic complex
\((F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S/\mathfrak{m}^{N}})_{N\geq1}\). Following \cite[\S4.1]{EK21}, define
\[
  \widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(F)\ :=\
  R\varprojlim_{N\geq1}R\Gamma\bigl(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S/\mathfrak{m}^{N}}\bigr)\ \in\ D(S).
\]
This is the completed stalk at the trivial character of the \(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\)-linear Fourier--Mellin transform
of \(F\).

The first lemma below identifies the complexes
\(R\Gamma(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S_m})\) with the tower of Question~\ref{question:BSS}, and the second
proves the finiteness of \(\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(F)\) and its base change to the Artinian quotients of \(S\).

<!-- lem-1: body_md -->
\label{lem:finite-level}
For every \(F\inD^{b}_{c}(A,\Lambda)\) there are isomorphisms
\[
  R\Gamma\bigl(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S_m}\bigr)\ \simeq\ R\Gamma\bigl(A,[m]^{*}F\bigr)
\]
compatible with the transition maps, those induced by \(\mathcal{L}\) on the left and the trace maps of
(\ref{para:transitions}) on the right.
<!-- end lem-1 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
By (\ref{para:tautological}), \(L_{S_m}\) is the local system attached to the representation of
\(\pi_1(A)\) on \(S_m=\Lambda[A[m]]\) through the surjection \(\pi_1(A)\twoheadrightarrow A[m]\) and
the regular representation of \(A[m]\). Since \([m]\) is finite Galois with group \(A[m]\), acting by
translation on the source, \([m]_{*}\Lambda_A\) is the local system attached to the same representation
\cite[Tags~\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/03RV}{03RV}
and~\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0DV5}{0DV5}]{stacks-project}, so that
\([m]_{*}\Lambda_A\simeq L_{S_m}\) as \(S_m\)-local systems. Moreover this is compatible with
the transition maps, the quotient \(S_{m'}\to S_m\) corresponding to \([m]_{*}\) of the trace
\([m'/m]_{*}\Lambda_A\to\Lambda_A\), both being induced by the quotient \(A[m']\to A[m]\).

The projection formula for the finite map \([m]\) then gives
\[
  F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S_m}
  \ \simeq\ F\otimes_{\Lambda}[m]_{*}\Lambda_A
  \ \simeq\ [m]_{*}\bigl([m]^{*}F\bigr),
\]
and applying \(R\Gamma(A,-)\), with \(R\Gamma(A,[m]_{*}Q)\simeq R\Gamma(A,Q)\) for the finite morphism
\([m]\), gives the isomorphism. The transition for \(m\mid m'\) is induced by the trace of
\([m'/m]\), which is the trace map of (\ref{para:transitions}).

<!-- lem-2: body_md -->
\label{lem:FM-finiteness}
Let \(F\inD^{b}_{c}(A,\Lambda)\). The complex \(\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(F)\) is perfect over \(S\), and for every
Artinian quotient \(S'\) of \(S\) there is a natural isomorphism
\[
  \widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(F)\otimes^{L}_{S}S'\ \simeq\ R\Gamma\bigl(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S'}\bigr),
\]
compatible with the transition maps of \(\mathcal{L}\) of (\ref{para:tautological}) as \(S'\) varies. 
\footnote{See also \cite[Proposition~4.1(1)--(2)]{EK21} for the same properties with integral
coefficients.}
<!-- end lem-2 -->
<!-- proof-2: body_md -->
Since \(S\) is regular, every complex in \(D^{b}_{\mathrm{coh}}(S)\) is perfect. By
\cite[Theorem~6.3(iii)]{Eke90}, the direct image \(R\Gamma(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}\mathcal{L})\) is a bounded
constructible adic complex over the point, and its reduction modulo \(\mathfrak{m}^{N}\) is
\(R\Gamma(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S/\mathfrak{m}^{N}})\). Since \(S\) is complete, the equivalences of
\cite[Theorems~7.1(i) and~7.2(i)]{Eke90} give \(\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(F)\in D^{b}_{\mathrm{coh}}(S)\) and,
compatibly as \(N\) varies, natural isomorphisms
\[
  \widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(F)\otimes^{L}_{S}S/\mathfrak{m}^{N}\ \simeq\ R\Gamma(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S/\mathfrak{m}^{N}}).
\]

For an Artinian quotient \(S'\), choose \(N\geq1\) with \(\mathfrak{m}^{N} S'=0\) and let \(B=S/\mathfrak{m}^{N}\).
Then \(S'\) is a \(B\)-algebra and \(L_B\otimes^{L}_{B} S'\simeq L_{S'}\). The projection formula for
the proper morphism \(A\to\operatorname{Spec} k\) \cite[Expos\'e~XVII, Proposition~5.2.9]{ArtinGrothendieckSGA4} gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
  R\Gamma(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_B)\otimes^{L}_{B} S'
  &\simeq R\Gamma(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_B\otimes^{L}_{B} S')\\
  &\simeq R\Gamma(A,F\otimes_{\Lambda}L_{S'}).
\end{aligned}
\]
Combining this with the isomorphism for \(B\) proves the statement for \(S'\). Naturality gives
compatibility as \(S'\) varies.

<!-- lem-3: body_md -->
\label{lem:tower-vanishing}
Let \(F\inD^{b}_{c}(A,\Lambda)\) and let \(K=\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(\mathbb{D} F)\). There is a natural isomorphism in
\(D(\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\)
\begin{equation}\label{eq:colimit-dual}
  K\ \simeq\ R\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}\Bigl(\varinjlim_{n}R\Gamma\bigl(A,[\ell^{n}]^{*}F\bigr),\ \mathbb{F}_{\ell}\Bigr),
\end{equation}
where the transition maps are the pullback maps. Moreover, the following are equivalent.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(K\in D^{\geq0}(S)\).
\item \(\varinjlim_n R\Gamma\bigl(A,[\ell^n]^{*}F\bigr)\in D^{\leq0}(\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\).
\item There is an integer \(e\geq0\) such that the pullback map
\[
  [\ell^{e}]^{*}\colon\mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[\ell^{n}]^{*}F\bigr)\longrightarrow\mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[\ell^{n+e}]^{*}F\bigr)
\]
is zero for all \(n\geq0\) and all \(i>0\).
\end{enumerate}
<!-- end lem-3 -->
<!-- proof-3: body_md -->
Let \(C_n=R\Gamma(A,[\ell^n]^{*}F)\) and \(C=\varinjlim_n C_n\). The definition of \(K\), cofinality in
(\ref{para:group-algebra}), Lemma~\ref{lem:finite-level} and the duality of (\ref{para:transitions}) give
\[
  K\ \simeq\ R\varprojlim_nR\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}(C_n,\mathbb{F}_{\ell}),
\]
with the trace maps corresponding to the duals of the pullback maps. For every integer \(a\), the
inverse system \((\mathrm{H}^{-a}(C_n)^{\vee})_n\) consists of finite-dimensional vector spaces, so it
satisfies the Mittag--Leffler condition and has vanishing \(\varprojlim^{1}\).
Since filtered colimits and \(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\)-linear duality are exact, the natural comparison map
\[
  R\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}(C,\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\ \longrightarrow\ K
\]
induces, for every \(a\), the isomorphisms
\[
  \bigl(\varinjlim_n\mathrm{H}^{-a}(C_n)\bigr)^{\vee}
  \ \simeq\ \varprojlim_n\mathrm{H}^{-a}(C_n)^{\vee}
  \ \simeq\ \mathrm{H}^a(K).
\]
This proves the isomorphism in \eqref{eq:colimit-dual}.

A vector space vanishes if and only if its dual vanishes, so
\(C\in D^{\leq0}(\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\) if and only if \(K\in D^{\geq0}(\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\). The latter is equivalent to
\(K\in D^{\geq0}(S)\). This proves that (1) and (2) are equivalent.

Since filtered colimits are exact, \(\mathrm{H}^{i}(C)=\varinjlim_n\mathrm{H}^{i}(C_n)\), and (3) implies (2).

Finally we assume (1) and prove (3). By \ref{item:M1} at \(m=1\) and \ref{item:M2}, \(K\) is represented by a bounded complex
\(D^{\bullet}\) of finite free \(S\)-modules, which by (1) is exact in every degree \(j<0\). Let
\(B^{j+1}:=d(D^{j})\subseteq D^{j+1}\). Since \(D^{\bullet}\) is bounded, the Artin--Rees lemma
\cite[Tag~\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00IN}{00IN}]{stacks-project} gives an integer
\(c\geq0\) such that
\[
  B^{j+1}\cap\mathfrak{m}^{r}D^{j+1}\ \subseteq\ \mathfrak{m}^{r-c}B^{j+1}
  \qquad\text{for all } r\geq c \text{ and all } j<0.
\]
Choose \(e\geq0\) with \(\ell^{e}\geq2g+c+1\). We claim
\[
  [\ell^{e}]^{*}\colon\mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[\ell^{n}]^{*}F\bigr)\longrightarrow\mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(A,[\ell^{n+e}]^{*}F\bigr)
\]
is zero for all \(n\geq0\) and all \(i>0\).

Let \(m=\ell^{n}\) and \(m'=\ell^{n+e}\). For a finite
free \(S\)-module \(D\) one has \(D\otimes_{S}S_m=D/I_mD\), so \(K\otimes^{L}_{S}S_m\) is computed by
\(D^{\bullet}/I_mD^{\bullet}\), and the map \(K\otimes^{L}_{S}S_{m'}\to K\otimes^{L}_{S}S_m\) induced by
the quotient \(S_{m'}\to S_m\) by the projection
\(D^{\bullet}/I_{m'}D^{\bullet}\to D^{\bullet}/I_mD^{\bullet}\).


Let \(j<0\) and let \(z\in D^{j}\) represent a class in \(\mathrm{H}^{j}(D^{\bullet}/I_{m'}D^{\bullet})\), so that
\(dz\in I_{m'}D^{j+1}\subseteq\mathfrak{m}^{m'}D^{j+1}\) by (\ref{para:group-algebra}). Then
\(dz\in B^{j+1}\cap\mathfrak{m}^{m'}D^{j+1}\subseteq\mathfrak{m}^{m'-c}B^{j+1}\), so \(dz=dw\) for some
\(w\in\mathfrak{m}^{m'-c}D^{j}\). Thus \(z-w\) is a boundary, since \(D^{\bullet}\) is exact in degree \(j\).
Since \(m'-c\geq(2g+c+1)m-c\geq2g(m-1)+1\), (\ref{para:group-algebra}) gives \(\mathfrak{m}^{m'-c}\subseteq I_m\),
so \(w\in I_mD^{j}\). Hence the image of \(z\) in \(D^{j}/I_mD^{j}\) is a boundary, and the map
\(\mathrm{H}^{j}(K\otimes^{L}_{S}S_{m'})\to\mathrm{H}^{j}(K\otimes^{L}_{S}S_m)\) is zero for every \(j<0\). By
\ref{item:M1}, this map is the trace map \(\mathrm{H}^{j}(A,[m']^{*}\mathbb{D} F)\to\mathrm{H}^{j}(A,[m]^{*}\mathbb{D} F)\), and by
(\ref{para:transitions}) the pullback map \(\mathrm{H}^{-j}(A,[m]^{*}F)\to\mathrm{H}^{-j}(A,[m']^{*}F)\) is its
\(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\)-linear dual, hence also zero.

<!-- lem-4: body_md -->
\label{lem:bertini}
Let \(X\) be a smooth projective variety over \(k\) and \(H\) a very ample divisor on \(X\). Let
\(Y\subseteq X\) be a smooth projective subvariety of dimension \(r\geq1\), and let
\(C\subseteq T^{*}Y\) be a closed conical subset all of whose irreducible components have dimension
\(r\). Then, for every member \(Z\) of a dense open subset of the projective space \(|H|\), the
intersection \(Z\cap Y\) is smooth of dimension \(r-1\) and the inclusion
\(Z\cap Y\hookrightarrow Y\) is properly \(C\)-transversal.
<!-- end lem-4 -->
<!-- proof-4: body_md -->
Embed \(X\subseteq\mathbb{P}:=\mathbb{P}\bigl(\mathrm{H}^{0}(X,\mathcal{O}_X(H))^{\vee}\bigr)\) by the very ample \(|H|\), so that
members of \(|H|\) are the hyperplane sections of \(X\). Let \(\pi\colon T^{*}\mathbb{P}|_{Y}\to T^{*}Y\) be
the canonical surjection of bundles on \(Y\) and let
\[
  C''\ :=\ \pi^{-1}(C)\ \cup\ T^{*}_{Y}\mathbb{P}\ \subseteq\ T^{*}\mathbb{P},
\]
a closed conical subset all of whose irreducible components have dimension \(\dim\mathbb{P}\). By
\cite[Lemma~1.3.7.2]{Sai} the hyperplanes \(\mathsf{H}\subset\mathbb{P}\) which are properly
\(C''\)-transversal form a dense open subset of \(|H|\). We claim that every such \(\mathsf{H}\)
satisfies the conclusion with \(Z=\mathsf{H}\cap X\). Let \(z\in\mathsf{H}\cap Y\), and choose a local
equation \(f=0\) for \(\mathsf{H}\) near \(z\). Let \(\theta=df_z\in T^{*}_{z}\mathbb{P}\), and let
\(\bar\theta\in T^{*}_{z}Y\) be its image under \(\pi\). Then \(\bar\theta=d(f|_Y)_z\).
Transversality of \(\mathsf{H}\) to \(T^{*}_{Y}\mathbb{P}\) gives \(\bar\theta\neq0\), so \(\mathsf{H}\cap Y\)
is a smooth divisor in \(Y\) near \(z\)
\cite[Tag~\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/057C}{057C}]{stacks-project}.
Its conormal line at \(z\) is generated by \(\bar\theta\). Transversality of \(\mathsf{H}\) to
\(\pi^{-1}(C)\) gives \(\bar\theta\notin C_{z}\), which is \(C\)-transversality of
\(\mathsf{H}\cap Y\hookrightarrow Y\) at \(z\).

Next we show proper \(C\)-transversality. Note that \(\mathsf{H}\times_{\mathbb{P}}\pi^{-1}(C)=\pi^{-1}(C)|_{\mathsf{H}\cap Y}\) is
an affine bundle of relative dimension \(\dim\mathbb{P}-r\) over \((\mathsf{H}\cap Y)\times_{Y}C\). Its
irreducible components have dimension at most \(\dim\mathbb{P}-1\), being contained in irreducible
components of \(\mathsf{H}\times_{\mathbb{P}}C''\), and at least \(\dim\mathbb{P}-1\), by Krull's principal ideal
theorem, since \(\mathsf{H}\) is a Cartier divisor in \(\mathbb{P}\) and \(\pi^{-1}(C)\) has pure dimension
\(\dim\mathbb{P}\). Hence every irreducible component of \((\mathsf{H}\cap Y)\times_{Y}C\) has dimension
\((\dim\mathbb{P}-1)-(\dim\mathbb{P}-r)=r-1\).

\label{lem:scaling}
Let \(f\colon X\to X\) be \'etale and let \(F\inD^{b}_{c}(X,\Lambda)\).
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\operatorname{cc}_{X}(f^{*}F)=f^{*}\operatorname{cc}_{X}(F)\) in \(\operatorname{CH}_{\bullet}(X)\), where
\(f^{*}\) is the flat pullback of cycle classes along \(f\).
\item For \(0\leq a\leq d\) and \(D_{1},\dots,D_{a}\in N^{1}(X)_{\mathbb{R}}\),
\[
  \gamma_{a}(f^{*}F)\bigl(f^{*}D_{1},\dots,f^{*}D_{a}\bigr)\ =\ \deg(f)\,\gamma_{a}(F)(D_{1},\dots,D_{a}).
\]
In particular, if \(h'\in N^{1}(X)_{\mathbb{R}}\) satisfies \(f^{*}h'=h\), then
\(c_a(f^{*}F)=\deg(f)\,\gamma_a(F)(h')\) for \(0\leq a\leq d\), where the characteristic numbers
on the left are computed with respect to \(h\).
\end{enumerate}
<!-- end lem-5 -->
<!-- proof-5: body_md -->
(1) is the \'etale pullback formula of \ref{item:S9}.

(2) Both sides are multilinear in the \(D_{i}\), so we may take the \(D_{i}\) to be classes of
divisors. Chern classes commute with flat pullback \cite[Proposition~2.5(d)]{Ful98}, so (1) gives
\[
  f^{*}D_{1}\cdots f^{*}D_{a}\cap\operatorname{cc}_{X,a}(f^{*}F)
  \ =\ f^{*}\Bigl(D_{1}\cdots D_{a}\cap\operatorname{cc}_{X,a}(F)\Bigr),
\]
and taking degrees gives the identity, since flat pullback along \(f\) multiplies the degree of a
zero-cycle by \(\deg(f)\) \cite[Example~1.7.4]{Ful98}. The last statement follows by taking
\(D_{1}=\dots=D_{a}=h'\).

\label{lem:flags}
Let \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(X,\Lambda)\). There exists a flag of smooth closed subschemes
\[
  Y_{0}\subset Y_{1}\subset\dots\subset Y_{d}=X,
\]
where \(Y_r\) is a complete intersection of \(d-r\) members of \(|H|\).
Moreover, if we define \(Q_r:=P|^*_{Y_r}[r-d]\), then each inclusion
\(\iota_r\colon Y_{r-1}\hookrightarrow Y_r\) is properly \(\operatorname{SS}(Q_r)\)-transversal and

  \qquad(1\leq r\leq d).

\label{prop:graded-estimate}
Let \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(X,\Lambda)\) and let the flag be as in Lemma~\ref{lem:flags}. Then for
\(0\leq r\leq d\)
\[
  \chi\bigl(Y_{r},\,Q_{r}\bigr)
  \ =\
  \sum_{a=d-r}^{d}\binom{a-1}{a-d+r}\,c_a(P),
\]
where the characteristic numbers \(c_a(P)\) are computed with respect to \(h\), and
\(\binom{-1}{0}=1\).

Consequently, if
\(f\colon X\to X\) is \'etale and \(h'\in N^{1}(X)_{\mathbb{R}}\) satisfies \(f^{*}h'=h\), then
for any flag \((Y_r)\) and sheaves \(Q_r\) constructed as in Lemma~\ref{lem:flags} for \(f^{*}P\),
\[
  \bigl|\chi\bigl(Y_{r},\,Q_{r}\bigr)\bigr|
  \ \leq\ 2^{d}\deg(f)\sum_{a=d-r}^{d}\bigl|\gamma_{a}(P)(h')\bigr|.
\]
<!-- end prop-1 -->
<!-- proof-7: body_md -->
Let \(j_r\colon Y_r\hookrightarrow X\) be the inclusion, and let \(h_r=j_r^*h\) and
\(\alpha_r=(j_r)_*\operatorname{cc}_{Y_r}(Q_r)\in\operatorname{CH}_\bullet(X)\). For \(1\leq s\leq d\), the
normal bundle of \(\iota_s\colon Y_{s-1}\hookrightarrow Y_s\) is \(\mathcal{O}_X(H)|_{Y_{s-1}}\).
The divisor pullback formula \ref{item:S9} and \ref{item:S3} give
\[
  \operatorname{cc}_{Y_{s-1}}(Q_{s-1})
  \ =\ (1-h_{s-1})^{-1}\cap\iota_s^!\operatorname{cc}_{Y_s}(Q_s).
\]
For \(\beta\in\operatorname{CH}_\bullet(Y_s)\), the identity
\((\iota_s)_*\iota_s^!\beta=h_s\cap\beta\) and the projection formula
\cite[Propositions~2.6(b) and~2.5(c)]{Ful98} therefore give
\[
  \alpha_{s-1}\ =\ \frac{h}{1-h}\cap\alpha_s.
\]
Since \(\alpha_d=\operatorname{cc}_X(P)\), iteration yields
\[
  (j_r)_*\operatorname{cc}_{Y_r}(Q_r)
  \ =\ \frac{h^{d-r}}{(1-h)^{d-r}}\cap\operatorname{cc}_X(P).
\]
Let \(c=d-r\). For \(c>0\), we expand \((1-h)^{-c}\) and take the degree of the zero-dimensional
component. By \ref{item:S8} and \ref{item:S4}, this gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:chi-refined}
  \chi(Y_r,Q_r)\ =\ \sum_{a=c}^d\binom{a-1}{a-c}\,
  \deg\Bigl(h^a\cap\operatorname{cc}_{X,a}(P)\Bigr).
\end{equation}
This holds also for \(c=0\), when only \(a=0\) contributes, with \(\binom{-1}{0}=1\).
By \eqref{eq:characteristic-numbers}, \(\deg(h^a\cap\operatorname{cc}_{X,a}(P))=c_a(P)\), so this
is the required formula.

For the bound, we apply the formula to \(f^{*}P\), which is perverse
since \(f\) is \'etale, use Lemma~\ref{lem:scaling}(2), and bound the binomial coefficients by \(2^{d}\).

<!-- lem-7: body_md -->
\label{lem:one-cut}
Let \(Y\) be a smooth projective variety, \(Q\in\operatorname{Perv}(Y,\Lambda)\), and let \(Z\subset Y\) be an ample
divisor, smooth and properly \(\operatorname{SS}(Q)\)-transversal, with perverse restriction
\(Q_Z:=Q|^{*}_{Z}[-1]\in\operatorname{Perv}(Z,\Lambda)\). Then
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y,Q)\simeq\mathrm{H}^{a-1}(Z,\,Q_Z)\) for \(a\geq2\), and \(\mathrm{H}^{1}(Y,Q)\) is a
quotient of \(\mathrm{H}^{0}(Z,\,Q_Z)\).
\item \(\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y,Q)\simeq\mathrm{H}^{a+1}(Z,Q_Z)\) for \(a\leq-2\), and \(\mathrm{H}^{-1}(Y,Q)\) is a
subobject of \(\mathrm{H}^{0}(Z,Q_Z)\).
\end{enumerate}
In particular
\[
  \sum_{a\neq0}\dim\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y,Q)
  \leq 2\dim\mathrm{H}^{0}(Z,Q_Z)+\sum_{a\neq0}\dim\mathrm{H}^{a}(Z,Q_Z).
\]
<!-- end lem-7 -->
<!-- proof-8: body_md -->
Let \(j\colon U=Y\setminus Z\hookrightarrow Y\) and \(i\colon Z\hookrightarrow Y\). Since \(Z\) is ample,
\(U\) is affine, so Artin vanishing \cite[Th\'eor\`eme~4.1.1]{BBDG18} gives
\(\mathrm{H}^{a}(U,j^{*}Q)=0\) for \(a>0\), and its Verdier dual gives
\(\mathrm{H}^{a}_{c}(U,j^{*}Q)=0\) for \(a<0\). The triangle \(i_{*}i^{!}Q\to Q\to Rj_{*}j^{*}Q\) and the
first vanishing give isomorphisms \(\mathrm{H}^{a}(Z,i^{!}Q)\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y,Q)\) for \(a\geq2\) together
with a surjection in degree \(1\). The triangle \(j_{!}j^{*}Q\to Q\to i_{*}i^{*}Q\) and the second
vanishing give isomorphisms \(\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y,Q)\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{H}^{a}(Z,i^{*}Q)\) for \(a\leq-2\) together
with an injection in degree \(-1\).

Proper transversality gives \(i^{!}Q\simeq i^{*}Q[-2]\) and makes \(Q_Z=i^{*}Q[-1]\) perverse by
\ref{item:S7}, thus implying (1) and (2). The inequality follows by summation.

\label{prop:recursion}
Fix \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(X,\Lambda)\) and a flag as in Lemma~\ref{lem:flags}. Let
\(B_{r}:=\sum_{a}\dim\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y_{r},Q_{r})\). Then
\[
  B_{r}\ \leq\ \bigl|\chi(Y_{r},Q_{r})\bigr|\ +\ 4\,B_{r-1}
  \qquad(1\leq r\leq d),
  \qquad
  B_{0}\ \leq\ H^{d}\,\sigma(P),
\]
and consequently
\[
  B_{r}\ \leq\ \sum_{s=1}^{r}4^{\,r-s}\bigl|\chi(Y_{s},Q_{s})\bigr|\ +\ 4^{r}H^{d}\,\sigma(P)
  \qquad(0\leq r\leq d).
\]
<!-- end lem-8 -->
<!-- proof-9: body_md -->
Fix \(1\leq r\leq d\) and let \(Y=Y_{r}\), \(Q=Q_{r}\) and \(Z=Y_{r-1}\). By Lemma~\ref{lem:flags},
\(Z\) is a smooth ample divisor in the smooth projective variety \(Y\), it is properly
\(\operatorname{SS}(Q)\)-transversal, and \(Q|^{*}_{Z}[-1]=Q_{r-1}\) is perverse, so Lemma~\ref{lem:one-cut}
gives \(\sum_{a\neq0}\dim\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y,Q)\leq2B_{r-1}\).
Thus
\[
  B_{r}=\dim\mathrm{H}^{0}(Y,Q)+\sum_{a\neq0}\dim\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y,Q)
  \leq\bigl|\chi(Y,Q)\bigr|+2\sum_{a\neq0}\dim\mathrm{H}^{a}(Y,Q)
  \leq\bigl|\chi(Y,Q)\bigr|+4B_{r-1}.
\]
For the base case, \(Y_0\) is a zero-dimensional complete intersection of \(d\) members of \(|H|\),
so \(\deg[Y_0]=H^d\). Since \(Y_0\) is smooth and \(k\) is algebraically closed, it consists of
\(H^d\) reduced points. Moreover, \(Q_0=P|^*_{Y_0}[-d]\), so each point contributes at most
\(\sigma(P)\) to \(B_0\) by \eqref{eq:sigma-definition}. Hence \(B_0\leq H^d\sigma(P)\).
Iterating the recursion gives the last inequality.

<!-- prop-2: body_md -->
\label{prop:finite-level}
Let \(f\colon X\to X\) be \'etale and let \(h'\in N^{1}(X)_{\mathbb{R}}\) satisfy \(f^{*}h'=h\).
Then for every \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(X,\Lambda)\) and every \(i\geq0\)
\[
  \dim_{\Lambda}\mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(X,f^{*}P\bigr)
  \ \leq\ 4^{d}\Bigl(H^{d}\,\sigma(P)\ +\ 2^{d}\deg(f)\sum_{a=i}^{d}\bigl|\gamma_{a}(P)(h')\bigr|\Bigr),
\]
and \(\dim_{\Lambda}\mathrm{H}^{-i}(X,f^{*}P)\) is bounded by the same expression with \(\mathbb{D} P\) in place
of \(P\).
<!-- end prop-2 -->
<!-- proof-10: body_md -->
Since \(f\) is \'etale, \(f^*P\) is perverse, so \(\mathrm{H}^i(X,f^*P)=0\) for \(i>d\) \cite[4.2.4]{BBDG18}, and we
may assume \(i\leq d\). Choose a flag \(Y_0\subset\cdots\subset Y_d=X\), cut out by members of \(|H|\), as in
Lemma~\ref{lem:flags} for \(f^*P\), and let
\[
  Q_r:=(f^*P)|^*_{Y_r}[r-d],\qquad
  B_r:=\sum_a\dim\mathrm{H}^a(Y_r,Q_r).
\]
By \eqref{eq:sigma-pullback}, the base case of Lemma~\ref{prop:recursion} gives
\[
  B_0\leq H^d\sigma(f^*P)=H^d\sigma(P),
\]
independently of \(f\) and of the chosen flag. For \(0\leq r\leq d\), 
Lemma~\ref{prop:recursion} and the Euler characteristic bound of
Proposition~\ref{prop:graded-estimate} give
\begin{align*}
  B_r&\leq 4^rH^d\sigma(P)
  +2^d\deg(f)\sum_{s=1}^r4^{r-s}\sum_{a=d-s}^d|\gamma_a(P)(h')|\\
  &\leq 4^r\Bigl(H^d\sigma(P)+2^d\deg(f)\sum_{a=d-r}^d|\gamma_a(P)(h')|\Bigr).
\end{align*}
For the second inequality, each inner sum is at most
\(\sum_{a=d-r}^d|\gamma_a(P)(h')|\), since \(s\leq r\), and
\(\sum_{s=1}^r4^{r-s}=(4^r-1)/3\leq4^r\).

For \(1\leq i\leq d\), Lemma~\ref{lem:one-cut}(1) gives
\[
  \mathrm{H}^0(Y_{d-i},Q_{d-i})\twoheadrightarrow
  \mathrm{H}^1(Y_{d-i+1},Q_{d-i+1})\xrightarrow{\sim}\mathrm{H}^i(X,f^*P),
\]
where the last arrow is the composite of \(i-1\) isomorphisms. Thus
\(\dim\mathrm{H}^i(X,f^*P)\leq B_{d-i}\). For \(i=0\), the same inequality is
\(\dim\mathrm{H}^0(X,f^*P)\leq B_d\). Taking \(r=d-i\) in the bound for \(B_r\) and using
\(4^{d-i}\leq4^d\) proves the estimate for \(0\leq i\leq d\). Finally the bound for \(\mathrm{H}^{-i}(X,f^{*}P)\)
follows from Verdier duality.

\]

By Verdier duality and the vanishing of cohomology outside \([-d,d]\), it suffices to prove the

\((\deg f)H^d=(f^{*}H)^d=q^dH^d\). Since \(H^d>0\), we have \(\deg f=q^d\).

Taking \(C_P\) to be the larger of the constants for \(P\) and \(\mathbb{D} P\) proves the theorem.

<!-- prop-3: body_md -->
\label{prop:HS-machine}
Let \(\mathcal{K}\) be a perfect complex over \(S\), and suppose \(\mathcal{K}\in D^{\leq g}(S)\) and that there is a constant
\(C\) with
\[
  \operatorname{length}_{S}\ \mathrm{H}^{i}\bigl(\mathcal{K}\otimes^{L}_{S}S/I_m\bigr)\ \leq\ C\,m^{2(g-i)}
  \qquad\text{for all } i\geq 0 \text{ and } m=\ell^n.
\]
Then \(\dim\operatorname{Supp}\mathrm{H}^{i}(\mathcal{K})\leq 2(g-i)\), that is \(\operatorname{codim}\operatorname{Supp}\mathrm{H}^{i}(\mathcal{K})\geq2i\), for all
\(i\geq0\).
<!-- end prop-3 -->
<!-- proof-13: body_md -->
Let \(M^{b}=\mathrm{H}^{b}(\mathcal{K})\), finite \(S\)-modules vanishing for \(b>g\). We argue by descending
induction on \(i\), the statement being vacuous for \(i>g\). The hyper-Tor spectral sequence
\[
  E_2^{-a,b}\ =\ \operatorname{Tor}^{S}_{a}\bigl(M^{b},\,S/I_m\bigr)\ \Longrightarrow\
  \mathrm{H}^{b-a}\bigl(\mathcal{K}\otimes^{L}_{S}S/I_m\bigr)
\]
has \(E_2^{0,i}=M^{i}/I_mM^{i}\) on its edge. The differentials into this spot originate from
\(E_r^{-r,\,i+r-1}\) with \(r\geq2\). These terms vanish for \(r>g-i+1\).

For the remaining terms, the inductive hypothesis gives
\(\dim\operatorname{Supp} M^{i+r-1}\leq 2(g-i-r+1)\leq 2(g-i)-2\).
Since \(x_1^m,\dots,x_{2g}^m\) is a regular sequence of \(S\), the Tor groups on the \(E_2\)-page are
its Koszul homology on the modules \(M^b\). For every nonzero \(M^b\), \cite[Corollary~7]{GR86} bounds these Koszul homology lengths by a polynomial
in \(m\) of degree at most \(\dim\operatorname{Supp} M^b\) (see also \cite[Proposition~6.14]{Mou24} for a multigraded
refinement). The inductive hypothesis therefore gives a constant \(D_i\geq0\), independent of \(m\),
such that the sum of the lengths of these source terms is at most \(D_i m^{2(g-i)-2}\).

The abutment in total degree \(i\) has length at most \(C\,m^{2(g-i)}\) by hypothesis. There are no
outgoing differentials from \(E_r^{0,i}\), so
\[
  \operatorname{length}_S\ \bigl(M^{i}/I_mM^{i}\bigr)
  \ \leq\ C\,m^{2(g-i)}+D_i m^{2(g-i)-2}\ \leq\ (C+D_i)m^{2(g-i)}.
\]
If \(M^i=0\), the desired bound is immediate. Otherwise, since \(I_m\subseteq\mathfrak{m}^m\), the same
upper bound holds for \(\operatorname{length}_S(M^i/\mathfrak{m}^mM^i)\). This function agrees for large \(m\) with its
Hilbert--Samuel polynomial, whose degree is \(\dim\operatorname{Supp} M^i\) and whose leading coefficient is
positive. Comparing growth along \(m=\ell^n\) gives \(\dim\operatorname{Supp} M^i\leq2(g-i)\).

Let \(F\) be a constructible sheaf and let \(s=\dim\operatorname{Supp} F\). Then \(F[s]\in{}^{p}D^{\leq0}(A,\Lambda)\), so
\(G=\mathbb{D}(F[s])\) lies in \({}^{p}D^{\geq0}(A,\Lambda)\), and its finite filtration by perverse truncations has
graded pieces \(\mathbb{D} P_j[-j]\) with \(P_j={}^{p}\mathrm{H}^{-j}(F[s])\in\operatorname{Perv}(A,\Lambda)\) and \(j\geq0\). The functor
\(\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A\) is exact, being a composition of derived functors, and \(D^{\geq0}(S)\) is stable under
extensions. Since \(\mathrm{H}^{i}(A,[\ell^{n}]^{*}F[s])=\mathrm{H}^{i+s}(A,[\ell^{n}]^{*}F)\),
Lemma~\ref{lem:tower-vanishing} applied to \(F[s]\) shows that both statements follow once
\(\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(\mathbb{D} P)\in D^{\geq0}(S)\) for every \(P\in\operatorname{Perv}(A,\Lambda)\).

Let  \(K=\widehat{\operatorname{FM}}_A(\mathbb{D} P)\) and \(\mathcal{K}'=R\operatorname{Hom}_S(K,S)\). By
\ref{item:M2} for the perverse sheaf \(\mathbb{D} P\), the complexes \(K\) and \(\mathcal{K}'\) are perfect and belong to
\(D^{[-g,g]}(S)\). Thus
\[
  \mathcal{K}'\otimes^{L}_{S}S_m\ \simeq\ R\operatorname{Hom}_{S_m}\bigl(K\otimes^{L}_{S}S_m,\,S_m\bigr)
  \ \simeq\ R\operatorname{Hom}_{S_m}\bigl(R\Gamma(A,[m]^{*}\mathbb{D} P),\,S_m\bigr)
\]
by \ref{item:M1}.


The ring \(S_m=S/I_m\simeq\mathbb{F}_{\ell}[x_1,\dots,x_{2g}]/(x_1^m,\dots,x_{2g}^m)\) is a local complete
intersection of dimension \(0\), hence Gorenstein. Its dualizing module
\(\omega_{S_m/\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}(S_m,\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\) is free of rank one
\cite[Tag~\href{https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0DWL}{0DWL}]{stacks-project}.
Choosing an isomorphism \(\omega_{S_m/\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}\simeq S_m\), adjunction gives the Artinian Gorenstein
duality isomorphism \(\operatorname{Hom}_{S_m}(N,S_m)\simeq\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{F}_{\ell}}(N,\mathbb{F}_{\ell})\), functorially in the \(S_m\)-module
\(N\). In particular, \(\operatorname{Hom}_{S_m}(-,S_m)\) is exact.

Since the residue field of \(S\) is \(\mathbb{F}_{\ell}\), a finite \(S\)-module annihilated by a power of \(\mathfrak{m}\)

\label{lem:special-generic-codim}
Let \(\mathcal{O}\) be a complete discrete valuation ring with uniformizer \(\varpi\), and let
\[
  R=\mathcal{O}[[x_1,\ldots,x_d]],\qquad \overline R=R/\varpi R.
\]
Let \(K\in D^b_{\mathrm{coh}}(R)\), fix \(i\in\mathbb{Z}\), and suppose that
\[
  \operatorname{codim}_{\operatorname{Spec}\overline R}\operatorname{Supp}
  \mathrm{H}^i\bigl(K\otimes_R^L\overline R\bigr)\ \geq\ c.
\]
Then
\[
  \operatorname{codim}_{\operatorname{Spec} R}\operatorname{Supp}\mathrm{H}^i(K)\ \geq\ c
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \operatorname{codim}_{\operatorname{Spec} R[1/\varpi]}\operatorname{Supp}\mathrm{H}^i\bigl(K[1/\varpi]\bigr)\ \geq\ c.
\]
Moreover, every component of \(\operatorname{Supp}\mathrm{H}^i(K)\) contained in the special fibre
\(\operatorname{Spec}\overline R\) has codimension at least \(c+1\) in \(\operatorname{Spec} R\).
<!-- end lem-9 -->
<!-- proof-18: body_md -->
Let \(M=\mathrm{H}^i(K)\). The reduction triangle gives an injection
\[
  M/\varpi M\ \lhook\joinrel\longrightarrow\
  \mathrm{H}^i\bigl(K\otimes_R^L\overline R\bigr).
\]
Let \(\mathfrak p\in\operatorname{Supp} M\). If \(\varpi\in\mathfrak p\), then Nakayama's lemma gives
\((M/\varpi M)_{\mathfrak p}\neq0\), and hence
\[
  \operatorname{ht}_{\overline R}(\mathfrak p/\varpi)
  =\operatorname{ht}_R(\mathfrak p)-1\ \geq\ c.
\]
Thus \(\operatorname{ht}_R(\mathfrak p)\geq c+1\).

Suppose that \(\varpi\notin\mathfrak p\), and let \(\mathfrak q\) be minimal over
\(\mathfrak p+(\varpi)\). Then \(M_{\mathfrak q}\neq0\), so Nakayama's lemma and the
injection show that \(\mathfrak q/\varpi\) belongs to the support on the special fibre. Since
\(R/\mathfrak p\) is a domain and the image of \(\varpi\) is nonzero, the principal ideal theorem and
catenarity of \(R\) give
\[
  \operatorname{ht}_R(\mathfrak q)=\operatorname{ht}_R(\mathfrak p)+1,
  \qquad
  \operatorname{ht}_{\overline R}(\mathfrak q/\varpi)
  =\operatorname{ht}_R(\mathfrak p).
\]
It follows that \(\operatorname{ht}_R(\mathfrak p)\geq c\). This proves the inequality over \(R\).
Localization at \(\varpi\) preserves the heights of primes not containing \(\varpi\), which proves the
inequality over \(R[1/\varpi]\).

\(N/T\) is torsion-free, and \((N/T)\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal{O}}E\simeq N\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal{O}}E\).
\end{enumerate}

(1) The triangle \(N\xrightarrow{\varpi}N\to N\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal{O}}\kappa\) gives the exact sequence of
perverse cohomology
\[
  0\to{}^{p}\!H^{-1}\bigl(N\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal{O}}\kappa\bigr)\to N\xrightarrow{\ \varpi\ }N
  \to{}^{p}\!H^{0}\bigl(N\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal{O}}\kappa\bigr)\to0.
\]
If \(\varpi\cdot\mathrm{id}_{N}\) is a monomorphism, the first term vanishes, so the reduction is
perverse in \(D^{b}_{c}(X,\mathcal{O})\) and equals the cokernel. The complex \(N\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal{O}}\kappa\) is
naturally an object of \(D^{b}_{c}(X,\kappa)\), and restriction of scalars along \(\mathcal{O}\to\kappa\) commutes
with \(i_{x}^{*}\) and \(i_{x}^{!}\) and preserves the vanishing of cohomology sheaves. Since the
perverse \(t\)-structures of \(D^{b}_{c}(X,\mathcal{O})\) and \(D^{b}_{c}(X,\kappa)\) are both defined by the vanishing of
\(\mathcal{H}^{n}i_{x}^{*}\) for \(n>-\dim\overline{\{x\}}\) and of \(\mathcal{H}^{n}i_{x}^{!}\) for
\(n<-\dim\overline{\{x\}}\) at the points \(x\) of \(X\) \cite[\S2.1]{Jut09}, it follows that
\(N\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal{O}}\kappa\in\operatorname{Perv}(X,\kappa)\).

(2) The category \(\operatorname{Perv}(X,\mathcal{O})\) is noetherian \cite[4.0(b)]{BBDG18}, so the ascending chain
\(K_{\nu}:=\ker(\varpi^{\nu}\cdot\mathrm{id}_{N})\) stabilizes. For large \(\nu\), \(T:=K_{\nu}\) contains
every torsion subobject, the kernel of \(\varpi\cdot\mathrm{id}_{N/T}\) is \(K_{\nu+1}/K_{\nu}=0\), and
\(T\otimes^{L}_{\mathcal{O}}E=0\).
