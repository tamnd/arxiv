We assume that  $v=(|v|,0)$. In this case,   $\hat D(i\mu+0)=k^2+(i|v|k_1+(i\mu+0))^2$. Denote
$K_v(\mu)=\{k\in\mathbb{R}^2: (k_1-\mu |v|\gamma^2)^2+k_2^2\gamma^2=\mu^2\gamma^4\}$, $\gamma=1/\sqrt{1-v^2}$. By the Plemelj formula,
\begin{equation}\label{PF}

\end{equation}
(see, for example, \cite[Lemma 15.3]{IKV2006}).
Hence, it suffices to show that  $\hat\rho(k) \not{\!\!\equiv}\, 0$ on $K_v(\mu)$ for $\mu\ne 0$.
Assume, on the contrary, $\hat\rho(k) \equiv 0$ on $K_v(\mu)$.
Then  $\hat\rho(k) \equiv 0$ in the ring  $ |\mu|(1-|v|)\gamma^2 \le |k|\le |\mu|(1+|v|)\gamma^2$  by  (\ref{rosym}). Hence,
$\hat\rho(k) \equiv 0$ in $\mathbb{C}^2$, due to the analyticity of $\hat\rho(k)$,
in contradiction  to the last condition in \eqref{rosym}.
