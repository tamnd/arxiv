\label{def:coupled-step}
Fix a descent profile $\mathcal D:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)$.
At center $z_t\in K_t$, a coupled step returns an upper certificate
$U_{t+1}\le U_t$, a valid affine cut
$a_t(u)=b_t+\left\langle g_t,u\right\rangle$, cut-normal norm
$G_t=\left\lVert g_t\right\rVert_{s^\ast}$, descent inexactness $\eta_t\ge0$, and cut
inexactness $\xi_t\ge0$, such that
\begin{align}
  U_{t+1}
  &\le U_t-\mathcal D(G_t)+\eta_t,
  \label{eq:coupled-descent}\\
  a_t(z_t)-(U_{t+1}-\Gamma)
  &\ge\Gamma+\mathcal D(G_t)-\xi_t.
  \label{eq:coupled-cut}
\end{align}
The movement norm is $\ell_s$ and the cut normal is measured in
$\ell_{s^\ast}$.
