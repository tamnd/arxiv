<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{thm:main}
Fix $\beta > 0$, and a non-negative $f_0 = f_0(z)$, with $\int_{\mathbb{R}^6} f_0(z)\, dz = 1$. Suppose that the solution $f(t,z)$ of the Boltzmann equation \cref{eq:boltzmann} exists on the time interval $[0, t_{\mathrm{fin}}]$, and that
\[
\|e^{2\beta |v|^2} f(t, x, v)\|_{L^\infty_{x,v}} \leq A < \infty, \quad \text{for all } t \in [0, t_{\mathrm{fin}}]
\]
(with no size condition on $t_{\mathrm{fin}}$).

Suppose also that $f_0$ satisfies
\[
\|f_0\|_{\mathrm{Bol}^\beta} + \|\nabla_x f_0\|_{\mathrm{Bol}^{2\beta}} \leq B_0 < \infty.
\]

Consider the 3 dimensional hard sphere system of radius $\varepsilon$ particles, as in 2.1, 2.2, with random initial datum $\mathbf{Z}^0$, given by the grand canonical ensemble in 2.2, generated by $f_0$, under the Boltzmann--Grad scaling \cref{eq:BG_scaling}. Let $\varepsilon$ be small, depending on $(t_{\mathrm{fin}}, \beta, A, B_0)$. Then, uniformly in $t \in [0, t_{\mathrm{fin}}]$ and in $1 \leq s \leq \log 1/\varepsilon$, the $s$-particle correlation functions $f_s(t)$, defined in 2.2, satisfy
\[
\biggl\|f_s(t, \mathbf{Z}_s) - \prod_{j=1}^s f(t, z_j)\, \mathbf{1}_\mathcal{D}(\mathbf{Z}_s)\biggr\|_{L^1(\mathbb{R}^{6s})} \leq \varepsilon^\theta,
\]
where $\theta$ is an absolute constant. Moreover, propagation of chaos holds in the full interval $[0, t_{\mathrm{fin}}]$.
<!-- end theorem-1 -->
<!-- corollary-1: body_md -->
\label{cor:BG}
Under the hypotheses of \cref{thm:main}, we have
\[
\|f_1(t, x, v) - f(t, (x,v))\, \chi_\mathcal{D}(x,v)\|_{L^1(\mathbb{R}^6)} \leq \varepsilon^\theta, \quad \text{for } t \in [0, t_{\mathrm{fin}}],
\]
where $f_1$ is the 1-particle correlation function generated by $f_0$, and $f(t, (x,v))$ is the solution of the Boltzmann equation with initial datum $f_0$.
<!-- end corollary-1 -->
<!-- remark-1: body_md -->
1.\ The Maxwellian (Gaussian) decay of $f(t,z)$, $z = (x,v)$, in $v$ is standard for solutions of the Boltzmann equation. The required integrability in $x$ for $f_0$ is at the level of $L^1_x$, which is the minimal requirement to define and normalize the ensemble.

2.\ For $s = 1$, it is conjectured that the minimal value of $\theta$ should be $1^-$.
The limiting (in $\varepsilon$) value of $\mathbb{E}(N) \cdot \varepsilon^2$ in \cref{eq:BG_scaling} can be replaced by any $\alpha > 0$.

3.\ The corresponding result was proved in \cite{DHM1} for $\mathbb{R}^d$ for any $d \geq 2$, with no change in the proofs.

4.\ The corresponding result for $\mathbb{T}^d$, $d = 2, 3$ was proved in \cite{DHM2}.
<!-- end remark-1 -->
<!-- theorem-2: body_md -->
\label{thm:wave}
Given any interval $[0, \tau^*]$ on which the solution $f(\tau, k)$ to the wave kinetic equation exists, we have
\[
\mathbb{E}\bigl(|\hat{u}(\tau \cdot T_{\mathrm{kin}}, k)|^2\bigr) = f(\tau, k) + O(L^{-\nu}),
\]
$\nu > 0$, on the full interval $[0, \tau^*]$, and ``propagation of chaos'' holds.
