<!-- ques-1: body_md -->
Is there an explicit recursive formula for the Poincar\'e and Hodge-Deligne polynomials of $\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{1,n}$?
<!-- end ques-1 -->
<!-- theo-1: body_md -->
Let $g\ge 1$ and fix a motivic invariant $\mathbf{H}$ satisfying the blowup formula \eqref{12}. 
Let $\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{g,r|s}=\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{g,(1^r,(0^+)^s)}$ be the moduli space of stable curves of genus $g$ with $r$ points of weight 1 and $s$ points of weight $0^+$. Let $\mathbf{H}_{r|s}:=\mathbf{H}_{\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{g,r|s}}$. Then for $r,s\ge 0$ with $r+s>0$ and $n>0$, the following hold:
   \begin{equation}\label{32} \mathbf{H}_{r|s} = \mathbf{H}_{0|r+s} + \sum_{j=1}^{\min(r,r+s-2)} \sum_{k=0}^{r+s-j-2} \binom{r+s-j}{k}\, \mathbf{H}_{j|k}  \cdot (\mathbf{H}_{\mathbb{P}^{r+s-j-k-1}}-1),\end{equation}
   \begin{equation}\label{33} \mathbf{H}_{\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{g,n}}= \mathbf{H}_{n|0} = \mathbf{H}_{0|n} + \sum_{j=1}^{n-2} \sum_{k=0}^{n-j-2} \binom{n-j}{k}\, \mathbf{H}_{j|k} \cdot (\mathbf{H}_{\mathbb{P}^{n-j-k-1}}-1).\end{equation}
All $\mathbf{H}_{r|s}$ are recursively determined by \eqref{32} if we know $\mathbf{H}_{0|n}=\mathbf{H}_{\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{g,(0^+)^n}}$ for all $n$.
<!-- end theo-1 -->
<!-- theo-2: body_md -->
The Hodge-Deligne polynomial $\mathbf{H}_{0|n}$ of $\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{1,(0^+)^n}=\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{1,0|n}$ is 
\begin{equation}\label{38}
\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor} \binom{n-1}{2j} (uv+1)^{n-1-2j} \left( \alpha_{jj}(uv)^{j+1} - \sum_{i=0}^{j-1} \alpha_{ij}(uv)^i\right),
\end{equation}
\[ +\frac{(uv+1)^{n-1}+(uv-1)^{n-1}}{2} + \gamma_{n,2} \frac{(uv+1)^{n-2}+(uv-1)^{n-2}}{2} + \sum_{j=3}^n \gamma_{n,j} (uv-1)^{n-j} \]
\[-\sum_{j=0}^{\lfloor\frac{n-1}{2}\rfloor} \binom{n-1}{2j} (uv+1)^{n-1-2j}  \sum_{i=0}^{j-1} \alpha_{ij}\beta_{2j-2i+2}(uv)^i(u^{2j-2i+1}+v^{2j-2i+1}).  \]
The Hodge-Deligne polynomial $\mathbf{H}_{n|0}$ of $\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{1,n}=\overline{{\mathcal M}}_{1,n|0}$ can be computed by the recursive formula \eqref{33} together with \eqref{32} and \eqref{38}.
