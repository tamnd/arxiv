<!-- lemma-1: body_md -->
If ${n\geq 1}$, $R$ is an algebra over a ring $F$ with $1/n!$, ${I\lhd R}$ and 
in the algebra $R/I$ there are no non-zero nilpotent (solvable) ideals of 
index (degree) $k$ for some ${k\geq n}$ (${2^k\geq n}$), then 
${(I)D\subseteq I}$ for all ${D\in \operatorname{Der}_{a, n}(R)}$.
<!-- end lemma-1 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
Due to ${w(x_1, \ldots, x_n, x_{n+1} D, \ldots, x_{n+l} D) D^n\equiv 
n! w(x_1 D, \ldots, x_{n+l} D)\equiv 0\pod I}$ for any\linebreak ${x_i\in I}$, 
${l\geq 0}$ and regular non-associative words 
${w\in F\langle X\rangle}$ of length ${n+l}$, 
\[
((I+(I)D)/I)^k\ =\ ((I+(I)D)/I)^{(k)}\ =\ \{0\}\,,\quad (I)D\subseteq I\,.
\]
<!-- end proof-1 -->

$\operatorname{Der}_{a, bd}(R)$.
