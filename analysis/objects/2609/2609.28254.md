<!-- ddd-1: body_md -->
We define the bootstrap class ${\mathcal{B}}$ as the localizing subcategory of $  \mathrm{KK}$ generated by ${\mathbf{1}}$.
<!-- end ddd-1 -->
<!-- theorem-1: body_md -->
\label{kopgwgrewfrw}\mbox{}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item  \label{kogwperfwf} For $A$ in ${\mathcal{B}}$ and $B$ in $\mathrm{KK}$, the functor $K$ induces an equivalence
$${\mathtt{map}}_{\mathrm{KK}}(A,B)\simeq {\mathtt{map}}_{{\mathbf{Mod}}_{KU}(\mathbf{Sp})}(K(A),K(B))$$ in ${\mathbf{Mod}}_{KU}(\mathbf{Sp})$.
\item \label{kgowperwrfwerfwef} The functor $K$ restricts to an equivalence $K_{|{\mathcal{B}}}:{\mathcal{B}}\stackrel{\simeq}{\to} {\mathbf{Mod}}_{KU}(\mathbf{Sp})$.

\item \label{kgowperwrfwerfwee33f}  The functor $K$ is the right adjoint of a right Bousfield localization
$$b:{\mathbf{Mod}}_{KU}(\mathbf{Sp})\rightleftarrows \mathrm{KK}:K\ .$$
\item \label{kogwperfwf1}  For $A$ in ${\mathcal{B}}$ and $B$ in $\mathrm{KK}$, the canonical morphism $$K(A)\otimes_{KU} K( B) \to K(A\otimes B)$$ is an equivalence.
\end{enumerate}
<!-- end theorem-1 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
Since $K$ is corepresented, it preserves limits and is a right adjoint.
 Since ${\mathbf{1}}$ is a compact object in $\mathrm{KK}$ (see \cite[Section 7]{Bunke:2026aa}), the functor $K$ preserves colimits. For any $B$ in $\mathrm{KK}$, the functor $K$ induces the identity
 $$K(B)\simeq {\mathtt{map}}_{\mathrm{KK}}({\mathbf{1}},B)\stackrel{\simeq}{\to} {\mathtt{map}}_{{\mathbf{Mod}}_{KU}(\mathbf{Sp})}(KU,K(B))\simeq K(B)\ .$$
This proves Assertion \ref{kogwperfwf} for $A={\mathbf{1}}$. The full assertion follows by extension along colimits.
This shows, in particular, that $K_{|{\mathcal{B}}}$ is fully faithful. Since $K({\mathbf{1}})\simeq KU$ and $K$ preserves colimits,
we conclude that $K_{|{\mathcal{B}}}$ is essentially surjective, which implies Assertion \ref{kgowperwrfwerfwef}.
     Furthermore, it follows from \ref{kogwperfwf} that the left adjoint $b$ of $K$ maps $KU$ to ${\mathbf{1}}$ and therefore maps ${\mathbf{Mod}}_{KU}(\mathbf{Sp})$ fully faithfully into ${\mathcal{B}}$. This establishes Assertion \ref{kgowperwrfwerfwee33f}.
     Finally, Assertion \ref{kogwperfwf1} is evident for $A={\mathbf{1}}$, and the general case again follows by extension along colimits.
<!-- end proof-1 -->
<!-- rem-1: body_md -->
\label{bobwaggqqihqffasf}
 In advantage of the conventions for $KK$-theory used in the present note, compared with the ones from  \cite{KKG}, is that the intersection of the  bootstrap class with the $\aleph_{1}$-compact objects coincides with the classical bootstrap class. To explain this, let us denote the $KK$-theory as considered in \cite{KKG} by   $\overline{\mathrm{KK}}:= {\mathtt{Ind}}(\mathrm{KK}_{\mathrm{sep}})$.  {W}e consider the functor
  	\[
