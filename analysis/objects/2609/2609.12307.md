<!-- head-1: body_md -->
\cite[Sec 10.1]{Higham2002}\cite{Demmel_Cholesky_89,Wilkinson_Cholesky_68}. Suppose the unblocked Cholesky factorization
of $A$ with machine precision $\epsilon$
runs to completion, yielding a triangular
factor $\hat{L}$, and a computed solution $\hat{x}$
of $Ax=b$.
Let $E = \hat{L}\hat{L}^T - A$, and let the
diagonal matrix $D$ satisfy $\ensuremath{{i{D}}(i,#4)} = \ensuremath{{i{A}}(i,#4)}^{1/2}$.
Then
the results satisfy the ``C'' grade error bounds:
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:CholBndC}
\|E\|_2 = O(n \epsilon) \|A\|_2
\hspace{.3in} {\rm and} \hspace{.3in}
\frac{\|x - \hat{x}\|_2}{\|x\|_2} =O(n^2 \epsilon)  \kappa(A)
\end{equation}
as well as the stronger ``A'' grade error bounds:
\begin{equation}\label{eqn:CholBndA}
|\ensuremath{{i{E}}(j,#4)}| = O(n \epsilon) (\ensuremath{{i{A}}(i,#4)} \ensuremath{{j{A}}(j,#4)})^{1/2}
\hspace{.3in} {\rm and} \hspace{.3in}
\frac{\|D(x - \hat{x})\|_2}{\|Dx\|_2} =O(n^2 \epsilon)  \kappa(H)
\end{equation}
<!-- end head-1 -->
<!-- head-2: body_md -->
If both \trsm and \syrk get either ``A'' or ``B'' grades,
then the output of Algorithm~1 satisfies bound~(\ref{eqn:CholBndA}).
If \trsm and \syrk only get (at least) a ``C'' grade,
then the output of Algorithm~1 only satisfies
bound~(\ref{eqn:CholBndC}).

<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
To simplify the error bounds,
we use $f(n)$ to denote any positive function bounded by a polynomial in $n$, since this is all we need
in an error bound to determine a grade.
                                                       
We use induction based on
the following block factorization:
\[
\aligned
A & = \begin{bmatrix} \ensuremath{{1{A}}_{1,#4}} & \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{1,#4}}^T \\ \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{1,#4}} & \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}}
\end{bmatrix} = 
\begin{bmatrix}
    \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}}\ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}}^T & \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{1,#4}}^T \\ \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{1,#4}} & \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}}
\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}
    \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}} & 0 \\ \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}} & I
\end{bmatrix} \cdot
\begin{bmatrix}
    \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}}^T & \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}}^T \\ 0 & \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}} - \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}} \cdot \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}}^T
\end{bmatrix} \\
& =
\begin{bmatrix}
    \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}} & 0 \\ \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}} & I
\end{bmatrix} \cdot
\begin{bmatrix}
    \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}}^T & \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}}^T \\ 0 &  \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{2,#4}} \cdot \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{2,#4}}^T
\end{bmatrix} =
\begin{bmatrix}
    \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}} & 0 \\ \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}} & \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{2,#4}}
\end{bmatrix} \cdot
\begin{bmatrix}
    \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}}^T & \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}}^T \\ 0 &  \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{2,#4}}^T
\end{bmatrix}
\endaligned
\]
where 
$\ensuremath{{1{A}}_{1,#4}}$ is $b \times b$ and
$\ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}} = \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{1,#4}} \cdot \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}}^{-T}$.
$\ensuremath{{1{A}}_{1,#4}} = \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}} \cdot \ensuremath{{1{L}}_{1,#4}}^T$ is
factored in line 2 of Algorithm~\ref{alg:Cholesky},
$\ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}}$ is computed in line~4 and
$\ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}} - \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}} \cdot \ensuremath{{2{L}}_{1,#4}}^T$ in line~5.

Denote computed quantities by $\matblock{L}[\hat]{i}{j}$
in lines 2 and 4, and
$\matblock{A}[\hat]{2}{2}$ in line 5.
Denote quantities that we need to bound by
\[
\aligned
\ensuremath{{1{E}}_{1,#4}} & = \ensuremath{{1{A}}_{1,#4}} - \matblock{L}[\hat]{1}{1} \cdot \matblock{L}[\hat]{1}{1}^T \\
\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{1,#4}} & = \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{1,#4}} - \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1} \cdot \matblock{L}[\hat]{1}{1}^T \\
\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}} & = \matblock{A}[\hat]{2}{2} - (\ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}} - \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1} \cdot \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}^T) \\
\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}' & = \matblock{A}[\hat]{2}{2} - \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{2} \cdot \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{2}^T
\endaligned
\]
Since $\matblock{L}[\hat]{1}{1}$ is computed using
conventional floating point without BLAS,
$\ensuremath{{1{E}}_{1,#4}}$ is bounded by
\begin{equation}\label{eqn_E11bnd}
|\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{1{E}}_{1,#4}}}}(j,#4)}| \leq f(n) \epsilon (\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{1{A}}_{1,#4}}}}(i,#4)}\ensuremath{{j{\ensuremath{{1{A}}_{1,#4}}}}(j,#4)})^{1/2}
\end{equation}
based on (\ref{eqn:CholBndA}).
This is also the base case of the induction.

First, we assume that $\syrk$ and $\trsm$ both
get an ``A'' or ``B'' grade. We note that for
Cholesky to complete, 
$\|\matentry{L}[\hat]{i}{1:j}\|_2^2 \leq \ensuremath{{i{A}}(i,#4)}$ up to roundoff.
Based on (\ref{eq:trsm-mixed})
\[
|\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{1,#4}}}}(j,#4)}| \leq f(n) \epsilon \| \ensuremath{{i{\matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}}}(:,#4)} \| \cdot \| \ensuremath{{:{\matblock{L}[\hat]{1}{1}^T}}(j,#4)} \|
= f(n) \epsilon \| \ensuremath{{i{\matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}}}(:,#4)} \|_2 \cdot \| \ensuremath{{j{\matblock{L}[\hat]{1}{1}}}(:,#4)} \|_2 \leq f(n) \epsilon (\ensuremath{{i{A}}(i,#4)} \ensuremath{{j{A}}(j,#4)})^{1/2}
\]
Next,
\[
\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}'' \equiv \ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}} - \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}\cdot \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}^T -
\matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{2} \cdot \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{2}^T = \ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}' - \ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}
\]
so we can bound
\begin{equation*}
\aligned
|\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}}}(j,#4)}| & \leq f(n) \epsilon (|\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}}}}(j,#4)}| +
\|\ensuremath{{i{\matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}}}(:,#4)}\|_2 \cdot \|\ensuremath{{:{\matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}^T}}(j,#4)}\|_2)
\\
& = f(n) \epsilon (|\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}}}}(j,#4)}| +
\|\ensuremath{{i{\matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}}}(:,#4)}\|_2 \cdot \|\ensuremath{{j{\matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1}}}(:,#4)}\|_2) \\
& \leq f(n) \epsilon (\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}}}}(i,#4)} \ensuremath{{j{\ensuremath{{2{A}}_{2,#4}}}}(j,#4)})^{1/2} \\
& \leq f(n) \epsilon (\ensuremath{{i+b{A}}(i+b,#4)} \ensuremath{{j+b{A}}(j+b,#4)})^{1/2}
\endaligned
\end{equation*}
and, by induction,
\[
|\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}'}}(j,#4)}| \leq f(n) \epsilon (\ensuremath{{i{\matblock{A}[\hat]{2}{2}}}(i,#4)}
\ensuremath{{j{\matblock{A}[\hat]{2}{2}}}(j,#4)})^{1/2})
\leq f(n) \epsilon (\ensuremath{{i+b{A}}(i+b,#4)} \ensuremath{{j+b{A}}(j+b,#4)})^{1/2})
\]
so adding the bounds on $|\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}|$ and $|\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}'|$ yields
\[
|\ensuremath{{i{\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}''}}(j,#4)}| \leq f(n) \epsilon (\ensuremath{{i+b{A}}(i+b,#4)} \ensuremath{{j+b{A}}(j+b,#4)})^{1/2}
\]
Altogether, we have the desired error bound in (\ref{eqn:CholBndA}) 
for $E = A - \hat{L}\cdot \hat{L}^T$:
\[
|\ensuremath{{i{E}}(j,#4)}| \leq
f(n) \epsilon (\ensuremath{{i{A}}(i,#4)}\ensuremath{{j{A}}(j,#4)})^{1/2} .
\]
The bound for $x - \hat{x}$ in (\ref{eqn:CholBndA})
follows from this 
\cite[Sec 10.1]{Higham2002}\cite{Demmel_Cholesky_89,Wilkinson_Cholesky_68}.

Second, we assume that $\syrk$ and $\trsm$ only get a
``C'' grade. First, we note that
\[
\| \ensuremath{{1{E}}_{1,#4}} \|_2 \leq f(n) \epsilon \|\ensuremath{{1{A}}_{1,#4}}\|_2 \leq f(n) \epsilon \|A\|_2
\]
follows directly from (\ref{eqn_E11bnd}).
Similarly,
\[
\| \ensuremath{{2{E}}_{1,#4}} \|_2 \leq
f(n) \epsilon \| \matblock{L}[\hat]{2}{1} \|_2 \cdot
\| \matblock{L}[\hat]{1}{1} \|_2
\leq f(n) \epsilon 
\| A \|_2^{1/2} \cdot 
\| A\|_2^{1/2} =
f(n) \epsilon \|A\|_2
\]
follows from the last row of Table~1
                          
and $\|\ensuremath{{i{L}}_{j,#4}}\|_2 \leq \|L\|_2 = \|A\|_2^{1/2}$
(the difference between $\|L\|_2$ and $\|\hat{L}\|_2$
is small and can be absorbed in the $f(n)$ factor).
The bound $\|\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}\|_2 \leq f(n) \epsilon \|L\|_2^2
= f(n) \epsilon \|A\|_2$ similarly follows from the
definition of a ``C'' grade for $\gemm$ and
analogously $\syrk$. The bound
$\|\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}'\|_2 \leq f(n) \epsilon \| \matblock{A}[\hat]{2}{2} \|_2
\leq f(n) \epsilon \|A\|_2$ holds by induction.
Adding the bounds on $\|\ensuremath{{1{E}}_{1,#4}}\|_2$, $\|\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{1,#4}}\|_2$,
$\|\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}\|_2$ and $\|\ensuremath{{2{E}}_{2,#4}}'\|_2$ yields the
desired  bound 
\[
\|A - \hat{L} \hat{L}^T \|_2 \leq f(n) \epsilon \|A\|_2
\]
needed for a ``C'' grade.
