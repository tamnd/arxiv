<!-- lemma-1: body_md -->
(\cite{[Duff]})\label{hofflemma} 
Let $F(t)\geq0$  and $F(t)\in L^{\frac{1}{p}}(0,T)$. Suppose that  $G(t)\geq0$  and 
\begin{equation}\label{2.1} 
\frac{d}{dt}F(t)+G(t)\leq CF(t)^{\frac{q}{p}}. 
\end{equation} 
Then, for $q>p+1$, there holds $G(t)\in L^{\frac{1}{q}}(0,T)$.
<!-- end lemma-1 -->
<!-- proof-1: body_md -->
Rearranging the inequality \eqref{2.1}, we have 
\begin{equation} 
\frac{\frac{d}{dt}F(t)}{(F(t)+1)^{\frac{q-1}{p }}}+\frac{G(t)}{(F(t)+1)^{\frac{q-1}{p }}}\leq C[F(t)+1]^{\frac{1}{p}}. 
\end{equation} 
A simple  calculation  leads to $$\int_{0}^{T}\frac{G (t)}{(1+F(t))^{\frac{q-1}{p }}}dt<\infty.$$ 
We deduce from the H\"older inequality that 
$$\begin{aligned} 
\int_{0}^{T}G^{\frac{1}{q}}(t)dt=&\int_{0}^{T}(1+F(t))^{\frac{q-1}{pq}} 
\frac{G^{\frac{1}{q}}(t)}{(1+F(t))^{\frac{q-1}{pq}}}dt\\ 
\leq& \Big[\int_{0}^{T}(1+F(t))^{\frac1p}dt \Big]^{\frac{q-1}{ q}}\Big[\int_{0}^{T}\frac{G (t)}{(1+F(t))^{\frac{q-1}{p }}}dt\Big]^{\frac1q}, 
\end{aligned}$$ 
where the H\"older inequality was used.

<!-- lemma-2: body_md -->
Let  $\frac{5}{2 (s+1)}<\alpha<\frac54$ and $f\in \dot{H}^{\alpha}\cap\dot{H}^{s\alpha}$. Then it is valid that

\frac52-2\alpha

\frac52-(s+1)\alpha

\end{aligned}\end{equation}

\frac52-2\alpha

Assume for a while we have proved that 
\begin{equation}\label{while} 
\int_{0}^{T}\|\Lambda^{k}u\| 
^{\frac{1}{k +1}}_{L^{\infty}}dt<\infty. 
\end{equation} 
(1) The interpolation inequality and Sobolev embedding theorem give 
$$\begin{aligned} 
\| u\|_{L^{q}}\leq \| u\|_{L^{6}}^{\frac6q}\| u\|^{1-\frac6q}_{L^{\infty}}\leq C\| \nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{\frac6q}\| u\|^{1-\frac6q}_{L^{\infty}}, ~6\leq q \leq\infty. 
\end{aligned}$$ 
After performing a time integration, one conclude by the H\"older inequality that 
$$\begin{aligned} 
\int_{0}^{T}\| u\|_{L^{q}}^{\frac{q}{q-3}}dt \leq C \Big(\int_{0}^{T}\|\nabla u\|_{L^{2}}^{2}dt\Big)^{\frac{3}{q-3}}\Big( \int_{0}^{T} \|u\|_{L^{\infty}}dt\Big)^{1-\frac{3}{q-3}}. 
\end{aligned}$$ 
As a result, by \eqref{while},  we get $  u   \in L^{\frac{q}{q-3}}(0,T;L^{q} (\mathbb{R}^{3})), ~6\leq q\leq\infty.$ 
 
(2) 
We derive from the interpolation inequality that 
$$\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}$$

\begin{equation}\begin{aligned}
