The discrete-time problem in Eq.~\eqref{eq:ocp_dis} provides a numerical transcription of the continuous-time problem in Eq.~\eqref{eq:ocp} for a sufficiently small discretization interval $\Delta t$. For state constraints, feasibility at the discrete nodes does not, in general, guarantee feasibility between nodes. A sufficient condition is that each constrained state component
$x_i(t)$ be monotonic over every interval $[t_k,t_{k+1}]$. In this case, its extrema occur at the interval endpoints, and satisfaction of the corresponding bounds at $t_k$ and $t_{k+1}$ implies satisfaction throughout the interval. When this condition does not hold, intersample constraint satisfaction must be
verified or enforced separately.
