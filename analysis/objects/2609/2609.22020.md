For clarity, before introducing our model for IOD, we report the example used by \citet{raissi2019physics} to introduce the PINN framework on Burgers' equation:
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
    \partial_t u + \mathcal{N}[u]&= 0, \\
    \partial_t u + u \partial_x u - \nu \, \partial_{xx} u &= 0;
\end{split}
\end{equation}
where $u(t,x)$ is the unknown solution and $\nu$ is a constant. Therefore, given a NN $u_\theta(t,x)$ that approximates $u(t,x)$, the physics-informed loss term is
\begin{equation}
    \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{PDE}}(\theta) = \frac
{1}{M} \sum_{j=1}^{M} \ell(\partial_t u_\theta(t_j, x_j) + u_\theta(t_j, x_j) \partial_x u_\theta(t_j, x_j) - \nu \partial_{xx} u_\theta (t_j, x_j), 0).
\end{equation}
