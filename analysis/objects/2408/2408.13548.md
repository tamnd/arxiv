Given two morphisms $l: A\to B$, $r: C\to D$ morphisms in $\mathcal{C}$. We write $l\Box r$ if for any commutative square given by the solid arrows
    $$\xymatrix{
  A \ar[d]_{l} \ar[r]^{f}
                & C \ar[d]^{r}  \\
  B \ar@{-->}[ur]^{t} \ar[r]_{g}
                & D             }$$
a morphism depicted by the diagonal dotted arrow exists such that both the triangles commute.

Given $l,r$ such that  $l\Box r$, we say that $l$ has the \emph{left lifting property} with respect to $r$ and $r$ has the \emph{right lifting property} with respect to $l$.

For a class $\mathcal{M}$ of morphisms in $\mathcal{C},$ denoted by $\mathcal{M}^{\Box}$ the class of morphisms $r$ in $\mathcal{C}$ having the right lifting property with respect to all morphisms $l\in \mathcal{M}.$
The class ${^{\Box}\mathcal{M}}$ is  defined dually.

\emph{\cite[Definition 4.1]{NP}}\label{df:cotorsion pair}
{\rm Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathbb{E}, \mathfrak{s})$ be an extriangulated category, and let $\mathcal{U}$, $\mathcal{V}$ $\subseteq$ $\mathcal{C}$ be a pair of full additive subcategories, closed
under isomorphisms and direct summands. The pair ($\mathcal{U}$, $\mathcal{V}$) is called a {\it cotorsion
pair} on $\mathcal{C}$ if it satisfies the following conditions:

(1) $\mathbb{E}(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{V})=0$;

(2) For any $C \in{\mathcal{C}}$, there exists a conflation $V^{C}\rightarrow U^{C}\rightarrow C$ satisfying
$U^{C}\in{\mathcal{U}}$ and $V^{C}\in{\mathcal{V}}$;

(3) For any $C \in{\mathcal{C}}$, there exists a conflation $C\rightarrow V_{C} \rightarrow U_{C}$ satisfying
$U_{C}\in{\mathcal{U}}$ and $V_{C}\in{\mathcal{V}}$.
}

\label{Thm1}
Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathbb{E}, \mathfrak{s})$ be an extriangulated category.
Then assignments
\begin{center}
$(\mathcal{U}, \mathcal{V})\mapsto (\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}, \mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V})$ $~~$ and $~~$ $(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{R})\mapsto ( \mathrm{Cone}(\mathcal{L}), \mathrm{CoCone}(\mathcal{R}))$
\end{center}
gives the mutually inverse bijections between the following classes:

{\rm (1)} Cotorsion pairs $(\mathcal{U},\mathcal{V})$ on $\mathcal{C}$;

{\rm (2)} Admissible weak factorization systems $(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{R}).$

<!-- proof-2: body_md -->
Assume that $(\mathcal{U},\mathcal{V})$ is a cotorsion pair on $\mathcal{C}$. We shall prove that every morphism $h:X\rightarrow Y$ factorizes as $h=gf$ with $f\in \mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$ and $g\in \mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}.$
Suppose first that $h$ is an inflation.
Then there is an $\mathbb{E}$-triangle $\xymatrix{X\ar[r]^{h}&Y\ar[r]^{y}&C\ar@{-->}[r]^{\delta}&}.$
Since $(\mathcal{U},\mathcal{V})$ is a cotorsion pair, there is an $\mathbb{E}$-triangle
$\xymatrix{V^{C}\ar[r]^{}&U^{C}\ar[r]^{}&C\ar@{-->}[r]^{}&}$ with $U^C\in\mathcal{U}$ and $V^C\in\mathcal{V}$.
By \cite[Proposition 3.15]{NP}, we obtain a commutative diagram of conflations
$$\scalebox{0.9}[0.9]{     \xymatrix{&V^{C}\ar[d]^{}\ar@{=}[r]&V^{C}\ar[d]_{}\\
X\ar[r]^{f}\ar@{=}[d]&Z\ar[r]^{}\ar[d]_{g}&U^{C}\ar[d]^{}&\\
X\ar[r]^{h}&Y\ar[r]&C.}}$$

\noindent The corresponding factorization of $h$ then appears in the leftmost square.
A dual argument applies if $h$ is a deflation.
If $h$ is an arbitrary morphism, we can factor it as
$$X\xrightarrow{\tiny\begin{pmatrix}1_X\\0\end{pmatrix}}X\oplus Y\xrightarrow{\tiny\begin{pmatrix}h&1_Y\end{pmatrix}}Y.$$
Note that $X\xrightarrow{\tiny\begin{pmatrix}1_X\\0\end{pmatrix}}X\oplus Y$ is a split inflation.
It follows that we can factor it as $\tiny\begin{pmatrix}1_X\\0\end{pmatrix}=g_{1}f_{1}$
\begin{align*}
\xymatrix{X\ar[dr]_{f_{1}}\ar[rr]^{\tiny\begin{pmatrix}1_X\\0\end{pmatrix}}& &X\oplus Y\\
&W\ar[ur]_{g_{1}}}
\end{align*}
with $f_{1}\in \mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$ and
$g_{1}\in \mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}$.
As $(h,1_{Y})$ is a deflation by \cite[Corollary 3.16]{NP}, $(h,1_{Y})\circ g_{1}$ is a deflation.
Hence we can factor it as $g_{2}f_{2}$ with $f_{2}\in\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$ and $g_{2}\in \mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}$ by above argument.
             
                                                                                            
                                                                                        
   
It follows that $h=(h, 1_Y){\tiny\begin{pmatrix}1_X\\0\end{pmatrix}}=(h, 1_Y)g_1f_1=g_{2}(f_{2}f_{1}).$
Note that $f_{2} f_{1}$ is an inflation and there is a conflation
$\mathrm{Cone}(f_{1})\rightarrow \mathrm{Cone}(f_{2}f_{1})\rightarrow \mathrm{Cone}(f_{2})$ by ${\rm (\mathrm{ET4})^{op}}$; also see \cite[Lemma 2.11]{HZZ1}.
Since $\mathcal{U}$ is closed under extensions,  it follows that $f_{2}f_{1}\in \mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$.
  Next, we shall prove that $\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}={^{\Box}\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}}$.
If $f\in \mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$ and $g\in \mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}$,
then $f\Box g$ by \cite[Lemma 2.13]{HZZ1}, which implies $\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}\subseteq{^{\Box}\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}}$.  If $h\in{^{\Box}\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}}$, then $h\Box g$ for any $g\in\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}$. By above argument, we have $h=g_1f_1$ with  $f_{1}\in\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$ and $g_{1}\in \mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}$. Since $h\Box g_1$, there exists a morphism $t: B\rightarrow C$ which makes the following diagram commutative

$$\xymatrix{
A\ar[d]_{h}\ar[r]^{f_1} & C\ar[d]^{g_1}\\
B\ar@{=}[r]\ar@{-->}[ur]^{t} & B.
}
$$
 Since $f_1=th$ is an inflation, one can get that $h$ is an inflation by Condition (WIC). It follows from Lemma \ref{lemma:WFS1} that ${\rm Cone}(h)\in {^\perp{\mathcal{V}}}$, hence $h\in \mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$ as $\mathcal{U}={^\perp\mathcal{V}}$, and ${^{\Box}\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}}\subseteq\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$. So we have $\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}={^{\Box}\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}}$, we can prove $\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}=\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}^\Box$ by a dual argument. Therefore,  $(\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U},\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V})$ is a weak factorization system. It is easy to check that $(\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U},\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V})$ is an admissible weak factorization system.

Conversely, let $(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{R})$ be an admissible weak factorization system and put $\mathcal{U}=\mathrm{Cone}(\mathcal{L})$ and $\mathcal{V}=\mathrm{CoCone}(\mathcal{R}).$
Since $\mathcal{L}$ and $\mathcal{R}$ are closed under retracts by Remark \ref{rm1}, both $\mathcal{U}$ and $\mathcal{V}$ are closed under direct summands.
By Lemma \ref{lemma:WFS1}, we have $\mathbb{E}(\mathcal{U},\mathcal{V})=0$ for each $\mathcal{U}\in\mathcal{U}$ and $V\in\mathcal{V}.$
In order to prove the existence of approximation sequences, consider $X\in \mathcal{C}$ and the following two factorizations
\begin{align*}
\xymatrix{X\ar[dr]_{i_{X}}\ar[rr]& &0\\
&V_{X}\ar[ur]}
\end{align*}
and
\begin{align*}
\xymatrix{0\ar[dr]_{}\ar[rr]& &X\\
&U^{X}\ar[ur]_{p^{X}}}
\end{align*}
with respect to $(\mathcal{L},\mathcal{R}).$
Thus there are conflations $X\rightarrow V_{X}\rightarrow U_{X}$ and $V^{X}\rightarrow U^{X}\rightarrow X$
with $V_{X}, V^{X}\in \mathcal{V}$ and $U_{X}, U^{X}\in \mathcal{U}.$
Hence $(\mathcal{U},\mathcal{V})$ is a cotorsion pair on $\mathcal{C}$.

\label{lem:CoCone}
Let $\mathcal{X}$ be a class of objects in $\mathcal{C}$.
Then the following are equivalent:

{\rm (1)} $\mathcal{X}$ is closed under cocones of deflations.

{\rm (2)} $\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{X}$ satisfies the left cancellation property.
<!-- end lem-3 -->
<!-- proof-3: body_md -->
$(1)\Rightarrow (2)$ Let $g:B\rightarrow C$ and $f:A\rightarrow B$ be morphisms in $\mathcal{C}$
such that $gf$ and $g$ are in $\mathrm{Infl} (\mathcal{X})$.
Since $\mathcal{C}$ satisfies  Condition (WIC), $f$ is an inflation.
By { \cite[Lemma 3.14]{NP}},  we have the following commutative diagram
\begin{center}
\scalebox{0.9}[0.98]{     \xymatrix{A\ar[r]^{f}\ar@{=}[d]&B\ar[r]^{}\ar[d]_{g}&\mathrm{Cone}(f)\ar[d]^{}&\\
A\ar[r]^{gf}&C\ar[r]^{}\ar[d]_{}&\mathrm{Cone}(gf)\ar[d]^{}&\\
&\mathrm{Cone}(g)\ar@{=}[r]&\mathrm{Cone}(g).}}
\end{center}
Since $\mathcal{X}$ is closed under cocones of deflations by the assumption, and both $\mathrm{Cone}(gf)$ and $\mathrm{Cone}(g)$ are in $\mathcal{X},$ it follows that $\mathrm{Cone}(f)\in \mathcal{X}$ as well,  so $f\in \mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{X}.$

$(2)\Rightarrow(1)$ Let $\xymatrix{X_{1}\ar[r]^{h}&X_{2}\ar[r]^{}&X_{3}\ar@{-->}[r]^{}&}$
be {an $\mathbb{E}$-triangle}  with $X_{2}$ and $X_{3}\in \mathcal{X}$.
Let $l$ be the zero morphism from $0$ to $X_{1}$.
Then $hl\in \mathrm{Infl}~(\mathcal{X})$, and so is $l$ as $h$ is in $\mathrm{Infl}~(\mathcal{X}).$
Thus $X_{1}\in \mathcal{X}.$

<!-- df-8: body_md -->
\cite[Definition 1.11]{DLLiang}
Two weak factorization systems $(\mathcal{L},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}})$ and
$(\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\mathcal{R})$ in $\mathcal{C}$ are called {\it compatible} if the following conditions hold:

$(\mathrm{CP1})$ $\widetilde{\mathcal{L}}\subseteq\mathcal{L}$ (or equivalent, $\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}\subseteq\mathcal{R}$);

$(\mathrm{CP2})$ given composable morphisms $\alpha$ and $\beta$ in  $\mathcal{R}$, if two of three morphisms $\alpha$, $\beta$ and $\beta\alpha$ are in $\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}$, then so is the third one;

$(\mathrm{CP3})$ given $l\in\widetilde{\mathcal{L}}$ and $r\in \mathcal{R}$,
if $rl\in \widetilde{\mathcal{L}}$, then $r\in \widetilde{\mathcal{R}}.$

Let $(\mathcal{L},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}})$ and
$(\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\mathcal{R})$ be two compatible weak factorization systems in $\mathcal{C}$.
We define
$$\mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}=\{\alpha~|~\alpha~ \text{can be decomposed as }\alpha=\widetilde{r} \widetilde{l}~\text{with}~ \widetilde{l}\in\widetilde{\mathcal{L}}~\text{and}~\widetilde{r}\in \widetilde{\mathcal{R}}\}.$$

<!-- lem-6: body_md -->
\label{lem:co}
Let $(\mathcal{L},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}})$ and
$(\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\mathcal{R})$ be two compatible admissible weak factorization systems in extriangulated category $(\mathcal{C}, \mathbb{E}, \mathfrak{s})$. If the composition $g=f h$ is in $\mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}$ with
$f\in \widetilde{\mathcal{R}}$, then $h\in \mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}.$
<!-- end lem-6 -->
<!-- proof-7: body_md -->
Since $(\mathcal{L},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}})$ is an admissible weak factorization system, we have $h=h_{2} h_{1}$ with $h_{1}\in \mathcal{L}$ and $h_{2}\in \widetilde{\mathcal{R}}.$
Then $g=fh_{2}h_{1}$ with $fh_{2}\in \widetilde{\mathcal{R}}$ by Remark \ref{rm1}.
If the conclusion holds for $h_1$, that is, $h_1\in \mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}$, then $h_1\in \mathcal{L}\bigcap \mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}$. It follows from \cite[Lemma 1.12]{DLLiang} that $h_1\in\widetilde{\mathcal{L}}$, so $h=h_2h_1\in \mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}$.  So we only deal with the special case that $h\in \mathcal{L}$.
Let $h:X\rightarrow Z$ and $f:Z\rightarrow Y$.
Since $g=f h\in \mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}$, by definition, there is a morphism $\widetilde{l}:X\rightarrow Z'$ in $\widetilde{\mathcal{L}}$ and a morphism $\widetilde{r}:Z'\rightarrow Y$ in $\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}$ such that the following diagram commutes:
$$\xymatrix{X\ar[r]^{h} \ar[d]_{\widetilde{l}} &Z\ar[d]^{f}\\
Z'\ar[r]^{\widetilde{r}} &Y
}$$
 Since $(\mathcal{L},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}})$
 is an admissible weak factorization system,  $f$ and $\widetilde{r}$ are deflations by definition. Thus we have the following commutative diagram:
$$\scalebox{0.92}[1.0]{     \xymatrix{&\mathrm{CoCone}(f)\ar[d]^{}\ar@{=}[r]&\mathrm{CoCone}(f)\ar[d]_{}\\
\mathrm{CoCone}(\widetilde{r})\ar[r]^{}\ar@{=}[d]&P\ar[r]^{\widetilde{f_{2}}}\ar[d]_{\widetilde{f_{1}}}&Z\ar[d]^{f}&\\
\mathrm{CoCone}(\widetilde{r})\ar[r]^{}&Z'\ar[r]^{\widetilde{r}}&Y}}$$
Then one has that $\widetilde{f_{1}}$ and $\widetilde{f_{2}}$ are in $\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}$.
Note that
$$\xymatrix{P\ar[r]^{\widetilde{f_{2}}} \ar[d]_{\widetilde{f_{1}}} &Z\ar[d]^{f}\\
Z'\ar[r]^{\widetilde{r}} &Y
}$$
is a weak pullback by the dual of \cite[Lemma 3.13]{NP}.
It follows that there is a morphism $\alpha:X\rightarrow P$ such that $\widetilde{f_{1}}\alpha=\widetilde{l}$ and $\widetilde{f_{2}}\alpha=h$.
Since $(\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\mathcal{R})$ is an admissible weak factorization system, there is a morphism $\widetilde{l'}:X\rightarrow P'$ in $\widetilde{\mathcal{L}}$ and a morphism $f':P'\rightarrow P$ in $\mathcal{R}$
\begin{align*}
\xymatrix{X\ar[dr]_{\widetilde{l'}}\ar[rr]^{\alpha}& &P\\
&P'\ar[ur]_{f'}}
\end{align*}
with $\alpha=f' \widetilde{l'}.$
Since $\widetilde{l}=\widetilde{f_{1}}\alpha=\widetilde{f_{1}}f'\widetilde{l'}$ in $\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},$ we have that $\widetilde{f_{1}}f'$ belongs to $\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}$ by condition (CP3).
It follows from condition $(\mathrm{CP2})$ that $f'\in \widetilde{\mathcal{R}}.$
Consequently, $h=\widetilde{f_{2}}f'\widetilde{l'}$ is in $\mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}$.

Two cotorsion pairs $(\mathcal{U},\widetilde{\mathcal{V}})$ and $(\widetilde{\mathcal{U}},\mathcal{V})$ are {\it compatible} if $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}\subseteq\mathcal{U}$ (or equivalently $\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}\subseteq\mathcal{V})$ and $\mathcal{U}\bigcap\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}=\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}\bigcap\mathcal{V}$.

<!-- thm-3: body_md -->
\label{Thm3}
Let $(\mathcal{C}, \mathbb{E}, \mathfrak{s})$ be an extriangulated category, and let $(\mathcal{U},\widetilde{\mathcal{V}})$ and $(\widetilde{\mathcal{U}},\mathcal{V})$ be two pairs of classes of objects in $\mathcal{C}.$
Then the following are equivalent:

{\rm (1)} $(\mathcal{U},\widetilde{\mathcal{V}})$ and $(\widetilde{\mathcal{U}},\mathcal{V})$ are compatible cotorsion pairs on $\mathcal{C}$.

{\rm (2)} $(\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U},\mathrm{Defl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{V}})$ and $(\mathrm{Infl}~ \widetilde{\mathcal{U}},\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V})$ are compatible  admissible weak factorization systems.
<!-- end thm-3 -->
<!-- proof-8: body_md -->
$(1)\Rightarrow (2)$ By Theorem \ref{Thm1}, $(\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U},\mathrm{Defl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{V}})$ and $(\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}},\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V})$ are two admissible weak factorization systems.
Now we show that they are compatible. By \cite[Theorem  3.9]{Z}
there is a thick class $\mathcal{W}$ of $\mathcal{C}$ such that $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}=\mathcal{U}\bigcap\mathcal{W}$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}=\mathcal{V}\bigcap\mathcal{W}$.
The (CP1) holds clearly.
Suppose that $f:X\rightarrow Y$ and $g:Y\rightarrow Z$ are in $\mathrm{Defl}~(\mathcal{V}).$
Then $g f$ is a deflation and then there exists a conflation
$$\mathrm{CoCone}(f)\rightarrow \mathrm{CoCone}(gf)\rightarrow \mathrm{CoCone}(g)$$
in $\mathcal{C}$ by \cite[Lemma 2.11]{HZZ1}.
Thus the condition (CP2) holds as $\mathcal{W}$ is a thick class.

To prove (CP3), let $h:A\rightarrow B$ be in $\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}$ and $f:B\rightarrow C$ be in $\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}$ such that $g=f h$ is in $\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}.$
Then $h$ and $g$ are inflations with $\mathrm{Cone}(h)$ and $\mathrm{Cone}(g)$ in $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}$.
Thus by \cite[Lemma 2.12]{HZZ1}, there is a conflation
$$\mathrm{CoCone}(f)\rightarrow \mathrm{Cone}(h)\rightarrow \mathrm{Cone}(g).$$
 Since $\mathcal{W}$ is a thick class, it follows that $\mathrm{CoCone}(f)$ belongs to $\mathcal{W}$.
Thus $\mathrm{CoCone}(f)$ is in $\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}.$

$(2)\Rightarrow(1)$ By Theorem \ref{Thm1}, $(\mathcal{U},\widetilde{\mathcal{V}})$ and $(\widetilde{\mathcal{U}},\mathcal{V})$ are two cotorsion pairs in $\mathcal{C}.$
If $X\in \widetilde{\mathcal{U}}$, then $0\rightarrow X$ is in $\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}$
and hence in $\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$, so $X\in \mathcal{U}$ which yields that $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}\subseteq\mathcal{U}.$
 Next we claim that $\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}\bigcap\mathcal{V}\subseteq\mathcal{U}\bigcap \widetilde{\mathcal{V}}.$
Take $M\in \widetilde{\mathcal{U}}\bigcap\mathcal{V}$.
Then $0\rightarrow M$ is in $\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}$ and $M\rightarrow 0$ is in $\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V}.$ Since $(\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U},\mathrm{Defl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{V}})$ and $(\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}},\mathrm{Defl}~\mathcal{V})$ are compatible weak factorization systems, it follows  from the Condition (CP3) that $M\rightarrow 0$ is in $\mathrm{Defl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}$ as $0\rightarrow 0$ is in $\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}.$
Hence $M\in \widetilde{\mathcal{V}},$ so $M\in \mathcal{U}\bigcap\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}.$

We put $\widetilde{\mathcal{L}}=\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}=\mathrm{Defl}~~\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}$.
To prove $\mathcal{U}\bigcap\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}\subseteq\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}\bigcap\mathcal{V},$
let $N$ be an object in $\mathcal{U}\bigcap\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}.$
Then $0\rightarrow N$ is in $\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$ and $N\rightarrow 0$ is in $\mathrm{Defl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}$.
Since $0\rightarrow 0$ is in $\mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}}$, it follows from  Lemma \ref{lem:co} that $0\rightarrow N$ is in $\mathcal{W}_{\widetilde{\mathcal{L}},\widetilde{\mathcal{R}}} \bigcap\mathrm{Infl}~\mathcal{U}$. Thus, by \cite[Lemma 1.12]{DLLiang}, $0\rightarrow N$ is in $\mathrm{Infl}~\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}$.
So $N\in \widetilde{\mathcal{U}}.$
On the other hand,  $N\in \widetilde{\mathcal{V}}=\mathcal{U}^{\perp}\subseteq\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}^{\perp}=\mathcal{V}.$
Therefore $\mathcal{U}\bigcap\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}\subseteq\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}\bigcap \mathcal{V}.$
We have proven that $\mathcal{U}\bigcap\widetilde{\mathcal{V}}=\widetilde{\mathcal{U}}\bigcap \mathcal{V}$, so $(\widetilde{\mathcal{U}},\mathcal{V})$ and $(\mathcal{U},\widetilde{\mathcal{V}})$ are compatible cotorsion pairs.
