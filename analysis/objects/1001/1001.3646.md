\label{lemme terme non-oscillant en g}
 
The $\ensuremath{1/x^N}$ order in the AE of $\ln\ensuremath{\operatorname{det}_{}\ensuremath{\left[I+V\right]}}$ contains the term
 
 
 
\begin{equation}
 
\ensuremath{\sum\limits_{\sigma=\pm}^{}}  {\ensuremath{ 
    \mathchoice 
    {\dfrac{1}{N}}
    {\dfrac{1}{N}}
    {\frac{1}{N}}
    {\frac{1}{N}}
}} \ensuremath{\left({\ensuremath{ 
    \mathchoice 
    {\dfrac{ i g'_{\sigma} }{ p'_{\sigma} }}
    {\dfrac{ i g'_{\sigma} }{ p'_{\sigma} }}
    {\frac{ i g'_{\sigma} }{ p'_{\sigma} }}
    {\frac{ i g'_{\sigma} }{ p'_{\sigma} }}
}}\right)}^N  \nu^2_{\sigma}  \; .
 
\label{expression term non-oscillant en g a la N}
\end{equation}
 
 
 
This is the highest possible power of $g$ that can appear in the non-oscillating part of the $x^{-N}$ order in the AE of $\ln\ensuremath{\operatorname{det}_{}\ensuremath{\left[I+V\right]}}$ \eqref{Asymptotic expansion log Fredholm}.

\begin{equation}

    \mathchoice
