w=\frac{m}{\rho}+\frac{\rho^{\theta}}{\theta}=v+\frac{\rho^{\theta}}{\theta},
\quad{z}=\frac{m}{\rho}-\frac{\rho^{\theta}}{\theta}
=v-\frac{\rho^{\theta}}{\theta}\quad\left(\theta=\dfrac{\gamma-1}{2}\right).
                              
\end{align*}
