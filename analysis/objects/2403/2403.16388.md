(\textbf{Grothendieck fibration})
		A \emph{Grothendieck fibration} (or \emph{fibration} for short) is a functor $P:
  \relax
  \StrLen{E}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1

  \else
    \mathbf{E}
  \fi

  \relax
  \StrLen{X}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{X}
  \else
    \mathbf{X}
  \fi
$ such that any arrow $f:X \to P(D)$ in $
  \relax
  \StrLen{X}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{X}
  \else
    \mathbf{X}
  \fi
$ admits a (strong) cartesian lift:
		\begin{equation}
			                                                                                                                                                                                     
			\begin{tikzcd}[ampersand replacement=\&]
				{
  \relax
  \StrLen{E}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{E}
  \else
    \mathbf{E}
  \fi

  \relax
  \StrLen{X}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{X}
  \else
    \mathbf{X}
  \fi

(\textbf{Examples of fibrations})\label{ex:fibs}
		For a category $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$, we denote by $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
^\downarrow$ the category of morphisms in $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$ with arrows given by the commutative squares.
		\begin{enumerate}
			\item \textbf{Domain fibration}. For any category $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$, the domain projection $\mathrm{dom} := \mathrm{dom}_{
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
} : {
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
}^\downarrow \to 
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$ is a fibration. For $u:I \to J$ in $
  \relax
  \StrLen{X}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{X}
  \else
    \mathbf{X}
  \fi
$, a cartesian lift of $u$ with respect to an arrow $g : J\to Y$ is given by the square:
			                                                                                                                                                                                                             
			\[\begin{tikzcd}[sep=4ex]
			I & J \\
			X & Y
			\arrow["u\fatsemi g"', from=1-1, to=2-1]
			\arrow[Rightarrow, no head, from=2-1, to=2-2]
			\arrow["u", from=1-1, to=1-2]
			\arrow["g", from=1-2, to=2-2]
			\end{tikzcd}\]
			\item \textbf{Codomain fibration}. If $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$ has all pullbacks, then the codomain projection $\mathrm{cod} := \mathrm{cod}_{
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
} : 
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
^\downarrow \to 
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$ is a fibration, also called the \emph{fundamental fibration} of $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$. If $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$ is locally cartesian closed, the codomain fibration gives rise to a model of \emph{dependent type theory} with $\Sigma$- and $\Pi$-types~\cite{seely1984locally}. The idea is to interpret a type $X$ in context (\emph{i.e.}, a list of free variables) $I$ as a morphism $X \to I$, hence an object of the slice $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
/I$, which is the fiber of $\mathrm{cod}$ at $I :  
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$.

			Given an arrow $u : J \to I$ in $
  \relax
  \StrLen{C}[\catarglen]
  \ifnum\catarglen=1
    \mathcal{C}
  \else
    \mathbf{C}
  \fi
$ together with a map $p : X \to I$, a $\mathrm{cod}$-cartesian lift of $u$ with respect to $p$ is given by pullback:
			\[\begin{tikzcd}[sep=4ex]
